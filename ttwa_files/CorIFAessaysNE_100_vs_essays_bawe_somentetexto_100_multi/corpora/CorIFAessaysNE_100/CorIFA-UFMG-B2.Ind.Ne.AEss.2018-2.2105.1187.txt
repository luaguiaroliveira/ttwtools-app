<The Role of the Industrialization on the Economic Development>

According to a Modern Theory about economic development, Countries cannot have an economic growth beyond rates that kept their balance of payments on equilibrium, once the exports rates has a positive role on GDP (which means the inflow of capital) and imports rates has a negative role (which means the outflow of capital), it means that exports rates and imports rates must be equality. About this theory, opened by Verdoom and Kaldor, the studies of great heterodox economists have been developed, as Thirwall and Sylos Labini. The improvement of the export are good to health of economy, it is caused by the relation between improving on balance of payments and less external dependency by an advanced process of industrialization. First of all, it is important to highlight that the GDP of a Country is calculated by measure of consumer spending (C) plus investment (I) and government spending (G) as well as its net exports, which is exports minus imports (X-M). On this way, the improvement of the export means that the GDP of the Country will growth either. Another effect of this improvement on the exports would be the growing in purchasing power of the local population. On this point, Economic Development demands an advanced process of industrialization, to promote a less external dependency, as will be demonstrated. 
In Countries that have not an advanced process of industrialization and are based on primary products (like agribusiness), the local population usually spend much money importing foreign products to supply the internal absence of this kind of product, as a result, the Countries that usually make this transaction has a deficit on their balance of payments, once they must pay this transaction with foreign currency, it means to pay this transaction with the money received by exportation. In addition, export based on primary products has a negative income elasticity of demand, it means that if we observe a growing in purchasing power in Countries that imports Brazilian products, making the external population increase consumption, this consumption will not be in favor of primary products, once the preference will be for the acquisition of high-tech products, once the high-tech products has a positive income elasticity of demand, according to recently researches. It explains the reason why Developed Countries has preference to do high-tech products, once this kind of products has positive income elasticity of demand, they export more than Countries which economy are based on commodities, and they don’t need to spend much money importing foreign products to supply the internal absence of this kind of product.
Elasticity of demand shows us how much a demand grows with the increase of income. For example, a family will not increase on big proportions the amount of food that they usually spends on their daily just because the income had a big improvement, on contrary, most part of this salary improvement will be spend to buy high-tech products. That’s why it is important to a Country to develop their industrialization process, moving from primarily agricultural to one which is based on manufacturing goods.
