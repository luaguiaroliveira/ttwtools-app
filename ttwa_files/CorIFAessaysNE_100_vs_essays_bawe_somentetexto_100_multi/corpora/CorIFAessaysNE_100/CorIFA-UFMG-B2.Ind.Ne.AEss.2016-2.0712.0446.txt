<Corruption at Brazil: the brazilian criminal law system about corruption>

The phenomenon of corruption is viewd by the law at differente ways in each country. Many coutries have hard punishments for criminal conducts of corruption and, obviously, the group of conducts that can be named by “corruption act” is differente in each coutry. Those differences depend, mainly, of wich theoretical apparatus of criminal law dominates at the national criminal law system. The question that rises is how the brazilian criminal law system dela with corruption? What actions exctally are considered corruptions acts? Are there coherence between the crimes and it's punishments? This essay will analyse the brazilian criminal laws' view of corruption. After to try to answer these questions, it will finally discuss if the criminal punishment is effective.

The brazilian criminal code is from 1940. Althought, there were many changes into it over time. At result of this, the general part of the criminal shows influences by the finalist criminal theory. On the other hand, at the special part of the code, it is possible to identify concepts and notions of many different tyoes of criminal theories. That theorical mix along the criminal code engenders a lot of different notions about corrutption crimes.

An example of that is the difference between the crime of “peculato” (Article 312; CRIMINAL CODE) and the crime of “Emprego irregular de verbas ou rendas públicas” (Article 315; CRIMINAL CODE). Both the crimes are applied for public agents – people that work at the goverment, independently of the government instance: federal, state or local. The first one, “peculato” refers to an situation when the public agent appropriated by himself of government's something. The crime of thconduct of “Emprego irregular de verbas ou rendas públicas” refers to a conduct of use the public money, during the work as a public agent, to do something different that the law and the congressmen decided to that money. Obviously, if an worker of a public administration catch to him a pencil it will be less prejudice to all the society than if that public worker order do use the health fund's recourse to make constructions at his public repartition. Besides of the big different between the effects, the pusishment to the first situation is to be arrested for 2 to 12 years of arrest and to the second situation, the punishment is to be arrested fot 1 to 3 months. So, it is clear that the way of the brazilian law deal with crimes, particularly with corruptions crimes is not coherent and consistent.

According to HART (1994), the fact of a conduct be or not be criminalized by law in a society reflects the cultural values of that society. So, it's possible to conclude that are many differents cultural views of corruption and hence there are many differents ways of the criminal law systems deals with it too. As a result of that incoherence, the brazilian criminal system looks like to not be effective to punishment justly and to avoid the corruptions crimes, as defended by ZAFFARONI (2001). Thereat, Brazil was classified as one of the 69 coutries more corrupted by the world, according the Corruption Perceptions Index of Transparency International.
