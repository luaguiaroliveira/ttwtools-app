<An ethical and critical work for journalists>

In 2009, Brazilian Federal Court of Justice decided that the obligation of undergraduate course of Social Communication or Journalism and professional record to exercise that profession was unconstitutional. In 2015, the question came back to the discussion field, but first decision still applies. However, this kind of work needs more than the ability to speak or to write. In fact, words are like weapons. Journalists should undergraduate to work more critical and ethical, not only technical. The reflection provided by the degree is essential to a serious work.
When a young person starts undergraduate course of Journalism, s/he could not know that the course is more than technical information. We have to know how to write a text for radio or for television, this is a fact. Seems that is the same thing, but there are details that say if we are talking to a expecting or a spectator, for example. However, a future journalist will discuss, also, theories about Communication field, the relation between communication and popular culture and ethical implications about this work. With this background we will have a journalist that is more able to work with responsibility. We are not saying that all of the journalists with undergraduate degree on the world will be corrects, but academic reflections should help these future professionals.
Journalists will be responsible for all words, ideas or concepts that use. We should remember that stereotypes and prejudices are nourished by simple communication between two or more characters. Well, one of the subsidies for conversation are things publicized by journalism. This is one side of the ethical journalism work. We need a journalism less sensationalist and more responsible. But we need, also, readers expecting for a constructive text. Respect has to be the key to the Communication.
Another important point about critical and ethical training for this professionals concerns to the use and approach with journalistic sources. Journalists should perceive that an incorrect or malicious text will reverberate over people, families, communities, social groups or even the History. The inattention with information and with the person that says this has serious consequences. In an undergraduate course of Social Communication or Journalism, specially, we have to cultivate in the students the value of treat ethically the sources.
A journalist text is more than independent words. Write is more than put letters in a specific order. Defends that journalists need a degree is more than say that another person cannot speak. Freedom of expression and use ethic to express some facts are two different things. All of us can say our opinions to an interlocutor. However, it is supposed that a good journalist text can express something that happened with the least damage possible: to the society, to the sources or to the own journalism. Only a good and critical education will help people that can talk to the society. Have this opportunity means do a work that impact the social constructions of the world.
