<Why diversity of wisdom is so important, nowadays?>

Interdisciplinary is a link between the understandings of the disciplines at their various areas. Some author affirm that ideas which refer to interdisciplinary are ancient, Plato and Aristotle are called "interdisciplinary" due to the proximity of their ideas to sense of an unification of wisdom. Meanwhile, it appears that this sense of unity of knowledge got lost in philosophy’s book. Nowadays, in Brazil, knowledge centers doesn’t worry about diversified knowledge, and it can bring amount of professionals so attached of their own ability that they cannot even give an opinion about other knowledge field. But the advent of dynamism at the world brings a necessity to understand the complexity of society and of other cultures.
It is widely accepted that society realizes a silence and/or yelling influence in different field in our life such as appearance, behaviour and the way that we learn. The role concepts of interdisciplinary believe that we can learn in every place and in every experience, even if those are good or not. Thus, we are expression of choices that we made and the paths that we decided to follow. Therefore, as many museums and parks that we visit, books that we read and people with different reality we interact richer our baggage of knowledge will be. In other words, wisdom is not inmate at Universities surroundings, but also any place that brings new experiences.
The advent of internet has been one of developments in the history. It brings to the world new possibilities, in order to achieve these opportunities we should be prepared. The internet has helped to make the world a smaller place, the necessity to understand multi-cultural became a reality not only for businessman but also for college students. For instance, in universities are growing the demand of language classes, international relations, history and culture from other countries. Also, knowledge from different field improves mental function. In spite of individuals become very attached for their own area, develop new abilities in different fields stimulates parts of the brain that, perhaps, isn’t use on a regular basics.
On the other hand, many students don’t desire go out from their comfort zone. This is a reflex of apathy from things that are not directly on the own reality and many times, institutions don’t encourage interdisciplinary education. In spite of, many classes stay empty, some universities don’t divulge and even less stimulates ungraduated student take class in different courses. And for many subjects the obligatory schedule is very intense, so became almost impossible to handle with new knowledge. However, UFMG, for example, is trying to adjust schedule of some courses in order to transfer part of obligatory schedule to alternative subject (related or not to student’s major). For example, in pharmacy undergrad course, approximately 300 hours of obligatory schedule will be transfer to alternative subject. With this change, the university want to turn student as the major responsible to construct their own journey in order to achieve wisdom.
In conclusion, interdisciplinary suppose openness of thought, curiosity that seek beyond itself. Since antique, interdisciplinary is a concept difficult to be defined and even more to put in our ordinary life. This barrier can be constructed by educational institution or by personal behavior. Meanwhile, dynamism of the world bring a necessity to understand the complexity of society and of other cultures, in order to achieve these new opportunities we need to be the major responsible to construct our own journey to catch up wisdom.
