<The necessity of mining activity in our lives>

Most people do not know, but our modern way of life only exist because mineral resources. Unfortunately, there are several records of accidents related to mining activity around the world, some of them accompanied by loss of human lives and serious environmental, economic, or social impacts. Because of that, its not difficult to see people condemning the mineral exploration and even sustaining the end of the activity. However, those people ignore that human race has been using mineral resources since the primitive civilizations and they are still essential to produce thousands of different products in our modern society. 
About two years ago, the city of Mariana (Minas Gerais state - Brazil), was the scene of the most serious environmental disaster ever recorded in the country's history. The accident was caused by the rupture of a dam containing millions of tons of iron ore-waste material mined in the region. The spill caused 19 deaths, destroyed a small town near the mine, left thousands homeless and severely disturbed the ecosystem related to one of the main hydrographic basins on the state (Branco and Ponso 2016). More recently, two other serious mining-related accidents in Brazil had a great impact in the national media: the breach of another dam waste in Barcarena - Pará state (Barbosa 2018), and the mining pipeline rupture in Santo Antônio do Grama - Minas Gerais state (Vale 2018). 
Because of news such as these, many people think that mining activity is a bad thing and the extraction of mineral resources should end. However, the use of mineral resources by humans is a common practice since pre-history and became more intense and essential to produce a lot of products in modern societies. Our first ancestors used several types of rocks available on the ground to make fire, rustic buildings, weapons, and other artifacts. As the cultures evolved, civilizations improve the use of raw minerals like crushed stone, sand, and clays in many types of constructions, in agriculture (fertilizers), and to make a lot of industrial materials commonly used every day: ceramics, glasses, papers, plastics, inks, rubbers, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, several chemical composites, etc. In the same way, a lot of minerals and rocks extracted from Earth are the only resources for production of metals (iron, copper, gold, silver, aluminum, tin, lead, chromium, manganese, nickel, zinc, cobalt, lithium, titanium, niobium, etc.), which are used to produce thousands of products, especially those from electronic, automotive, metallurgical, and steel industries. Without them there would not exist computers, mobile phones, electrical wires, internet, cars, cement, or concrete, and many others essential materials that are essential in our lives.
In that case, instead of condemning the mining activity because the bad problems that occasionally occur, what we need is acquire a culture of sustainable development in mining activity and charge the government and companies for better inspections and more rigid laws that would prevent any kind of accidents or other problems related to mining.
