<Learning a new language outside the books>

It is well known the benefits of learning a new language, however, many people do not even try and, sometimes, it is because of the many books they think they will have to read, or the many “boring” exercises they will have to do in order to learn it. Well, things do not need to be that way exactly, of course, learn about the grammar is always good and as important as the vocabulary, but, for the beginning, learning a new language can be fun, and sometimes, you can do it without even realize. Based on a lot of research and a personal experience (since I have never taken an English class besides high school), I will talk a little about the idea of “build a new vocabulary on a new language” outside the books. 
Music. Music is something that most of the people have accesses to and the benefits of it in a process of learning are beyond pleasure alone. Research points out that music has the capacity of reach the body directly on the cells and organs and indirectly acting on the emotions, with have a huge influence in learning. Through music you practice your listening and understanding, once you have different speeds and timings, you also practice your pronunciation, because when you listen to something repeated times your brain is going to “save” that and, unconsciously, you are learning to speak something new. For the same reason comes the vocabulary, in addition to the new worlds you are going to learn by music, you can also learn some “slang”, with is definitely new, once you difficultly is going to find it in some books, but you definitely is going to listen to people saying it. Besides all that the memorization gets a lot easier with music, because, again, everything happens unconsciously. The influential power that music has is enormous and can be extremely positive in your learning. 
Other than music, you can also learn a lot of a new language with movies and Tv shows. With a simple action as watching those with subtitles instead of dubbed you can improve your learning a lot more. In movies and series you can find a lot of new expressions, you can train your ability of understanding a native talking in the language you are studying, you will learn a little about a new culture, you can “travel without travelling”. As all the things in this essay, the pleasure with which you learn has an immense influence in your learning since your brain can work better when you are learning with something you really like. And, like that, you can learn without knowing. 
In the title you see “outside the books”, but I cannot let this pass without talking a little about the books. But, it is not the books you are thinking, it is not grammar books. The benefits of, at least, trying to read a book you really like, and you have already read it in your native language, in a new language are enormous as well. You will open your mind for something more challenging and, with this your brain is going to “save” the things better. Reading something you like in another language is really going to help, not just your reading skills, but your ability in understanding something wrote in another idiom, is going to show you how the same thing can be wrote in different ways and improve a lot your vocabulary, not to mention how fun it can be and how many “new worlds” you can find. 
In conclusion, I could talk a lot more about the good things those methods can bring to us, but, for now, you could see how beneficial and “simple” can be to start learning a new language. With a little willpower and some small and pleasurable actions you will start learning without even notice, and that can lead you more easily to a lot more of good things. It can be very fun learning a new language and it not need to be sitting on a chair and going through all the difficult books.
