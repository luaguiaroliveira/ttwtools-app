<TOPIC: The Importance of Environmental Preservation in Brazil for its Future>

Human being is completely reliant on of the usage of water, in Brazil is not different, but they are surely dependent on their water because they use it for two main things. First, is for the trivial things in human lives like drinking, washing, taking shower, etc., and second for generate their electric energy. More than 80% of the electricity generation is based in hydroelectric, so if Brazil have a problem with the rainfalls or with the ground waters, it will be a disaster. Unfortunately, Brazilians do not care about their water, this source were always plenty for them. Since 2014, they have problems with their water reservoir because rains less than they imagine, so the government started doing some programs to reduce the consumption and maintain water for population.

To not be as dependently as Brazilians is, there are some ideas for them starting to think and make a change the way of using their water. For maintenance of their water fountains they could start doing a controlling and monitoring system of their principals environmental areas (those who had a directly impact on their sources), promote reinstatement in affected areas and always keep looking throw new technologies for conservation and preservation of, unless, these principal regions.

Like said before, other huge problem caused by water’s deficient is the generation of energy, almost all electric energy, in Brazil, is generated by hydroelectric, therefor using water. For improve this situation, the government could encourage people and investors (using tax incentive or giving other types of benefits) to build and invest in renewable energy sources like solar or wind energy. Brazil have a vast potential to use solar and wind power and, these kinds of generation are considered clean (do not emit carbon gas to atmosphere) and are less harmful than the usual forms.

Finally, other object important for Brazil invest is in education! Everything might be change if they start to change the culture of a population, if Brazilians start to understand how important water is and start to see the relation with water and their habits they will change their conscious and begin to take care of nature and treat it with respect. This changing maybe will take a long time, but it is essential for a healthy living and keep the planet in harmony, we can initiate it with kids, in school, and then in two or three generations we would see results.

Knowing the importance of have a good relation between human being and nature it is time to change and start to care with our planet, not just Brazilians but all world. It is essential to change our current view that is focus on profits, even if they are gain from exploration and degradation of nature, without thinking in the future consequences of these actions. Nature is bigger than we are, so we need to start a new vision for living, thinking in future and in maintenance of our species.
