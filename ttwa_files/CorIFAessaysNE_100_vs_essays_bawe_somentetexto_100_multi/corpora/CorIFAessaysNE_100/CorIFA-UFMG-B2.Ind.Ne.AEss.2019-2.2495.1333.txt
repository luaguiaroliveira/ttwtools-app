Title: Is necessary a specific arts for specific audience?>

I have been heard a lot of artist talking about how youth are far away from contemporary dance and because of that the youth does not usually go to theater. We can blame the education or put the reason of this problem in our public policies, because they do not incentive arts. But I think that one problem is the language of arts.  We should have specific arts for youth in dance field and it is important discovered and create strategies to approach and engage youth audience with contemporary dance. But, how we could do this? Which do factors make young people choose watch a dance? There is no recipe, but it is important to notes that each notion of youth reflects a notion of arts and a way of create and produce arts. So we need to be clear about our concepts. It is considered three important concepts in this situation: youth, art and creation.
First, it is necessary to be clear about what we think it is youth. If we understand youth as people in growing who needs a strong guiding because the adults know what it is better for them, probably we will create arts in one direction. It is possible we will create arts for them. In another way, if we consider that youth has their own wisdom and they know about themselves more than the adults, i.e., no one is more expert on their own lives than themselves. In this case, it will be valuable knows what they think about arts and culture. And maybe we will create arts with them. With this point of view, we could provide artists and youth opportunities and capacities to make decisions about their participation in the formation of their culture and arts, recognizing their perspective and encouraging them to create in their own reality.
Second, it is necessary to know what we think about arts. If we believe that arts is some preview defines and the good arts are the classical arts, probably we will create dance based in the principal of this kind of arts, that it is, most of the time, the European Arts. And once more time, we probably will intent to give the youth what it are good arts without asked them. For other side, if we perceive arts as a construction where arts are not an unchangeable definition unchangeable, probably we will have critical thought about arts. Arts can be used as a tool to establish hierarchies of expression that allow that one dominated other. If we do not hear the youth we are contributing to legitimize some kinds of arts and use this as a way of control the others. Listening them, we will look for ways for be together and recognize diversity. No universalism of arts but diversity. In this way, the artists are interested to understand what it is arts for youth and how invent arts with them.
Finally, it is necessary to reflect about the process of creation. In one side, we can thing that creation and presentation is an object that the artist presents for the audience. In another way, we have the creation and presentation like a relationship between artists and youth. In this point, the relation it is crucial and the artists will be more close to audience. Each thought it will be determine the process and define the way of you rearrange the show and the experience of audience. 
In conclusion, how exactly to do this is complex because this is not a calculate process. But it is crucial to think that our notions will determine our creation and if the youth will connect or not with arts and how they were be in touch. My research is based on the idea that youth it is a social condition, and there are lots of ways to experience that time of life. In this way, I claim that youth know better about theirs situation them adult so it is essential to learn hearing them about what they expect of arts. So, hearing what they want and what they understand about arts, i.e., including them in the process of creation, we will discover elements to create a dance for, with and of youth. In this way, we would recognize that the arts are as relationship and approaching and this does not mean to imitate or pretend to be youth like them, but to be sensible about their condition and wake up for this diversity. Probably, we will more close to discover which factors make youth choose dance and to proposing actions, projects and arts more coherent with their times of life. Possible in a part of our path we will connect and be together with them as an artist.
