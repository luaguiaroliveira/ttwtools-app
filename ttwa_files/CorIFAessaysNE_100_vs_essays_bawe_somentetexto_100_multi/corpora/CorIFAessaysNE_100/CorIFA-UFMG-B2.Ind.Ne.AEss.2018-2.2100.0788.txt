<Biomedical Engineering in people's life>

Biomedical engineering, also known as bioengineering, is a combination of the design and problem solving techniques of engineering applied on the biological and medical sciences. These both areas, together, focus on the advances that improve human health and health care at all levels. It is responsible for the development and creation of prostheses, medical instruments, diagnostic equipment and the study about the living organisms from the engineering point of view. The oldest known occurrence is from a mummy in Thebes. The body had an instance of prosthesis in the form of a wooden toe that had been attached to the foot using string. Since then, bioengineering has developed a great deal and keep growing every day. Therefore, considering its importance, it is necessary to invest and improve the techniques already existing.
It is generally believed that different areas may work together to provide a wide range of knowledge. Thus, by the combination of medicine and engineering it is possible to improve life of the people. It is crucial for the biomedical engineers confront, understand and solve highly complex problems in biomedicine, bringing together the tools available in the fields of experimental science, life science and engineering in all their facts. This science is responsible for the creation of artificial organs, automated patient monitoring, blood chemistry sensors, advanced therapeutic and surgical devices, biomechanics for injury and wound healing, among many others. Biomedical engineers are present in research centers, in companies, in hospitals and universities. Besides that, Bioengineering is now one of the fastest growing fields.
Meanwhile, some obstacles hinder bioengineering of increase equally around the world. A new product takes time and needs investments to be created. Electrocardiography, an important vital signal recorded and monitored in most clinical routines, took many scientists experimenting over the years, from the famous electricity experiment on legs of a frog in 1786, until the construction of the first commercialized device in the 20th century. Unfortunately, investments for the search and development of a product do not occur all the time. Some companies, then, prefer to import machines already ready than develop a national one. However, biomedical engineering is a new area, and it is promising for the next years. In 2012 there were about 19,400 biomedical engineers employed in the US, and the field was predicted to grow by 27% from 2012 to 2022. In the future, it will be necessary people specialized in this rising area.
Finally, it is possible to conclude that investments in biomedical engineering is important for the evolution. The society is made up of people, the ones who maintain the status quo of the environment. However, these people need to be cared too. Around the world, new technologies are being created all the time. Indeed, the expectation of life also has been increasing over the years. Overall, considering the development of technology, it is crucial to make products to help people improve their health, comfort, and happiness.
