<Title: Active Learning>

Active learning is a way of teaching based on tasks accomplishment. The teacher gives some tasks to the students, related to the subject, and they have to accomplish these tasks by doing research and/or asking for tips from the teacher. The grade is based, mainly, on the accomplishment of the tasks, however, the teacher can apply some written tests too or another kinds of evaluation. Given that, active learning is a teaching method that should be best the the traditional methods, in engineering courses, if the classes is well managed by the teacher.

A good engineer should be capable of find an elegant and efficient solution to a given problem. Every problem in many professional areas, including engineering, is different, and should be dealt with in a specific way, requiring the professional to adapt his/her knowledge, do some research and find the best solution. The active learning method teach the students to seek solution to different problems by themselves, with the supervision of the teacher, and, this way, prepares the student better for the labor market.

An interesting thing in the active learning method is that each student can achieve his/her own solution for the problem. These different solutions can all receive the same grade since the task proposed is accomplish. In the traditional teaching method, usually, just one answer is correct for the problems proposed in the written tests and many times, the students just memorialize the answer (and forget a couple of days after the test) instead of learn the subject.

Subjects based on active learning can have lectures and written tests as well. All depends on the subject content and the evaluation methods that the teacher wants to adopt. In this way, active learning can be a complementary method to help the students to understand a given subject matter by applying that knowledge to solve a problem, and, in addition to that, helps them to learn how to find solutions by using the several sources of knowledge available nowadays.

It can be argued that isn’t possible to apply active learning in basic subjects where the students are yet learning the fundamentals to understand problems and advanced subjects in engineering as well; and this is true but not for all the basic subjects. For example, some physics theories and laws like the Einstein’s General Relativity Theory or the Gravity Law, are theoretical issues that can be tested but they’re just a part of the basic knowledge necessary to understand a more complex problem subject and it’s solution. On the other hand, a basic subject in engineering like Calculus can be the only knowledge necessary to solve many practical problems, so this is an example of a basic subject where active learning can be applied.

Active learning is a very good method for teaching several subjects in engineering and explores the knowledge of the students and their capacity to find solutions for problems, helping them to secure the knowledge acquired and acquire new ones in a better way than when they just have theoretical classes and written tests. With some adaptation, can be possible to apply active learning in another knowledge fields besides engineering, changing this old teaching method that is so rooted in the colleges and universities around the world.
