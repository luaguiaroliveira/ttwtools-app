<Computer and video games are harmful to children?>
In modern society, the way children interact with computers and video games is important. One reason is that with the increasing popularization and advancement of technology, these medias are widely present in children's life. The influence of these electronics is considerate by many people as harmful, considering the time children’s plays, the unconscious influence and others ways to have fun. However, it is necessary to consider their benefits and the context in which they are used, as well as the need to supervise these medias in order to ensure the best use of them. In this essay, I will argue that, because there are clear advantages for children in having access to these technologies, computer and video games can not be generally considered harmful to them – it only needs some precautions in use.
First, the way children have fun today is different from years ago. Computer and video games has taken the place of the “street games”, as collective games - and this can not be considered a bad change, but a consequence of the development of technology, urban structure and culture. More than that, these medias entertain children in the safety of home, exposing them less to risks. With a globalized world, it is no longer possible to eliminate the technology from children’s life.
Another reason is that, with the proper supervision, no technology is harmful to kids. Some people think that computer and games takes much time of children, influence behavior unconsciously and are a bad way of fun because its individuality. But, the point in this issue is that, if you select what type of games and time that children play, this is not a option. For example, one can control the time that children play, the type of games and let them play only with supervision. Furthermore, should be talked openly with them about that. With an open relationship with children, the responsible will become a behavior influence and not the video or computer - and the children will still have time to study and do other social things. So, there are many ways to prevent and eliminate misuse of technology – they are not harmful by their own.
Finally, technology is likely to bring more benefits than anything else. Most of responsible do not have the time to play with your children everyday– like many people nowadays. So, the solution is not to struggle with technology. Technology appears in nowadays context as a ally for parents in fun and study. The fact is that computers and video games strongly develop creativity and cognitive thinking, as well as can be used for interactive learning, even for a new language. In this way, we should see technology as an opportunity to develop children's skills.
In conclusion, like anything in the world, computer and video games can be used in the wrong way, but it will only be harmful to children if some points were neglected. Its a wrong definition to consider them harmful by their own. With the right orientation and in a supervised context, computer, video games and other types of technology are an opportunity to develop children's skills and can only bring benefits.
