The important of Quality manager in Aerospace Industry

The industry already has gone through many phases. In the early twentieth century, the companies around the world were worried about production, so, as more outcome the company could produce, more power they would have. In the middle of the twentieth century, the companies changed their mentality, in that as more money the company had, more powerful they were. Today, the quality of the income governs the world’s markets, and the quality manager position is very required the aerospace industry, especially.
Nowadays, there are many companies that work relates to aerospace. Among them, there are companies that work with maintenance of airplane or parts, aircraft assembly, research and development of aerospace technology and companies that work with design and manufacture of airplane’s parts. Therefore, the quality manager has importants roles in aerospace company around the world, specially in companies that work with design and manufacture of aerospace parts.
In these companies, there are many types of process which the product need to pass to reach the phase of delivery with desire quality required by client. The first step assure that the parts can be manufactured by the company or need some design modification to better adapt the type of process that the company have. After that, there is the process of manufacturing of the parts, which can be laminating in composite material, that precedes the finishing phase, where the parts goes through a final verification, to know if it is compliance with the initial requirement presented by client. In the case of non-compliance, the process have to be repeated.
The quality manager is in all this steps, since the contract phase until the process to delivery of the product to client. During the contract phase, the quality manager need to verify if the company is able to make the product that the customer require. After that, he need to guarantee that during all step of manufacturing, the company is complying with the customer requirement. This verification is possible though monitoring all the processes, finding and correcting failures. At the end, before delivery the product, the manager needs analyze the degree of compliance of the parts, through different types of inspection.
For that, the quality manager has important roles in industry. It is through of the manager that the company can know if the process is in accordance with the company’s policies, if the process is spending more money than predicted, if it had losses of raw material or even parts, product. Along with all these knowledge relates to manufacturing process, the manager can standardize processes, which can reduce the cost of manufacturing and allows assure quality of the product. 
Summarizing, the companies needs to have quality manager to get better competitiveness in today’s globalized market, especially in an aerospace area. The important to have quality manager in the company is standardize process, assure compliance of the product with customer requirements, decrease of production cost.
