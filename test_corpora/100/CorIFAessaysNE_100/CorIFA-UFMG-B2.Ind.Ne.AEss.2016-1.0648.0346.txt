<Privacy on social network systems who is the responsible>


Every now and them we are looking our timeline on social network system. And only Facebook has data from about 3 billion of users around the world. But frequently there are some information that you do not want to share with everyone, and sometimes it brings some embarrassment or even real problems. So who is responsible to the privacy of the data on the social media and the security of these contents, should this is on the users hand or the companies have to make actions to protect the users?

Nowadays everyone has at least one account in some social network. Mainly the youths, share their daily activities, favorite place and events they are attending. Although it is a commonly activity, they need to take care who they want to see their posts and who has permission for it. All the social network systems have to have tools that allows their users to control their profiles security. And the users should be aware who they are communicating. Even though mostly of the users do not know how the social networks systems really works. Some of them are sharing all their information with the entire world and simply do not know this.

It is known that social network companies concentrate a huge amount of people information in their systems. A great part of that information are sensible in terms of privacy and security. Moreover some of that information, the users do not want to share with someone. But the information is there and the companies are the directly responsible for any leak of that information. To prevent this it need to have some measures to prevent and to block any undesirable access to that information. And alert their users how to prevent this leak of information is a good way to avoid some future problems.

Be connected and post what you are feeling or doing in online social networks are an integrated routine in our lives. The advances in the technologies allow, improve and make it more and more natural for us in the daily routines. However, neither the users have concerns in what they are sharing nor the companies are controlling or have tools to prevent that. With the increasing in the quantity of information shared and the growing of the systems, both the sides have to be aware of the contents and the behaviors are present on the social environment.

Finally, all the improvements in the technologies are change the human life in a fast way. From the day to night we can do thing we never have imagined. And it do not seems to be stopping, it is quite the opposite actually, almost every month we have some new technology at hand. Despite all the companies have a minimum concern in how the information are stored and shared, everyone have to be alert and be responsible for what they are posting and doing on public environments.
