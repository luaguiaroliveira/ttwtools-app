<Evolution of the pharmaceutical care>

In the beginning of 20th century, the medicines were handcrafted by the pharmacist in the apothecary. But with the large scale of drug production by the pharmaceutical industries, the pharmacist need to discovered news ways to work and develop others skills. The pharmaceutical professional has changed over the time. In the United States, the crisis in the profession and the loss of role in the health care were solved in the hospital setting. Because that was emerged the clinical pharmacy as a solution of this problem. From this, the clinical practice of the pharmacist has been developed and enhanced to guarantee the effectiveness and safe the medication use (ANGONESI; SEVALHO, 2010).  In Brazil, the Federal Council of Pharmacy adopted the concept of Pharmaceutical Care as a patient-centered practice (CFF, 2013). Significant changes in demographic and morbidity and mortality profiles have happened during the last century. Fertility and mortality from infectious diseases reduced and life expectancy and deaths from chronic diseases increased. In addition, aging is a phenomenon that happens in Brazil and worldwide. It is a high prevalence of multiple and noncommunicable chronic diseases, causing to increase polypharmacy and complex therapies and, consequently raising health spending (AIOLFI et al., 2015; MARIN et al., 2016; MELCHIORS et al., 2007; MODIG, HOLMDAHL, BONDESSON, 2016; PEREIRA et al., 2017). The high number of drugs prescribed and the presence of multiple morbidities lead to unnecessary drug use. Pharmacological combinations represent potential risks of adverse reactions and drug interactions, contraindication and may increase the risk of iatrogenics, hospitalizations and death (PEREIRA et al., 2017). More than 700.000 individuals are admitted to United States emergency unit as a consequence of adverse drug events (ADE) each year. People 65 years or older have seven times risk to be hospitalized than younger people. While in Australia, approximately 230,000 hospitalizations are medication-related per year. Up to 75 of these hospitalizations are considered potentially preventable (JOKANOVIC, et al., 2017).
Various actions have been oriented to insertion of the pharmacist in patient health care. In Brazil, the insertion of the pharmacist in health care happened with the creation of Extended Family Health Team (EFHT). This is compound by different professionals, as pharmacist, physical therapist, psychologist, nutritionist, others. EFHT is a support team to the health team in primary health care. In 2013, the Federal Council of Pharmacy elaborated the Resolution n585/2013, which established about the need of humanized relationship between pharmacist and patients. This consist respect, expectations and attitudes focused on patients health conditions and medication use (CFF, 2013). Therefore, an integrated action by the pharmacist with the centered health team for health promotion, protection and recovery and disease prevention (BRASIL, 2014). Moreover, pharmacists were in unique position to provide clinical services, improve outcomes and the quality of the drug use in throughout the care process (MARR et al., 2018).
Changes have occurred on pharmaceutical market in last years, because that the pharmacist needed to transform as professional. These modifications have many impacts in the way that the pharmacist sees himself as a health provider. Before the focus were on the drugs and now he need to developed news skills to take care of people who use medicines. Communication, empathy, knowledge of pharmacology, pharmacotherapy, physiology and others are skills that the pharmacists have the necessity to build on and improve their practice. It is known that the pharmacotherapy follow-up by this professional enhance the quality and effectiveness of medication use and minimize adverse drug reactions, medication interactions, unnecessary hospitalization. However, numerous aspects in pharmacy courses demand serious changes in the curriculum to prepare more qualified pharmacist for care.
