<Why not reduce criminal majority>

The debate around the controversial issue of reducing criminal majority is not new for  Brazilian people. Furthermore, it divides population's opinion between the ones that defend the diminishing of criminal majority and the ones against that. In view of this polarization, it is necessary to think about some deeply questions involving this topic. First of all, holding people is really a solution for security problems and violence in Brazil? Significantly when the posible prisioners are adolescents and children? Secondly,  it is substantial that Brazil's prison population already is the third biggest in the world but nonetheless the violence statistics are still high. And thirdly, there are big private companies around the globe profiting a lot with mass incarceration, including in Brazil. Above all, in this essay will be discussed why criminal majority should not be reduced in Brazil.
Supporters of the criminal majority reduce in Brazil foster that this would shorten the high indexes of criminality and violence across the country, despite this there are  reasons for not believing that. It is true that nowadays the organized crime coopts children and adolescents under 18 years old but this does not happen because they are not punished as defenders of reducing criminal majority up hold. Significantly, law transgressions are social problems that can be caused by several specific cirscumstances of subject's life. Provided that, the efforts to solve criminality problems have to be related with giving solutions for this social problems, instead of just punishing people.
Moreover, Brazil has now the third biggest prison population in the world, as recent data collected by Justice ministry shows. This fact proves that imprisoning persons does not resolve the high indexes of violence and criminality or Brazil would already be a peacefull country. In addition to that, most of  inmates in brazilian's prisons are provisional prisioners yet, in light of this information it is posible to infer that reducing criminal majority will significantly worsen this situation, holding even more citizens, in this case adolescents and children, without the right of judgment ensured.
With this in mind, it is important to see how lucrative is for private organizations building and administrating prisons. This companies develop partnerships with government and receive huge amounts of public money to maintain prisons, the number of its public concessions has increased specially in the last few years in Brazil . The amount received per prisioner should be used to provide decent living conditions inside the prisons as well as education and working programmes in order to guarantee satisfactory indexes of reintegration into society. But instead of it, the reality of brazilian's prisons is overcrowding and low indicators of reintegration. Above all, it is evident why some sectors of society are so interested in the opportunity of putting even more younger people in jail: because it is lucrative to them.
All things considered, it seems reasonable to assume that reducing criminal majority will only worsen social problems in Brazil, create new ones and, beyond that, will not resolve the problems concerned with public security. In addition to that, this change would be profit to a small group of persons in Brazil, what is the real interest of the diminishing majority. While few tatics are being developed to really resolve or avoid the situation of adolescents and children that are co-opted by organized crime and other forms of criminality.
