<The importance of the talent and hark work for success>

Nowadays the main goal of the people is to succeed in life to satisfy their needs. To get this success there is a question of which one is more important: talent or hard work, and that is a subject very debated, each one has their opinion, showing their argument and point of view. So to try to get at a conclusion, it will be shown both sides in a way that the combination of them reaches the goal.
	People tend to believe that talent is more important because talent is a gift, a natural and an inborn thing, something that cannot be chosen, and provides a facility to learn, understand, and be able to do anything. Talent is believed to be an innate ability that is a potential to succeed. For example, a piano player who never did music lessons but can sit in front a piano and play naturally, like he knows how to do it since he was born, just because he has talent, he has a facility to play. With talent a person can get anything that she wants which in accordance with her expertise. And the key to that is the word “want”, she might have that desire to get or else she can fail just like if she has no talent.
While hard work is the effort, the persistence and the desire to succeed that motivates a person to go on, independent if there is a talent facilitating, or not, she just keep going until she get there. Someone who works hard will get experience and skills because of the practice, and practice makes person perfect, and perfection is a form of success. So, for example, the same guy above, the piano player, what if he tried to play soccer? Would he have the same natural ability as he has playing piano? Probably not, but if he practice a few months, dedicate time to improve, he might be successful at this too, despite not having a talent.
And now looking for talent and hard work together, the facility allied with the effort, this already suggest something successful. Exemplifying, a child who is very intelligent, can learn just hearing the professor, does not need to study at home to have regular grades, but if she does study at home, do exercises and prepare better, she can get better grades than she was having. So she uses her talent and improves it with hard work and that causes an improvement in her grades, and so on she will have success.
In conclusion, the success can be achieved with talent and hard work separately, but if combined, because both of them are important to reach the goal, is almost sure that it is going to be faster and beyond average. The combination rises the odds and decreases the time until the achievement.
