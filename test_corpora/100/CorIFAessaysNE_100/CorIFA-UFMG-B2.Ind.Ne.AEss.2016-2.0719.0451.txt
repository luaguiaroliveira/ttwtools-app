<Which is more important: talent or hard work?>

Which is more important: talent or hard work? This is an issue that do not have consensus among specialists from several knowledge fields – arts, music, sports, education etc. The talent is considered an innate ability – like a virtue – that turn us able to do somethings with a superior quality, but without great effort. On the other hand, the hard work may be seen as something required only for the people that would be unprovided of talent. Because of this, the talent people are regarded better than the people that need to do hard work to achieve the excellence degree. In this essay I will argue that, oftentimes, the talent will be not enough to achieve the excellence, which is enable fundamentally through the hard work and, therefore, everyone is habile to do anything on the better way.
It is unquestionable that talent is very important to give quality in our dids. In the Arts there are several examples about artists very talented – Caravaggio, Rembrandt, Vincent van Gogh etc. Pieces as “The Conversion of Saint Paul”, “The Night Watch”, and “Bedroom in Arles” show their talent. “The Brandenburg Concerts”, the fugues, “The Well-Tempered Clavier” are Bach’s masterpieces, which would not compose if he was unprovided of talent. Generally, the talent is perceptible, especially in works considered emblematic. Examples of this are the Architectonic Complex of Pampulha – designed by Oscar Niemeyer when he was a young architect –, “Monalisa” of Leonardo da Vinci, and Auguste Rodin with him “The Thinker”. If these artists and musicians were untalented, may be we could not joy these prodigious pieces.
On the other side, in common sense, the hard work is seen as a thing needful by talentless people. Because of this, the who needs work hardly may have difficult to have them works recognized. In sports, the athletes called “workers” are, commonly, considered underdogs in sports betting. In school, some students need to make effort and study more than others, to overcome their difficult in the various subjects. However, with hard work is possible to achieve goals, like the talented people. In Tennis, for example, there are players with less talent – like David Ferrer – that obtain good results, winning tournaments and great prizes. In the same way, student may acquire knowledge to get good grades.
Finally, the talent and the hard work are not opposite: the first may be complemented by the last. Even they is talented, probably the artists and musicians mentioned above did not their masterpieces if they do not work hardly. Even if the talent is a innate virtue, it need to be develop to improve the quality. Roger Federer – considered the best tennis player of all time – have trainings daily to keep him good physical and technique fit. Otherwise, he will not stay in the top ten of the world ranking.
In conclusion, the talent is very important; but with no hard work, the talent will do not develop and will turn stagnant. Therefore, the talent needs the hard work to emerge and qualify the human dids.
