 The modern society, most notoriously after World War II, has shown an excessively consumption behavior. This consumptive pattern was extremely important for the economies recover after that, mainly on those from capitalist block of countries. However, for each behavioral movement there is a consequent result. The consumption leads to disposal and its consequential garbage production increase, with a noticeable effect near urban population concentration. The international community already perceive that this consume model has shown unsustainable. Countries around the world are adopting recycling programs and legislations as way to minimize the effects of wasted materials. In Brazil the most significant results come from associations of recyclable materials collectors (i.e. ASMARE - Associação dos catadores de material reciclável). Perhaps there is an enormous field for increasing of consciousness and education environment policies that can mitigate more efficiently the problem.
	The consciousness level of Brazilians population it is bellow from what is necessary to tackle a correct disposal problem. Quick television news watching on rainy periods it is enough to observe the amount of floating garbage carried by the rain, what is a strong evidence of opportunities to government improvements on environmental education policies. Research and articles about environment education [1] started at early 90, although in small number and publishing.
	By another hand, the catchers of recyclable materials have contributed for avoiding an even worst country scene. With an undeniable financial biased actuation, they prioritized materials such as aluminum and PET (polyethylene terephthalate) and some papers. This procedure circumscribes the recycling process, wasting good opportunities for a wide range of materials. In spite of its importance for social income distribution, their overall performance could be improved by achieving more scholarship. An example it is the concept of PET just associated with bottles of refrigerants, ignoring many others kind of applications for this material. Almost all kind of metals are subject to a recycling process, but the case of aluminum recycling poses Brazil in a good position on the rank of countries that recycle it.
	Although its long list of programs and actions [2, 3] on the field of selective collection and recycling process, the results are shy for a country like Brazil, most of it due to slowly government actions. The reversal logistic, in which the producer of some kind of good is responsible to enhance the final level of waste destination, is still incipient. The most known program just applies to batteries but could also be to tires, cars, electronics etc.. This kind of program is spoiled by political and corporative interest, reducing even more the process of recycling.
	The problem of dealing with wasted materials it is complex, enrolling different groups of interests and economic issues. A continuous increment of government policies for environment education, specially applied to children, may absolutely produce a better scenario in a long run. In a short time the reversal logistic seems to be the most effective solution, despite of economic implications and conflict of interests.
