<ANN as the best methodology to calculate Forest Fire Risk>

Forest Fires became a great concern in terms of environmental problems, as it affects the vegetation, the air quality and the soil. The fire risk assessment is important to establish the priority risk zones to have a better planning of prevention and firefighting activities. Although, to determine this fire risk there is a lot of approaches, as statistic methods, such as logistic regression, qualitative and quantitative methods such as multi criteria analysis, and artificial intelligence methods such as artificial neural networks (ANN) and fuzzy logic. The one that is not commonly used, but has showed effectiveness results is the ANN, based on human nervous system, comprehends a network of neurons which restore a knowledge learn in a training process. So, this essay will compare a lot of methods and show that ANN is the best one to used for calculating the forest fires risk.
The multi criteria analysis is a robust method that depends a lot on the preview knowledge of specialized people. Since its structures as a hierarchy decision, where is decided that elements which affects more the fires received higher weights, and those which affects less received lower weights, and this weighting is given by the decision makers based on their knowledge on the study area. Chuvieco et al (1989) used this method in his research and your results were not satisfactory, in a comparison with real fires spots and your map for risk areas, only 22,72% of the spots were classified as high risk. Above all, this method does not look very effective, but can help to have an idea of the areas with risk of fire.
In the other side, the Fuzzy Logic, instead of the multi criteria analysis that have solid limits between the classes of the elements, doesn’t have a defined transition. So in the other method, for example, the weight for precipitation until 100 mm were classified as high risk and the precipitation from 100 to 150 mm were classified as moderated risk, in this approach the classes are defined as a function comprising a fuzzy set. The continuous classes of the fuzzy sets are constructed from concepts defined a priori using the experience, science or heuristic knowledge. So this method is already not so suitable, besides have better performance.
The logistic regression it is independent from the knowledge of the researcher, it only needs a set of data of fire and no fire places, that is the dependent variable, and the elements that affects the fire, that are the independent variables. It is a statistical method, where the probability of a fire occur is given by a function were each variable is associated with a coefficient, so the fire data is used to calculated this coefficient. The ANN, similarly, also need this data to work, but the variables became the input layer of the network, that are linked in the neurons, where in each neuron, the entries are multiplied by a weighted given in the connections and the data is summed up and applied in the best transfer function. Being the difficulty of this process the calculation of this weighted, that are given from a training, where is used the fire data. In an experiment of Goldarag (2006) is compared these two methods and he found a accuracy of 50.84% for the logistic regression and 91% for the ANN. So the ANN has better performance.
Despite the complexity of the Artificial Neural Networks it is the method that have the better performance among other and the more confident, as it uses techniques of artificial intelligence and converts the knowledge given to it in results.
