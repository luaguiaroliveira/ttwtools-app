<The Literacy and Composition Practices of Portuguese‐Language Learners in an Online Fanfiction Community>

In order to understand  this essay, we need first to visualize  that level of Portuguese literacy  is lower comparing other level of proficiency from in developing countries. This essay was built based in checking the levels of reading proficiency of students enrolled in third grade of High School. For supporting the this thesis, two mains education assessments made by Federal Government was adopted as a source of investigation. The analyses of them show that the level of literacy of students from public is lower. According to this context, this essay intends to discuss how a pedagogic strategies can aid these students to acquire better grades. This essay  focus in practicing very knowing among adolescents called the fan  fiction  writing or fanfiction. My thesis is through the fanfictions,  young students from public schools can acquire better literacy while reading and writing stories of idols. The participants were Portuguese Language teachers and students enrolled in third grade at a Public High School. This essay will explain the methods opted in order to carry out this study: bibliographic study, as well as desk and field researches.
Theoretical foundations came from the contribution of several authors who discuss about language, as well as new methods of teaching with the new technologies of information and communication, such as: Bakhtin, (1953/2011), Rojo (2004; 2009; 2012; 2013), Gomes (2010; 2012; 2014; 2015), Coscarelli (2011; 2013),  Coscarelli and Cafiero (2013), among others. The bibliographic study shows how relevant is this issue mainly because many schools do not incorporate the technology in their pedagogic routine currently. The importance of this point must be overlooked: while devices are remodeled through the time, education field has the same structure of  last century. New practices need to be adopted in education field  and them must make sense with our lives in community. Chandler-Olcott and Mahar (2003) drew on the work of several New Literacy Studies  researchers to examine the fanfiction practicing as a valid literacy practice. This essay seeks to extend their work into the virtual realm by  a theoretical lens to explore the ways the digital medium not only scaffolds but also is an integral part of the literacy and meaning-making practices (e.g., composing and interacting in multiple genres and social registers and offering sophisticated reviews and meta-analyses of fictions) that adolescent student of Portuguese language use as they post fanfictions. To that end, some literacy researchers claimed to look in earnest at the Internet and the opportunities it provides for authentic linguistic and social interactions within contexts that are meaningful for participants.
Furthermore, the desk research reveals there is a public policy for adoption of digital technologies in class. Although, the illiteracy it is not solved only supply schools  with digital gadgets. Thus, analyses focus on the various ways in which interplay enables adolescent students to forge connections with other fans and to establish a social base within this discourse community. This, in turn, provides them with 24-hour access to  your community and facilitates authentic, meaningful use of language and literacy (Warschauer, 2000). It reveals that digital inclusion precedes  behavior involvement allied on social interests. 
Moreover Freeman and Freeman (1994) discussed the interplay of a range of in- and out-of-school factors that contribute to a language learner’s access to Second Learning Acquistion, such as level of acceptance by the community, background knowledge and experiences, and interactions with peers and teachers, as well as teaching and learning approaches within the community. This is particularly relevant for literacy  researchers and noteworthy for educators is the kinds of writing going on, the metatalk about writing, the meaning-making and identity work related to writing, and the sort of people who are doing the writing. This statement was proofed on data collection which was held through interviews, questionnaires and participant observation. Many participants reported the range of their writings and acceptance of their works among peers from other communities.
In conclusion, the  thesis points the adoption of simple pedagogic strategies can produce efficient works in the field of language, as well as on Portuguese literacy, considering the use Technology as an important tool for improving the digital literacy. The use of fanfiction genre as a good example how we can use simple and modern technique to stimulate for producing good writings. However, the survey field revealed  the necessity of investments in teacher training, projects, actions and policies in reading teaching, in order to improve the level of  high  school education. These results showed how relevant is provide  a better development of all reading skills in students, which is essential for their schooling, as well as their full right to an emancipatory public education, in all levels.
