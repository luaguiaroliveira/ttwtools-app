<Does Technology make us more alone?>

We are living in a world where the information runs each times faster, and it is getting hard to not belong or follow these new tendencies. We stay all day with our phones in hand, receiving and sending information, talking at the same time with a friend in China and with our neighbors. But the question is, are we really talking to them? Is it possible to have a real conversation at the same time with lots of people and to stay present in each one? That is the question that disrupts our generation and that is really present in the 21th century. In this Essay I am going to discuss if, with the new technologies and ways of communication, people are getting more distant one of each other.

One of the characteristics of technology is that it can change distances, for example making someone who is in the other side of the world be talking to you at real time, which can make great distances closer. But how it can change the short distances? People who are near you begin to become more distant because we are starting to use technology as a bridge to all kind of relations, we talk to our friends who are in the same room as us through our phones and we talk to our parents and brothers who live in the same house as we using a whatsaap group and not in a lunch or dinner every day. People are starting to see the technology as a better and unique way to interact.

Another issue considerable is that the use of technology as the main platform to work leads to reduce the capacity of the workers to pass trough uncomfortable situations and reach objectives by them selves. It’s easier to send a message to your employer when you are late or when you did something quite wrong than to personally go to talk with him, make explanations and probably be reproved. Video conferences and e-mails are also ways to alleviate the impact of the workers facing an uncomfortable situation. People are getting too much comfortable and reducing their capacity of interaction in the work plan, making thing in the easier way and it leads to the formation of professionals less able to do their work.
This use of technology to interact with other people plus the adaption of people's professionalism to an easy way to make work interactions can also result in a self-exclusion of society. Work are being bring to our homes and with this elasticity to do everything you need in your professional area from home makes people stop interacting with the society and excluding themselves. Is it easier to access everything you need without leaving home, food, clothes, movies, work, “social interaction” that it becomes to be a lifestyle. A new word where we can do and be whatever we want is inside, and the outside reality gets the second plan.
Finally, what I wanted to emphasize is this accommodation in a new reality created by us. With technology, everything can be made with more facility, but not completely effective, and it is easier to avoid uncomfortable situations. We start to be used to take easier ways in every dimension, at work, at school, with our Family and friends, and by that way we end up loosing a bit of the reality. People should be more careful about how they are using the new Technologies and try to not make it impact in their relation with other real people.
