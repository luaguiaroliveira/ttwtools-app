<Why Self-knowledge is Indispensable in Every Aspect of our Lives>

In nowadays the outside information is increasingly gaining more importance than the knowledge that we can gather from ourselves. The experiences that the world can provide leads us to chase materialistics goals, and, for many times, we struggle at things that are supposed to be simple, as the interpersonal relationships. The profound analysis of how our brain thinks is the main tool to understand yourself and others, and from this, be able to react better to adversities - as Carl Jung (founder of analytical psychology) defended. The theory around this subject is very rich and, after the comprehension of the enormous applicability of knowing how to use it, is impossible to just live without.
How can we stop and think more about our actions instead of just live in the moment? This can be the hardest task of our lives, and, if we don’t get to know the methods to achieve this, it becomes impossible. The first step in solving any kind of problem is to identify and characterize it. So, in this case, we need to establish the central points of our failures. The method of developing this is to have the courage to face the ugliests imperfections of our own.
Every fear that we have is derived from an unreasonable motive. If it is not faced and rationalized, it will hunt you for the rest of your live. Take a time to think rationally in those situations: a woman who is jealous of her companion, an anxious student on exams eve and a man afraid of take blood. Wouldn't be easier to see if there is any practical action they can take instead of unreasonable overthinking the problem? In general lines, facing our problems shall be painful, but is the shortest path to achieve the so wanted inner peace.
To apply this train of thought is very important to know yourself, but also the others around you, since we live in community and relations between people are imprescindible. There is always a better method of proceeding in everything, and in this case, to unravel the brain functions that grants the characteristics of our personalities. The model proposed by Carl Jung, David Mark Keirsey and Isabel Briggs Myers is widespread and well accepted as a complete well-funded theory. Briefly every person in the world can be categorized as belonging to one of sixteen personalities, having each one four predominant functions. Knowing how to categorize yourself and the people around you makes easier to comprehend each individual mistakes and qualities, including yours.
To course the journey of self-acceptance and knowledge have got a little easier now, isn’t true? Once rationalized the existing risks and possibles unforeseen by a well-studied methodology everything is possible, because now you comprehend the motives behind the facts, and is no more a victim of them.
