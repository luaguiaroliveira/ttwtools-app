Children are not submitted in a formal education to learn how to speak. In general, children develop the ability to speak just listening people, for example their parents, and trying to speak these new words. These arguments point to the hypothesis that children are more propensive to learn foreign language faster. Putting the foreign language in the children scholar curriculum will contribute to prepair them to the subsequent learning education levels, mainly because often it is necessary to read non native language articles or books, which is a barrier for those who do not know the foreign language of interest. This methodology would accelerate the learning rate even in another areas and will enhance the global educational level. Another point is that stimulating a foreign language class is a great approach to face the general problem in older people that are restrictive to make mistakes when they try to speak. Children have no fear to speak and make mistakes in a class, what is, probably, the secret of children learn faster than older age students. The process to repeatedly trying is an empirical sense that the learning rate of someone is greater than the others. The human brain learns by the process of repetition. When we learn something that is not put in practice, is too likely we forget that thing. But when we are under the needing to speak, for example in a class environment, we force our brain to accomodate the new knowledge in a memory section more likely to be used frequently. In another way, the brain will make weak links among synapses that can be recycled by the brain any time. Learning by problems is a paradigm that have to be stimulated even in children curriculum for development of the knowledge world. Finally, stimulate the early foreign language learning is a great approach for our children curriculum.
