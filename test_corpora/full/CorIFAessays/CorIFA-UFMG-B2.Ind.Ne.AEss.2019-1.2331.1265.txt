<General Relativity and the Develpment of Innovative Devices in Electrical Engineering>

Albert Einstein (1915, 1916) published the general relativity theory, a geo-
metric description of how gravitation works. This theory had an important role
on developing modern astrophysics. However, it has implications in other areas,
such as electrical engineering. Leonhardt and Philbin (2006) used Einstein's
deductions in association with recent metamaterials discoveries to formulate in-
teresting applications to the control over electromagnetic elds. In this essay,
I will describe how the use of general relativity can be benecial in developing
innovative devices in electrical engineering.
Einstein's theory brought a new way on working with physical space. General
relativity introduced the idea of time-space media, which can be applied to elec-
tromagnetic media. One application is the development of invisibility devices.
Metamaterials have nanostructures that can be built to have specic proper-
ties and follow a certain function. One of these properties is the employment of
anisotropic media, where electrical permittivity and magnetic permeability vary
through spatial coordinates. This type of media allows negative refraction and
therefore, it is possible to manipulate a electromagnetic eld to t in a space
around a determined area without interacting with it (Leonhardt & Philbin,
2006). This means, for example, that light can pass through an object with-
out any perception of it, because the media created with metamaterials curves
propagation direction around the object.
Another application of negative refraction Leonhardt (2006) refers to is the
creation of perfect lenses. These devices act as usual lenses, but they have
additional properties that make them more precise. Since metamaterials' size
is comparable to wavelengths of electromagnetic waves, they can be molded
to turn a desired function into fact. This means that a lens made with them
facilitates an exact coordinate transformation, so the resolution of the image
gets nearly perfect since eects of diraction barely exist.
On one hand, the general relativity can help electrical engineering with new
and precise devices. On the other hand, the engineers can help develop other
sciences with those devices, including astrophysics. Black holes are a diffcult
object of study because of its complexity and measure limitations. These diffi-
culties can be solved by engineering devices allied with the metamaterial appli-
cations explained before to create articial black holes in laboratory (Leonhardt
& Philbin, 2006). This would allow physicists to study the elusive effects of gen-
eral relativity deeply and space-time singularities caused by black holes.
In conclusion, the evolution of metamaterials and the way they interact with
electromagnetic time-space can produce innovation in electrical engineering. In-
visibility devices, perfect lenses and creation of articial black holes are examples
of how this new technology can help the development of eld and wave electro-
magnetics manipulation, besides other areas that can utilize these devices for
another applications.
