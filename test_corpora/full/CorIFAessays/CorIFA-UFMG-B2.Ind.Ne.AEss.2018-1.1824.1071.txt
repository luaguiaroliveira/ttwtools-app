Antibodies are known to react specifically to certain substances, making it a great ally in the creation of immunospecific devices, best known as immunosensors. Their importance are highlighted when is considered that snake attacks account for thousands of deaths annually, mainly in tropical regions [1] and developing countries. [2] As a result of the authorities' high level of negligence, World Health Organization (WHO) in 2009 included snakebites on the list of neglected tropical diseases. Between 2007 and 2015 1,180,847 accidents were caused by venomous animals in Brazil, of which 179,501 were caused by snakes of the genus Bothrops. [3] Advantages over venom type diagnosis using immunosensors can be listed as, for example, their rapid response and high sensitivity capability.
Because the construction method used in this kind of sensor, the antibodies are positioned on the top of the sensor, making possible that they make direct contact with the sample. [4] This characteristic is responsible for the rapid response of the biosensor as they do not need previous complex preparations as in other methods. For example, other methods can take hours to provide result, while biosensors take minutes. [2;5]
As antibodies have high sensitivity, small sample amounts are needed for the diagnosis. [2] For the patient, this is a very important advantage, since some tipes of snake venoms can cause big blood loss across hemorrhagea. [1;3] An other interesting fact is that a high sensitive sensor does not need big venom concentrations on the blood to accuse a positive signal for the presence of the poison, making diagnosis more straight and simple. [2]
With all the exposed, it is simple to conclude that researches across poison identification are very important to decrease the time needed for the result come out and increase the chance of survival of the patients, principally of childrens.
