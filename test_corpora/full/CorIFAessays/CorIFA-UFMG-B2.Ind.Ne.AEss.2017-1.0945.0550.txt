<Some people believe computer and video games are harmful to children, while others disagree.> <What do you think?>
Technology has invaded the everyday routine of everyone and kids are not out of that. They are also a target, considering their necessity of safety, special equipment and products and, mainly, entertainment. Because of that, industry is developing more and more devices to keep children entertained: computers and video games are examples of what kids have access and as every gadget, they have pros and cons. Considering those “cons”, it is possible to affirm that they can be harmful.
Firstly, it is important to consider the concept of something harmful. It is not about things that cause only problems; to offer h arm, one thing is enough: the potential to be bad in some situation. So, if what you are analyzing has 1% of chance to do not be good, it can be considered harmful. All of that means that basically anything potentially harmful, once the environment will always affect.
Another condition that exemplifies the power of harm existence in those devices is the addiction. Obviously the games and apps available are very fun to those who are using it, and a lot more interesting than doing housework, homework or to tidy a room. That is the moment when limits are required: parents should establish a time and date that kids can play video games or use the computer for fun. Once there are limits, the use will not be a problem; it will just help on entertaining little people full of energy.
One more aspect that can be a trouble is the content available. Children can have access to information that they do not have maturity enough to deal with or, if they do have, maybe it will not be approached on the best and most appropriate way. Once again, parents should interpose and be aware of what is being used, searched and what kids are in contact with and how it influence them.
In conclusion, computers and video games are harmful to children, once they can expose them to inappropriate content, get them addicted and with a limited line of though. But, if those gadgets are well used, there will not be bad consequences to kids; on fact, it will contribute to improve a faster logic, to entertain, to allow them to get in touch to what is happening outside their city, country or continent.
