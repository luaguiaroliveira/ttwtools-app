<Working Title: The disadvantages of not having access to the Internet>

The Internet is an indispensible resource considering it grants many positive marks in society nowadays. Its origin is related to the 1960s, when the United States succeeded in interconnection computer networking. Since then, it has improved considerably, and many tasks which needed to be done in person have been automatized, which results in the fact that a person who does not have Internet access lacks all these opportunities granted by this device. Hence, Internet should be available to everyone because it innovates education, simplify searches and it also increases companies and personal productivity.

Internet spreads updated knowledge and influences many people to study online by making available all sorts of texts and files, video lessons and specific courses such as graduations. By consulting Google, for example, a person can find inumerous information about an updated specific subject from anywhere around the world instantly. Moreover, there are many examples of qualified courses, such as edX, a massive open online course founded by Harvard University and MIT in 2012, which makes possible for a student from any country to learn about many fields of interest, such as engineering, languages, biology and marketing. In this way, education has also been inovated due to the cyberspace.

Concerning communication, distance cannot be considered an issue anymore because the Internet made available communication through the use of e-mail, chats and video calls. In this way, it is possible to contact anyone who has access to this resource, independently of where this person is. Moreover, it also impacts a country's economy. Online shopping is already a reality to many companies, phenomenom which increased considerably selling due to the practicity this way of shopping provides since people can purchase a merchandise even without leaving home. It also helps costumers to decide which product is, indeed, the best for them and they can also look out for the store where that specific item is cheaper. In conclusion, it provides another possibility to buy products without the need to wait in any queue.

Pondering all positive impacts the Internet causes in personal and professional daily situations, it can, indeed, be considered a simplifier to society. By automatizing, many different aspects such as education, communication, productivity and searches have improved since the creation of the Internet. All those services do not demand that people do it personally anymore. Therefore, distance cannot be considered an issue any longer and it takes less time to do them than before the existance of the cyberspace. For those reasons, everyone should have access to Internet in order to be benefited by the inumerous innovations regarded to this resource.
