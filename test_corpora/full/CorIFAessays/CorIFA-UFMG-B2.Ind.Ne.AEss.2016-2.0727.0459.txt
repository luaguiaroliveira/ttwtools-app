<Does technology make us more alone?>

Technology has grown considerably over the years and its impact can be observed worldwide. Many changes have occurred and continue to occur due to technological advancement, bringing with them many advantages. We can highlight progress of media, greater ease of communication between people who live far away, scientific advances, among other factors. But, despite all this, it is possible that technology make us lonely? I believe that often, yes, and here I will show my reasons.
People addicted to technology tend to be antisocial. This can be observed when someone spends more time at home playing a computer game than going out with friends, or when someone, on a table surrounded by friends, only pay attention to your phone, instead of interacting with others.
Another reason is that, sometimes, people prefer to interact by social networking than in person. This ends up distancing the parties involved, besides contributes to a sedentary lifestyle. Furthermore, the fact of not seeing the person personally, can lead to wrong interpretations and misunderstandings.
Finally, the wide accessibility of social networks often contributes to discrimination. Whether by race, religion, sexual orientation or social class, we see many cases of widespread discrimination by technology. This leads to the estrangement of people with different characteristics and opinions, making them indirectly loneliest.
In other words, technology can be a good thing in many ways, but from the moment it becomes an addiction or is used for bad intentions, is no longer beneficial to society. The conscious and moderate use of technology is essential to keep a good relationship between people.
