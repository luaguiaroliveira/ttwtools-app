<Are Electric Vehicles better than ICE vehicles?>

The idea of the Electric Vehicles (EV’s) is very good, it is about reducing the pollutions of the Internal Combustion Engine Vehicles (ICE), but the technology and way which are built the current EV’s makes then not much better than ICE Vehicles or motorbikes. The wrong discard of batteries of an EV can pollute more than emissions of an ICE vehicle, these batteries are also expensive and does not lasts a long time. This kind of cars also does not have great autonomy. This essay will explain this three main reasons why currently the EV’s are not much better than other vehicles.
The discard of batteries of an Electric Vehicle is a great issue when this type of car be popular, like other batteries it has a big amount of chemicals which can harm the environment – can be even more than pollution of the ICE vehicles. The same way people can’t throw away in ordinary places the batteries of their TV’s remote controls, they must not discard the batteries of their EV’s in wrong places. The correct places to discard might also be expensive, which is something that companies who sells this cars don’t tell. The electric vehicles have also other issue, like any other electric device, their batteries does not have a great lifespan, so considering the actual technology, these batteries must be replaced in small period of time, and this can be expensive for many reasons, like size of the battery and taxes in countries like Brazil.
The autonomy of this kind of car is other problem, usually it does not have a great autonomy so people can’t make travels with them, which have a relation to others issues as long time to recharge – some of these cars can take almost twelve hours to recharge their batteries - and the infrastructure in countries like Brazil, to have this kind of service in gas stations in road, especially on the countryside.
In conclusion, the idea of car that not produce gases to environment is good, but it can pollute in others ways and have a great price, so currently, solutions like Hybrid vehicle – a car that have a combustion engine and also a electric engine – or just Internal Combustion Engine Vehicles with other fuel – like Ethanol – can be a better solution presently. But in the future, the technology of the EV’s will probably evolve and this car might be as popular as ICE vehicles.
