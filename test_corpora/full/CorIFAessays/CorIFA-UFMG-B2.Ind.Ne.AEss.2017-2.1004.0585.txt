It is known that cloning is a production of identical genetical cells or individuals. Identical twins are an example of cloning, and that type of example have being seen for a long time in nature. The first animal clone, intentionally created by man, appeared in the late 19th century and, dispite it controversies, human cloning can be a priviledge to every body.
In 2005, a ban on all forms of human cloning was adapted by the General Assembly of the United Nations. This declaration claimed that this type of procedure is incompatible with human dignity and security. Problems could be caused by the release of cloning. The possibility of creating a black market for clones, modifying genetic mortability, or later the difficulty of identifying people in criminal or other situations are examples of problems.
In other wise, human cloning could collaborate with the medicine progress. Infertile couples could have the possibility of having children with their genetical cells and medicinal theraphies could be improved. The chances of achieving a transplantation of genetically compatible organs would increase with the release of cloning. And these are just a few benefits that the population could have.
Although it is something that still needs to be widely discussed by society, human cloning should be considered for a huge advance in world medicine. Further discussions and campaigns should be made to publicize the subject in order to make human cloning a public issue. In this way, the society would be able to speak on the subject and perhaps change the decision of the General Assembly of the United Nations.
