<The impacts of the real estate in Soberbo stream – Serra do Cipó, Brasil>

The city of Serra do Cipó is suffering due the time with a big real estate expansion, correlated with an intense populational grow. This modern situation affects the fauna and flora of this place, considering that the place is historically a natural refuge, causing the disappearance of many species, especially species of fishes. This essay will provide a diagnostic about the human interference and the disappearing of fishes in Soberbo stream, the main river of the city.

The disappearing of fishes it’s correlated with the decrease of the water quality of the river. Fishes are really sensitive to changes in their environmental, because their body have some specificity about the levels of Ph, Oxygen demand and amount of heavy metals present in water, it means that if the water present different characteristics than before the animals will die. Is known that brutal changes in the water quality are mainly caused by humans. Humans are the only animal who produces trash and sewage, and these wastes are throws directly in nature, polluting the ground and the rivers. Normally these wastes present a high rate of heavy metal and matter organic, which decrease the levels of Ph and oxygen demand of the water, causing a death of these animals.

Cutting the riparian forest has a great impact in reducing the volume of the river, causing the ecosystem to change and the disappearance of several animals, principally fish. Deforestation on the banks of the river is caused mainly by the real estate expansion and this fact causes the silting, which reduces the volume of water in the river. The reduction of such volume causes the change in the local ecosystem, that is, fish adapted to great depths will disappear, because their food will disappear. This fact is more significant than you can imagine, because the animals adapted to greater depth are the most economically visable fish, such as the Dourado and the Traíra, thus causing an economic impact to the fishermen.

In conclusion it is possible to observe that the dumping of garbage and sewage caused by real estate expansion can drastically affect the quality of the water and consequently the fish, besides that such real estate growth can bring other impacts, such as the cutting of the siliceous forest and the silting of the river, which in turn has a negative impact on fish of economic interest. From this, environmental preservation measures such as the treatment of waste and sewage must be taken, in addition to the preservation of the ecosystem near the banks of the river.
