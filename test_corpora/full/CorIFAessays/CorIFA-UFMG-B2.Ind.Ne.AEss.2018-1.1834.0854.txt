<The advantages of the knowledge of Econometrics in the Market> 

When we speak of economics, we are already referring to great changes and transformations and one of the most vital parts in every developed society. However, like almost everything, it lives with variations and surprises that can often end with years of planning. Is it then possible to try to predict future changes according to certain population data? The answer is yes, and this is the Task of Econometrics. We will talk about the importance of studyng it and how we can use it, demonstrating how a professional with this knowledge excels in the market.
The big business of using econometrics is the facts that, from it, we can do a critical analysis of a situation found and then take the best solutions. By definition, econometrics would be the set of techniques and statistical tools that aims to study the relationship between various economic variables (such as macroeconomic factors). For this, we established several economic hypotheses or theories (of the researcher's desire) and we test them from the Econometrics, rejecting them or not and arriving at a consistent conclusion through data analysis.
It’s expressive the advantage of a person who has the knowledge of Econometrics under another that does not have such a domain. A student of econometrics has the ability to transform a agglomeration of data (""unconnected"" to many) into something palpable, where it is possible to obtain a variety of vital information. Using mainly the Linear Regression Model, we can explain a dependent variable (such as ""salary"", for example), by several other explanatory variables, such as age, sex, schooling, among others. Through the statistical methods, the researcher can even see that a variable inserted in the model is really significant for the Model or if it can be excluded from it. We then move from an abstract theory to something more solid and practical, obtaining a formula that can predict an approximate future result according to the variation of explanatory variables.
The use of econometrics greatly helps the interpretation and prediction of future events so that, with the results obtained, the researchers can take the best solutions for each situation found. Through the statistical analysis of the variables, we can determine the most statistically significant characteristics and thus focus particularly on them to create the desired changes. In the future, the tendency is for Econometrics to enter more and more as an area of ​​professional activity, since data tend to increase with the need to understand them.
