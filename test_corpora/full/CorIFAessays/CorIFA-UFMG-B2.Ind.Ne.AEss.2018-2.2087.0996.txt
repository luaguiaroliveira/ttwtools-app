<Animal tests: an unavoidable evil>

Pharmacological treatment of diseases is the major factor of life expectancy growth. The development of new drug has necessarily a step in which animal tests have to be performed and, although this practice could lead to bad feelings to some people, it cannot be avoided. This text aims to discuss about limitations of non-animal tests and ethics aspects of these tests.
A lot of new techniques have been developed in last years to avoid animal tests during drug development. These techniques include, as example, computational and cell culture tools that aims to mimetize biologicals phenomes to study them. Although these tools are important to explain mechanisms of action of studied drugs, they do not allow to infer about their behavior in a complete biological system. This can be explained considering that biological systems (such as cardiovascular, humoral, pulmonary and neuronal) communicate and regulate each other in a complex pathway that cannot be exactly transpose to a simple microenvironment. In fact, non-animals tools can reduce frequency and amounts of animal test but cannot avoid them.
Although animal tests are really necessary to drug development, they have to follow ethical rules that regulate their use in a research. The main idea of ethical rules for animal experiments is to use them when extremely necessary, a minimal as possible and with lower suffering during experimental period. Before using animal in tests, researchers have to submit a research project to an Animal Ethic Committee, in which all experimental conditions, including housing, feed and procedures to reduce suffering have to be detailed and will be analyzed by a group of experts who will decide about agreement. Then, filling all the ethical rules, a safe animal test can be performed.
In conclusion, during drug development process, although new tools are available to reduce the number of animals used in tests, their use is still necessary. Indeed, this use should be performed according to ethical rules that guarantee the best conditions to perform the test, whit last possible suffering for the animal.
