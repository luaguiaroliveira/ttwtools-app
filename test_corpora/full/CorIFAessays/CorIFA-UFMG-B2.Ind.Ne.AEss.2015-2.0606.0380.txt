I agree children should begin learning another language as soon as they start school, because this type of learning process is much more effective and easily done when a person is under 12 years old, according to several studies promoted by neuroscientists all over the world. Considering the actual context of a connected world, it is extremely important to know how to speak and write in a foreign language, in a way that a bilingual or trilingual individual is more appreciate in the labor market, improving his or hers earnings and job opportunities. Furthermore, still considering the global arrangement of a world with less frontiers and more interconnectedness, it is relevant that younger generations receive more preparation to deal with different cultural habits and learn to communicate with individuals from several locations. Moreover, the contact with another language, especially one that differs largely from the native language, consists in a mechanism that allows children to be aware of the existence of distinct forms of culture and social constructions, hence, those children will become more tolerant and flexible citizens, an element of utmost importance in the construction of a democratic and cooperative society. It is also relevant to highlight that many forms of entertainment are transmitted in foreign languages, such as films; music; plays; musicals; books and series, and the access to these elements are widely spread due to the Internet and cable television programs, that lead to their users a variety of instruments of entertainment from several distingue locations and nationalities. However, translations and captions might not capture the real meaning of a word or sentence, in a way that the original meaning and humor are not transmitted completely. So, to avoid this situation, the sooner people are educated in a foreign language, the understanding and utilization of foreign entertainment elements will be done in a more acute way. Hence, it is beneficial, in a long-term period that the children are educated in more than one language, as soon as possible.
