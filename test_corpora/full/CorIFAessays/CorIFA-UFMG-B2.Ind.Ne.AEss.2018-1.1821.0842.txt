<Did They Have Choice?> 

In 1948 the State of Israel was officially created, to the Jewish people that day was perhaps the better day of their lives, to the Palestinians maybe the worst. From that moment the Jewish people star to expulse the Palestinians from what today is Israel – before 1948 was the Palestine – and colonialize the lands. 

The Palestinian people see the Jewish as a new European colonizer, thus some author like Edward Said try to show all the bad things the Jewish people did against the Palestinians. Opposite of this, the Jewish people use the premise that that land belongs to them for a very long time, but they were systematically expulsed from this land several times, however this time they come back to stay. Thus, some Israeli authors admitted that the Jewish people did things that they do not have reasons to be proud, but that they did not have choice, like Benny Morris. The question that someones can do is Did they have a choice? The Jewish people could have chosen another way? 

Good Israeli authors would say no. In this moment the Jewish people (whom came to Israel after 1945 and whom immigrate to Palestinian in the last few decades of the nineteenth century) tried to build a nation for the Jews, a safe place for them. However, after the Second Big War the Palestine was under the British control, and the Jews were afraid to be betrayed (again) this time by the British army. 

Many of the Jews who went to Israel did not have money anymore, did not have a nation, some of them neither their family, they just have their religion (the reason why they were persecuted few years before) and they needed a safe place to live, because the Europe was not a safe place anymore. The Jewish people also needed land to build their houses and to cultivate, some of these lands started to come for the Jewish hands through the Zionist Movement and the Kibbutizm. 

Therefore, the Israelis cannot pretend that they did not do bad things against the Palestinians, However, some of these things were did for a reason, and using a citation present on Benny Morris' book, by W.
