<Why are university students supposed to make Scientific Initiation>

Generally speaking, when people get in college they found themselves immersed in a new world, full of possibilities and opportunities. This situation usually makes students think a lot about how they are going to invest their time during graduation years, and a point that is mainly considered is to make some kind of volunteer work, which is most of the times Scientific Initiation. This, of course, has particular benefits for the undergraduate students, such as having direct contact with people who can help them to learn better about a specific area, and also to get experience they can use during postgraduate studies.        
The first main advantage students can get by participating of a Scientific Initiation Program   is to be in frequent contact with a group of researchers who can guide them in favor to obtain progress. When students use to have this kind of relation with graduated people, they are supposed to acquire a larger range of knowledge in the area their leaders actually work with. This may encourage the students to be further applied in their graduation studies, as they see how beneficial their effort is being for them.                                                        
Doing this kind of work may also increase the interest students have in a specific subject, since they are almost everyday in the research environment. When it happens, the students might found themselves in the position to get all experience that can be necessary for them to start their postgraduate studies. They are also supposed to build relations with people that later may become their mentors, which can smooth the adaptation needed for people to work together for some years. 
In conclusion, the disposition for students to being a part of a Scientific Initiation Program brings some benefits for university students: acquirement of knowledge on its interest area, obtainment of experience with a specific kind of work environment and interaction with active graduated people.  It is important for students to know about those benefits now, and in the future to inspire new students to clear the science fields.
