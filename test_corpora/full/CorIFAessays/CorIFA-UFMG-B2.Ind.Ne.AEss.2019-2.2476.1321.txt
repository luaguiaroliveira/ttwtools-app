<The benefits of early age sterilization in dogs and cats> 

<Introduction>
The growing number of animals abandonment and its rapid proliferation represents a public health problem in Brazil. Homeless animals are vulnerable to mistreatment, to suffer and to cause accidents and to spread diseases to each other and to humans. The castration is the main mechanism to control this population in a human way. This essay analyzes the financial and welfare benefits of the early age sterilization of the needy animals
<Body>
It is estimated that this year in Belo Horizonte, more of 4.000 animals lives in the street. Patronek et al. says that animals most at risk of abandonment is those who was adopted, those with behavioral problems, older that 6 months and those who was not trained. What increases the population of abandoned animals is their big capacity of generating descendents, once female dogs and cats are multiparous, have quick gestations and achieve sexual maturity early. Abandoned animals could generate many sanity problems: they are affected and transmit zoonozes as leishmaniasis, rage and sphorotricosis, can be victims and cause auto accidents; they make attacks to other animals and to people and are totally vulnerable to mistreatment crimes. 
The cirurgic sterilization - ovariosalpingohisterectomy in females and orquiectomy in males - is a efficient, definitive and safe to prevent the reproduction of these animals. Moreover, as Johnston says, these surgeries have the advantage of giving prevention to various diseases of the reproductive organs and estrogen dependents problems. Generally the emasculation is made in the age of 6 months, when both male and female animals are sexually mature. However, how do explain Mackay, several programs of conventional age castration have failed in North America because the adopters did not return with the animals for the surgery when they achieve the age. 
In the premature gelding, the dog or cat gives the shelter castrated, regardless their age. This turns the control most effectively, once it do not depend of the tutor responsibility. Furthermore, according to Macedo, this procedure has quick recovery and lower incidence of complications, being so safe as when realized in any other age. More advantages of this method are less surgical and anesthetic expenses and efficiently application of the resources whereas the animal are already in the shelter and they don't need to recall them.
In conclusion, the early age sterilization could be the best mechanism to control the needy animals population because of the financial and welfare benefits that it brings. The governments and ONGs, as the vets, should consider adopting this protocol in the future in way to give efficiently application of their wherewithal.
