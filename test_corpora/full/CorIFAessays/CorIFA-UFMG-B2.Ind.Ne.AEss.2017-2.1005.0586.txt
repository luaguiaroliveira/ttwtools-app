The reparation program of brazilian Justice Minister’s Amnesty Commission needs to pay more attention to gender justice. This is a national program created to repair human rights gross violations victims, their families and the whole society, perpetrated by state during the dictatorship, between 1964 and 1985. Although this is a very large-scale program, it was not created to deal with specific gender-based violations, like sexual violence, or rape. Indeed, this lack of attention about women’s experience as gross violations of human rights victims and its adequate accountability has led to significant consequences in women’s lives, wich provides a growing demand for studies in this area, International Law of Human Rights field.
Therefore, we need to discuss what gender justice is. Gender justice “can be defined as the ending of – and if necessary the provision of redress for – inequalities between women and men that result in women’s subordination to men” (GOETZ, 2007, p. 30-31). For example, we can think about all public politics implemented to deal with and put end on women’s subordination, as implementation of universal suffrage. Moreover, the domestic’s labour division is a gender justice discussion very important aspect. Therefore, the gender justice conceptualization is important because it is through those public politics that government can put an end to women’s subordination.
However, if the government do not implement public politics to eliminate those inequalities, there will be consequences. For example, sexual violence against women was a gross human right’s violations that ocurred in last Authoritarian regime, perpetrated by dictatorship’s agents and keeps occurring in democratic regime. Here in Brazil, a woman is raped every eleven minutes (https://exame.abril.com.br/brasil/os-numeros-da-violencia-contra-mulheres-no-brasil/). Futhermore, domestic violence, wich includes physical and psychological damages, needs governments attention too. For example, the Maria da Penha Law, wich was promulgated in 2006, it did facilitate women’s protection through state’s action.
