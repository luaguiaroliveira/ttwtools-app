<Learning English online or in the Classroom>

The choice between learning English on line or in the classroom have not to be made, because blending learning is the best new choice. Nowadays, learning online is becoming more common than in a few decades, and learning other languages, especially English, is getting more and more popular. Since the early development of the online programs, people are lead to think about which the best is the best way to learn. Learning English has been using as an exemple, but the advantages and disadvantages of each method can be extended to other trainings. 
Now in 2018, the technological apparatus, the reduced price of the computer, the growth smart-phones number, and their applications, are bringing more and more students to leaning English on line, but this approach of learning far from school is not new as demonstrated bellow.
“Since the internet as we know it is a relatively recent phenomenon, it's easy to assume the history of distance education has been a short one. Simply accessing course content online, however, is only one of the latest developments in a history that's been around since the 1700s.” (Miller. G, 2014)
However, vantages and disadvantages have to be considered before the final decision. For instance advantages of learning online when lessons can be taken anywhere, lots of software and smart-phones app available, and the communication with tutors is very easy online or by email, so distance is not an obstacle. On the other hand, in the classroom, the students socialize more with other students, doubts can be resolved immediately with the instructor; as most lessons are charged, more attention and dedication are supposed for them and consequently learning are better, finally classroom attendance is preferred by many students.
Neither learning on-line exclude learning in class, nor learning in class excludes the first one, for we are able to have regular class, and in advance improve our training by having on line class and use the existent facilities to help learning more in apps or internet sites. Furthermore, if we are applied as regular students of a public university and some privates also, several English on line curse may be available. Using internet also can provide not only additional trainings in foreign universes, but also additional free material to help learning. In conclusion, there is no choice to be done because  the best of each method can be used together.
