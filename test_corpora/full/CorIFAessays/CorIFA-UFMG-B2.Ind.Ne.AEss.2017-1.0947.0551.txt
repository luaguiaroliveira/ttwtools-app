<Some people believe computer and video games are harmful to children, while others disagree. What do you think? Take a position and defend it.>
In modern societies, technology has been conquering its space by making people’s daily routines easier day after day. Either by providing fast internet access facilitating research, or by improving communication among people in distant places, the advance of technologies changed the population’s lives; not only the adults but also the children’s ones. With the expansion of computers and video games, many children prefer to play with these electronics instead of the old games to spend their free time. In this context, some people believe computer and video games are harmful to children so, in this essay, it will be discussed how the use of technologies affects children’s lives and how it can have a positive impact.
Besides the fact that the fast and easy internet access facilitated research and democratized access to information, it provided the emergence of a vast quantity of games with different themes, in a way that everyone can find one of his or her own interest. This can help practicing and, as a consequence, improve someone’s skills in this area. A common example is the logical games that are easy to find on the internet and enable thinking abilities.
Computer and video games became very popular in infant universe. Having the possibility of living a different life, doing activities like playing soccer with famous people, exploring new worlds and building a hole city is very stimulating for a child’s mind, turning it into a huge attractive for them. This is one reason that justifies why the use of these electronics have been arising in the last years; without consider the fact that either boys or girls are able to play with them.
In the modern actual world, everything is being based on technologies. Companies spend significant quantities of money for having the right of using softwares and also to have someone who has the ability to deal with them. So, by using electronics since an early age, children are likely to know how to work in a technological context and, in addition, as they are used to it, they will have the creativity to do other stimulating games and good computer programs.
It is obvious that everything in excess is harmful, not only for children but for everyone. Knowing how to have balance in life is a thing that everyone must learn and try to apply on a daily routine. Having contact with technology since young do prepare children for their adult life, in their work and in their academic life, since the majority of tasks are done in the virtual world.
