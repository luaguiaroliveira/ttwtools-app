Since the end of 2012, solar energy in Brazil has become an option for consumers who wish to generate their own energy through the installation and use of so-called photovoltaic systems connected to the grid. The School of Engineering has an Experimental Photovoltaic Plant installed on the fourth floor and this essay will address the possibility of monitoring a large photovoltaic network with equipment at low cost. This research will show that Raspberrys are the tools used to make this happen.

Firstly, it is known that the classic profile of the user of solar energy in Brazil, today, is the family of medium or high income with a property of considerable value, which looks for the solar photovoltaic system as a way to save on your energy bill. To ensure this for a larger network of people, Raspberry Pi is a credit card-sized computer, which connects to a computer or TV monitor, and uses a standard keyboard and mouse, developed in the United Kingdom by the Foundation Raspberry Pi. The research involves relating them to the inverters and switches that are already working to control and monitor the exchanges made. This ensures that solar energy consumers will know effectively if the system is working.
	
In addition, currently, public and private banks offer specific financing for society to acquire a solar network. Having a private public initiative in this field is fundamental for the development of the project and guarantee of the expansion of the study. On the other hand, the purchase of the materials was made and we are waiting for your arrival. The purpose of this is the implementation within the School of Engineering of UFMG.

In conclusion, having control of the data that the photovoltaic panels issue is crucial to make sure the conversion is happening. Ensuring this certainty is important for the Engineering School because students will be able to work on that and create new studies. Given the economic nature of the Raspberrys, we note that the project is cheap and can be implemented in low-cost networks.
