<Essay- Topic: Which is more important: talent or hard work?>
Achieve or able to expert level of ability in your area are dream for everybody. Be the best in anything requiring greater level of proficiency. Nowadays, exist different studies and theories for try explain what influence anybody is or not expert, investigations in game sport use test by laboratory for discuss this subject.‘ Achieved or able to’ expert level is question that return for discussion:” Which is more important: talent or hard work?”. My argumentation will be based in research in area of game sports and I believe with this, hard work is more important, but cannot forget which existed talents.
First, work hard is one way for acquire experience. In game sports, experience is very important. This affirmation is based in research where can verified who expert in game sport dedicated many hours of practice. This practice can divided in hours by training and hours in competition. Because during practice in game sports, training or competition, person experiment different situations that will be stored in your memory. This memory will be important when athlete perceive one new situation similar already happened. So, will can remember like made decision and achieved the best result in task.
Continuing the previous idea, work hard is opportunity for practice in greater level of quality. Ericsson, Krampe e Tesch-Romer (1993) argue that it’ s possible achieve expertise with 10.000h of deliberate practice, with this practice it’ s possible stored experience, and create mental links that can develop abilities achieving new levels by practice, also obtain better results. But, practice in lower level of quality cannot help a person develop your abilities, because this kind of practice not requires who expert aces new mental links and cause learned.
Finally, work hard is opportunity for automatize movements. According Greco (2006b), one of the moments by technique training in game sports, pass for moment of automatize movements and reduce use attention for realize the technique. So, it's possible athletes direction your attention for other information in environment and perceive what be options of actions for achieved the best result in certain situation. Furthermore, with automatize the athletes can reduce spent byenergy for execute task ,and stored energy for other actions that require greater spends.
In conclusion, with work hard athletes can acquire experience creating link of memory for next practice, can practice in greater level of quality and obtain new levels by results and automatize movements reducing use by attention , so direct attention new information in environment.
