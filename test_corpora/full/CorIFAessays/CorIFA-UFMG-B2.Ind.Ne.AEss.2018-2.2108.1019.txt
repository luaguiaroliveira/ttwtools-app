In 2016 alone in the USA, road risks caused over 40,000 deaths are related with vehicles and more than 1,000 people per day injured due to distracted driving, besides that an amount of $87 billion of costs in accidents due to human errors in 2013. An alternative to this is to use driverless buses, more safety, they are the future in the public transport.
The Ibope published in 2016 a report which 20% of the urban workforce spend more than an hour to go to work. This scenario will be worse in ten years when Belo Horizonte for example, will have 91 kilometers of traffic jam in rush hour, the consumption of gas and costs of transport will increase. Although investments in the public buses were made and creation of BRT in capitals, a similar and cheaper alternative to subways, to enhance traffic and improve efficiency in transport. But can be dangerous when parking in the bus station and to board passengers, today subways implement many control systems to park correctly on stations, prevent accidents like derailments and collisions. The BRT can be the first driverless models of buses due to fewer environment variables than normal traffic.
To install upgrades and become a automated bus, firstly the current fleet will need to be modified by adding multiple sensors and actuators, next in streets using computer vision mapping streets, the buses will be tested with a bus driver on board to take action if necessary, then the artificial intelligence will learn how to drive on those streets, after that when gets approved by public opinion, the vehicle can be operated remotely. Finally, the automated vehicle will run nonstop integrated which can monitor the streets, send information about the traffic to improve the bus fleet management in real time. 
From an environmental and economic point of view, by building electrically-powered buses, companies are able to reduce gas emissions, spend less in a cheaper power supply, easier maintenance of electric motor due to less mechanical parts and can run uninterruptedly only need to stop to recharge. The electric buses associated with driverless buses might create a  high technology industry, making Belo Horizonte one of the pioneer's cities in this area.
In conclusion, nowadays China, Germany, Japan, and others countries are studying and testing this idea. However, Brazil has some problems to resolve before such as the lack of legal support, tax incentives, programmes of technology development, and partnership with companies to agree with this change in the fleet. with public support, politics might create incentives that provide a condition to start the development and test in the national territory.
