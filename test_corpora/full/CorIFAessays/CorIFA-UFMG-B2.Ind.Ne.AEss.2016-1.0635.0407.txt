<Educational system failure>

The analysis of the proposals of changing the academic grade made by MEC (Secretaria de Educação Básica do Ministério da Educação- Department of Basic Education of the Ministry of Education) shows that the system is poorly innovative, focusing only in modifying the feature of the existing school subjects. But I believe that the answer to improve the learning process is far from the conventional methods. Our actual educational system is headed to mass teaching, using classes fulfilled with the maximum amount of people, and disciplines that brings mainly theoretical scientific knowledge forgetting about the application even in the academic field as in the routine life.
A seminary on the Educational Council in July 7th, 2015 that meant to reformulate the high school presents ideas that presents the weakness of our system like “A schedule of a country can not be unable to answer three things: for whom to offer education, for what and how to offer this education”, José Fernandes de Lima integrant of the National Educational Council, and “We are doing a Enem (National High School’s Exam) that fits the curriculum as the high school was a transition stage for the college”, Eduardo Deschamps, president of the National Council of Secretaries of Education (Consed). This shows that MEC chose to dispose subjects that will lead the students to the college, highlighting the lack of concern about the personal improvement and the exacerbate concern about the academic issue. Although they want to lead the students, the first quote reveal that is not clear the profile of our students and their needs.
We do not have disciplines focused on functional matters like economics, politics, social issues, for example simple things as going to the bank, how is our political structure, comprehension of our social standards. And more important, the development of the vary intelligence types as Musical, Naturalist, Bodily-Kinesthetic and Interpersonal intelligence offering singing and instruments classes, culinary, dance and rhythms, sports of every kind and oral performance.
The educational system should focus on the interest of the student individually giving them options to build their own curricular grade and not instituting a relentless grade that is the same for all the students. And in that way build a person capable of critical analysis in deferent areas instead of build a reproducer of general methods and other people ideas.
