I am an animation student at UFMG, coursing Cinema de Animação e Artes Digitais. It is a great opportunity, since there aren’t many alternatives to this kind of specialization here in Brazil. However, the structure of our discipline program isn’t very good. It tries to embrace all kinds of animation techniques in addition to, as it wasn’t enough, the “Artes Digitais”'s area. That’s a huge mistake, of course. The truth is: the course should be more specific.

“Is important to learn the basics of every area of the animation industry”. All right, we can understand it, and we do agree with it. But, we can’t spend all of our time learning the basics. That should be an introduction, and, as soon as the student reaches a certain stage of the course, he choses a more specific trajectory.

Animation is a huge technique. To do a good animated movie, you must go through a long process. There are many different kinds of animations, and each one of them demands a different set of skills. It is a hard craft to teach, and it gets harder if you try to teach a different craft, such as Digital Art, jointly. Consequently, the students interested in Animation struggles to pass through the Digital Art’s side of the course, and vice-versa. Animation needs and deserves a graduation course entirely devoted to it.

A student shouldn’t have to try so hard to pass through disciplines that he does not like to learn the one thing that he loves. If that was an essential part of his formation, ok, but it is not. As a result, we have professionals that didn’t have the time to study what they are going to do for the rest of his life because he wasted it learning just enough of other areas of his profession to pass the exams. And now, he is alone to find his own way on the industry. That is the consequence of what we have now, so why don’t change?
