<The contrasts of public and particular education in XXI century>

I am a tutor in the city of Belo Horizonte. I teach quemistry, mathematics and sciences (for early kids). I am the one that in most of time people search when their tests are about to happen. So today, I will talk about my reflections concerning education latterly.
Public education is a chaos. The government did a reform called “plural school” in which they cannot reprove people for low profit. The result is analphabetism, bad education, inversion of moral values, disrespect with the professor and with the classmates, and many other factors. The idea of “I can do anything that nothing will happen to me” is common in young’s minds.
Today’s families are different from past families. In contrast with the past, both mother and father go to work and they expect that putting their kids in a very good school, the school will teach them everything (in many cases it includes how to behave too). I deal with kids and adolescents who study in high-priced and renowned schools, as Magnum and Maximus. In many cases, they do not know even how to write a text with verbal concordance, and they do not respect their parents.
Particular classes are expensive. It is not something accessible to anybody. In the end of the academic year, they contract me to “bombard” their children with all the given matter in one year. Moreover, the result is that the children only memorize the sufficient to pass, and then they forget the matter.
I never gave classes to a student from a public school, because they do not need to endeavor to pass the year. We can conclude that is very hard for a person who came from a public school to ingress in a federal university as UFMG. In contrast with this reality, we go to particular schools, and we can infer that the school can be the best of all schools, but if the kids do not have an everyday support, they will not have good results. Besides, these parents have money to pay for a good preparatory to university. Hence, we have elitist federal universities.
