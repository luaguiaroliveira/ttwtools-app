<Using wood can change sustaintability and occupation> 

Sustainable architecture is needed nowadays because only 5 % of our constructions are reused. We need to start thinking about how we inhabit and how it impacts our lifes, because constructions produces too garbage. Besides reusing materials can be easier than we imagine. Today there are strategies to be less shocking in environment at building, we can use wood and recycled concrete for example, so we can be more positive in habitating our planet.  

The concrete is one of the biggest problem on sustainable world, because it really accumulates a big volume of garbage on environment. So, one option to change this reality is to use recycled concrete, wich is product of other construction that where demolished. The second option is the use of wood, even on tropical countries, where wood can be attacked by insects. The wood can be treated, by removing it’s water, so it becomes more protected. The use of wood on constructions is a good option because it takes off billions of CO2 of Earth's atmosphere and can be reused too. So, is more sustainable and helps to solve another problems on environment 

Another advantage in using wood in constructions is that it can be really economic, because the owner of a building can order exactly how much wood will be needed, and wastes can be drastically reduced. Than, construction can be more clean and organized, but a skilled labor is required because of details in using this type of material. In Brazil, only 3% of 500 thousand m³ of wood is used in constructions. This number reveals a cultural prejudice in using wood because is believed that it is not strong enough to be a structure of a house and is too expensive. All of this precepts are not true and delay the use of wood in our country, meanwhile it’s use grows like a trend in another parts of world. 
 
So, trying to help environment on our constructions should not be a option, but something primary in our ways of life. We really need to change our way to inhabit, because it don’t match with our humanity that is inserted on a environment that is not only ours. Or even better, is ours as well. Today, we have good strategies and options that are good enough to be really used, like using wood on constructions, so that’s a question of concernment. Besides that, in Brazil, we have area sufficient to reforest and to plant wood for wide use on constructions. All we need to do is to change some wrong prejudgements and the way we inhabit our shared home, that's Earth.
