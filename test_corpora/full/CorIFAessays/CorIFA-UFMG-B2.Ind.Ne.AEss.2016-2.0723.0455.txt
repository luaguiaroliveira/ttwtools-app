<What is more important: hard working or talent?>

While our life it is commom think in what we should do to reach our dreams. In this time, we usually face a doubt: what is more determinant, hard working or talent? Hard working can be summed up in wake up early and chase our goals, be good in something through training, studying and dedication. In other hand, when somebody has talent, be good in something is intrinsec and does not depend on hard working. In this essay, I will argue that hard working is more important than talent.
When people realize that do not have talent enough to achieve some point, they can hard work and learn. It is painful, but the hard working process help them grow up as person and professional. This process shows that win it is not easy and adds value to dream, so after hard work and get there, this people probably will be happier than people who reaches the same goal by talent, because is hard get sucess, but they got. Thinking in productivity and quality in a company, for example, the hard working will have more sucess.
Other consequence of the process have to be considered. When people hard work, they face a lot of problems, like lack of time, difficulty to learn and fatigue. If they go on in the process, this troubles can help them grow up and get maturity to face adversities. Besides that, the hours of study and dedication can results in a excellent professional, with know-how above average.
In this sense, more work and dedication results in sucess. And this sucess have more quality than the sucess reached only by talent. So, in conclusion, hard working is more determinant than talent. Thomas Edison said: Genius is 1% inspiration and 99% sweat.
