<The importance of knowing Genetics>

Genetics is the study of genes, genetic variation, and heredity in living organisms. It is generally considered a field of biology, but intersects frequently with many other life sciences and is strongly linked with the study of information systems. We live in a society surrounded by Genetics issues, such as human cloning, transgenic food and Genetics manipulation in living beings. However, Genetics does not have to be seen as a subject from school, but as a knowledge to understand many aspects in our society.
Many well knower human diseases have genetic causes. Genes   have information that give instructions to tell your body how to make all the proteins it needs to survive and grow. Diseases can occur due to a defect in a single gene or in a set of genes. Dysfunctional gene behavior is commonly termed as a mutation. These mutations are responsible for causing illnesses. Moreover, if the gene mutations exist in the egg or sperm cell, children can inherit the defective gene from their parents. Moreover, in the future, doctors and scientists hope to use our genetic information to diagnose, treat, prevent and cure many illnesses.
Understanding Genetics helps people being able to give opinions in political and society topics that appears in media news and involve ethics and political issues. Geneticists believe that the methods and techniques of genetics are applicable throughout the spectrum of biological activity, such as cloning, genetic manipulations in embryos, plants, mammals, products and foods. Some of these living beings has ethical and population importance, as human embryos modifications. In addition, basic tools of modern biology such as analysis of genomic sequences and bioinformatics are most intelligently used in the knowledge of the Genetic principles that underpin the design and application of the software. Thus, a knowledge of genetics is fundamental to  understanding a lot of society issues.
To sum up, Genetics is present in our lives, from food to prevent hereditary diseases. For this reason, the understanding and the knowledge of basic terms and topics in Genetics are useful in society and have to this have a great importance in our society nowadays. For this, we have to be concerned about stay aware and understand key topics in Genetics. The basic knowledge of Genetics should perpetuate from school to day-to-day life.
