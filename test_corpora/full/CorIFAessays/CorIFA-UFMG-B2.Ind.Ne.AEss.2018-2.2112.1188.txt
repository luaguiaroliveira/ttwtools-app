<The benefits of investing in renewable energy sources>

Alternative energies are powerful tools to combat the undesirable effects of fossil fuels. Nowadays, their technologies are still not popular among society, either because of costs, fear or even discredit about their efficiency. However, there are several associated advantages, and this essay will emphasize them from three different views: environmental, economic and technological. 
Firstly, the renewable energies offer several benefits for the nature. In this way, their sources are unlimited from human scale when compared to fossil fuels. This fact provides a conscious exploitation of natural resources. Furthermore, they proportionate life quality with cleaner air, by reducing emission of greenhouses gases. For instance, the adequate recycling of the organic matter provided in the biomass generation, avoids contamination of the environment and odors in the neighborhood. Therefore, alternative energies contributes to the preservation of the earth. 
Secondly, the generation of alternative energies stimulates national economy. In order to achieve efficiency for the system, they allow the creation of new jobs, even in peripheral areas. That is to say, for example, biogas or solar power stations can reach isolated areas, such as the countryside and disadvantaged regions of financial movement. Likewise, this independence encourages the energetic autonomy of the country instead of importing fossil fuels. As a consequence, the benefits from the implementation of these sources of electrical and thermal energies boost new oportunities on the labor Market, and the economic emancipation to the nation.
Thirdly, the researches in the area are emerging and point to a great potential of constant improvement. To give an illustration, there are strategies of treatment of the biomass that guarantees the production of non-harmful waste, even when applied artificial processes.  In the final stages, the residues are used as biofertilizers, and external reagents can be recovered and re-applied in the same methodology. Moreover, the search for cost reduction has increasingly simplified the machinery, and adapted installation dimensions. Accordingly, the technology applied to renewable sources is in constant development, which ensures adequacy and overcoming. 
In conclusion, despite insecurity about alternative energies, there is a wide range of benefits to be explored. Three of these deserve to be highlighted: ecologically positive, economically stimulating and promising. This issue exposes the need for investment and expansion of alternative energies around the world, so that the potential for human and nature service can be harnessed.
