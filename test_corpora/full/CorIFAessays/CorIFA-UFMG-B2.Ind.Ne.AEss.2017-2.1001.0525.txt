<The Introversion Phenomenon>

The beginning of the XX century was caracterized, specially in western society, for a strong changing in terms of personal and social interaction. From this context, interactions became over stimulated. As a result, a large part of world’s population- introversion people- has been seen in a new perspective. In this essay, I will discuss, in a brief way, the introversion phenomenon and how important is to know about it.
Introversion was defined as a personality trait by the Swiss psychiatrist and psychotherapist Carl August Jung at the beginning of the 20th century. Based on the reactions to stimuli, specially in terms of social interactions, Jung divided people in two groups: the group of extroverted people and the group of introverted people.
According to Jung and also to recent studies about this topic, most of the time an introverted person is characterized by being more reflective, spending more time with himself or herself, with his or her thoughs and feelings. For an introvert, it is more comfortable to engage in activities of this nature than activities that envolve constant interaction of many people. In addition, introverted people prefer to develop their tasks in a silent places. They are also good listeners and greats observers and usually communicate better by writing than by speaking. Another interesting fact about people with introverted personality is that they appreciate loneliness. Contrary to many think, loneliness is not a synonymous of sadness for an introverted person. In fact, it is a necessity. Introverted people need more moments of solitude to reset the energy.
It is a consensus among researchers that no one is totally introverted or extroverted. However the features mentioned above are more visible and strong in introverted people, what it does not mean this group is not sociable. According to the researcher Tatiana Mourão, from the Department of Mental Health of The Medical School of the Federal university of Minas Gerais, introverted people are able to perform social activities in the same proportion as extroverted people. In despite of that, this group is often victim of prejudice, because we have lived in a society that over stimulate interaction. Families, schools and labor market , in general, do not see in a positive way people with introverted personality. The super interaction is so valued that nowadays schools and offices are designed for extroverted people. In schools , the majority of teachers and professors belive that a good student is that one that interact a lot, work in groups the most times. A similar situation is found in labor market in which a lot people work together in the same place, where offices are openned, without dividing walls. By extension, families also are induced to think that introversion is a sick and that introverted children or teenagers are not normal.
Faced with so much misinformation about the phenomenon of introvertion, many individuals are harmed. Children, teenagers, students and adults are often labeled of antisocial and ploblematic people and, unhappy, many times, they become problematic people since they feel rejected by a society that insists on dictating pattern of behavoirs. Besides that, a lot of introverted people do not develop in a effective way because schools, families and labor market are not prepared to receive them, which is a strong problem, specially because one third of the world population is composed by introverted people.
Therefore, it is clear that the introversion have to be more studied. Professionals in field of education and heath should be motivated to study and to know this phenomenon throughout their professional formation. Through these changings, introverted people can be treated in a more appropriate way, which will be positive for these group and for all society.
