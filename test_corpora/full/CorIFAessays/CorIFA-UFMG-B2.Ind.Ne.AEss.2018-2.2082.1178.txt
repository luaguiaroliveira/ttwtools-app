<How does music change lives?>

It is common sense that music is part of our lives in various aspects, nowadays people are often seen with electronic devices plugged to some sort of music, either to celebrate, to reflect, to embrace emotion, to have fun, to dance, to cry etc. 

Music is been a regular and active part of the society routine habits. What many people still do not know is that music also has, scientifically proven, therapeutic effects and is been used as a treatment for more than a century. 

As the history tells us, music is an instrument that has been applied, through years, with many objectives, and its therapeutic using is been one of them. It is known that the members of  the first civilizations already believed that music could be used to guarantee homeostasis, for example, and centuries late, during the Second War, nurses used the music to help the rehabilitation of soldiers, many of them with several neurologic issues. From that point on, music was made and recognised, effectively, as main object of a sort of therapy, and  the Music Therapy (MT) was born as a career.

Music Therapy can be considered a science which uses the music, with neuroscientific and psychological embasement, aiming to help and improve one’s well-being.
As a therapeutic method, MT uses the relation existing in between music and humans to create a musical experience with therapeutic purpose. Its main support is the connection established in humans’ brain, which are known as the most complete connections one has noticed. When listening to music, the human brain is completely activated and it gives to the Music Therapist the possibility to diagnose the needs, prescribe and apply a treatment to the person.
 
The emotional relation in between humans and music, summed up to the effects of music in the brain, which are so extensive, turn MT into a expressive and successful therapeutic approach. 

Using Music Therapy is possible to treat, basically, any type of condition or disorder, such as Anxiety, Autism, Afasia, Parkinson disease, Alzheimer, Dyslexia, Depression, Breath conditions etc. That’s how music is been changing so many lives and helping to offer a helphier and, to some people, an even more dignified experience of life.
