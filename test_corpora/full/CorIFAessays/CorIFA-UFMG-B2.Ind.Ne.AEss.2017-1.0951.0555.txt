In the last century the world saw a great increase in life expectancy. This is due to an expansion on researches and development on several areas of science, including medicine, pharmacy, bioengineering and others. This phenomenon led to an growth on the older population which have many impacts on society.
One of the most important impacts to be highlight is that the older population is less economically active and have higher costs with medical care and sometimes with especial treatment due to a disease is. When you have an expansion in this part of the population, the impact on the economy is visible, in Europe for instance, the public costs to maintain the welfare state is increasingly but in other hand, the revenue is stop growing, and this could led to an crisis, like it happened in Portugal, Spain and Greece in the last decade.
Another idea to highlight is that great expectancy of life do not always mean better quality of life. There is estimates that, in 1994, 37 percent of men and 55 percent of woman aged 85 years or older were living with help from other person to perform daily activities. Researches on improve life expectancy not necessarily have impacts on nonlethal chronic diseases like Parkinson and Alzheimer. But there is researches that indentify risk factor for disability in adults, including obesity, sedentary lifestyle, smoking, drinking. The change of some of this factors could reduce the chance of disability on advanced ages.
It is known for a fact that the aging of the population is a global tendency and there is no way to stop this movement, but some actions could be taken to minimize the impacts on society. Reduce the rate of disability in older people is the most important objective, this led to an drecease on costs of living of the older population in general and increase the number of economically active people. In order to active this objective, more researches should be done to reduce the impacts of chronic diseases, with treatment, cure and prevention.
