<A little information about eating animals>

It is common knowledge that industrialization have changed the way we consume. In this essay, I will talk about meat production and how our culture makes us believe in a different reality. 
In USA 99% animal creations are industrial, this means that the animals are living in  unbelievable conditions, chickens are stored in cages that does not fit them, pigs are not allowed outside, they are too genetically modified to develop faster, to be killed faster and increase profit that they stopped being animals and became products (today two companies own three quarters of all the genetics from cut chickens). Our culture, propaganda and language does not help to differ which ones are created properly. Natural does not mean anything, as well as organic and cage free,  this expressions are more open to nuances than you think. 
As well as the decrease of quality of animal life the industrial creations are contributing to global warming 40% more than all the transport sectors. A few data that scares me is about the last fifty years in the United States; the average cost of a new house come up to almost 1500%, of a new car, 1400% wile milk went up 350% and meat did not even duble.Today animal protein cost less than in any other period in history. Part of that can be attributed to the noltagic vision of the fisherman in the lake, and the farmer knowing all of his animals, that image is not true for most animals. As long as the low cost remain being the priority the quality of life will decrease, how long will humanity endure this atrocities?
In conclusion, the meat industry has been causing a lot of damage. It is up to us as society to suggest this discussion and improve the quality of life for these animals.
