<In general, people are living longer now>
It is a fact that the world population is living longer and the cause of that is in the increasing progress of medicine and science. The volume and the quality of developing technology, besides the researches, brings incredible possibilities to improve health and make people live till old age. Things that we could never imagine a few years ago are every day closer to become true. But what this really represents? There are many implications of this phenomenon. Some of them pretty good, but some not that good.
One may think living longer means, for example, more free time to enjoy life after many years of work. It can be true, but most of people who survive to old age faces some problems that change a little the reality. Disability and lack of energy are examples that can affect the olders and compromise the expectations. Because if you cannot make certain movements than you will have difficulty to do many kind of things.
Get old means also that you have more experience than younger people and you will be able to make better choices and know better what to do in certain situations. And that is a fact. But nowadays, the youth do not respect the olders like they use to do in the past, and becomes very common not to listening the advices and not taking seriously the teachings.
It all shows that aging is accompanied by judgments and conditions that can disturb the good part of being old. It is necessary to know that judgments about your beauty or your capability will exist, but you cannot let it put you down. Beyond that, it is a good idea to look for things that you can do with what you have, discovering which good things you can do with your condition.
