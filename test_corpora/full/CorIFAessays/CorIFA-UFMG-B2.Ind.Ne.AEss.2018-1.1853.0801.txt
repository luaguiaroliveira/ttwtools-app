<Why to use multiphase motors?>

Three-phase machines are traditionally used since the grid is three-phase as well. However, with the use of power converters, the machines could have any number of phases [1]. Multiphase machines have several advantages, when compared to three-phase counterparts [1], [2]. The most appreciated are the reduced power per phase and the reliability [1], [2], [3], [4], [5], [6]. Multiphase machines are inherently fault-tolerant since they can operate with some phases open or short-circuited [1]- [6]. These advantages make them more interesting than three-phase machines to especial applications such as electric vehicles, aerospace and high power applications [1], [2]. Researching multiphase machines is intensifying in the last years [1]. The use of electric vehicles is a clear tendency for the future, which will popularize the employment of multiphase motors [1].
Multiphase drives have several advantages when compared to conventional three-phase drives. It can be cited: reduced power per phase (reduced current, voltage or both); reduced rotor harmonic currents; torque pulsation with reduced amplitude and increased frequency; lower DC link current harmonics; higher reliability and higher power density [1], [2], [7]. These advantages, especially the higher reliability, make multiphase machines attractive in applications such as: electric/hybrid vehicles, aerospace, ship propulsion, and high power industrial applications [1]-[7]. There is also interest in multiphase power generation, particularly in grid-connected wind energy conversion systems and stand-alone applications [2]. Nine-phase machines are already employed in ultrahigh-speed elevators [1], [8].
On the other hand, some challenges can be found due to the consequent complexity due to the higher number of phases. For instance, the models present more dimensions, more currents must be measured and controlled, there is a need for a converter with more legs (although there is the possibility for the cost of the converter to be reduced if the voltage is reduced), the pulse width modulation must be revised since undesirable harmonics can appear in the multiple harmonic planes. However, there are many efforts in the literature to understand and to increase the practical feasibility of operating multiphase machines [1].
To summarize, multiphase machines are very adequate to applications which mainly require high power and/or high reliability. The research and the use of multiphase machines are growing in the last years. The tendency is to become more popular mainly with the application in electric vehicles, since vehicles using gasoline are expected to disappear in the future.
