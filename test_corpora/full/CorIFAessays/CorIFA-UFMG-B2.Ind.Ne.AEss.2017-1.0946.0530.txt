<Essay title: Some people believe computer and video games are harmful to children, while others disagree. What do you think? Take a position and defend it.>
Nowadays, computers and video games are the favorite fun for many children. They prefer these “technological toys” instead of traditional games, as play with a ball or dolls. In this context, a question arises: Are these technologies harmful for children? In this essay, I will argue about that and demonstrate why computer and video games are not harmful and might be beneficial to children.
In the past, boys in majority played with balls and little cars while girls with dolls and kitchen toys. These toys are preparing them for the adult life, because in the past strictly men drive and the women basically take care of the kids and home.
Actually, the situation is different and exists many different technological toys that both, boys and girls play. However, it is a good thing if we consider that the functions and jobs changed. In this context, computers and video games play an important role preparing kids for the new jobs and challenges in adult life.
The technologies contribute to prepare the kids in abilities with the tools that they have to use in their professional life. Computers and video games contributes with “high-tech” knowledge and also to learn other languages as English. These “preparation” in childhood is essential too for a successful academic life, where technologies knowledges are even more required.
Considering the exposed above, it is possible to conclude that computer and video games are much more beneficial than harmful to children. They contribute with future professional and academic life improving their high-tech and language knowledge.
