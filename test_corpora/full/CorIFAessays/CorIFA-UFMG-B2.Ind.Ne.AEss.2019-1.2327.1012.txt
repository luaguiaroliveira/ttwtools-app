<Impacts of the privatization of the Parque das aguas of Caxambu>

Water, the most important and valuable fluid on Earth, is it possible to price it? For some people, yes. The prefecture of Sao Lourenco, a municipality located in the south of Minas Gerais, granted the exploitation of mineral waters to Nestle, a private company. Considering the alleged success in the sales of bottled mineral water, Caxambu prefecture, a neighboring town, threatens to do the same, putting at risk the largest hydromineral complex in the world. This can gering diverse social and environmental impacts that must be considered. 

First, it is important to know how wonderful the existence of mineral water sources in Caxambu is. They have medicinal properties capable of curing skin irritations, gastritis, kidney stones, and other diseases. Princess Isabel herself had her anemia cured by taking one of the ferruginous waters that the park possesses. The tourism and commerce of the city are based on the park and its reassuring environment - being a refuge for tourists of big cities – on its medicinal sources and on the balneary, possessing several hydromassage baths with relaxing functions. 

Like Caxambu, Sao Lourenco has the same characteristics, its development linked to the richness of the mineral waters. However, with the privatization of the park, there has been an extreme increase in the exploitation of water, which can be exploited as a mineral good, that is, the company can exploit it until its end. Obviously, the high exploitation caused the drying of one of the sources of mineral water. In addition, the entrance fee in the park increased considerably, limiting the entrance of the inhabitants who had the entrencce previously released. 

Taking all this into account, it is necessary to see how privatization can negatively impact the city of Caxambu. Caxambu is the largest hydromineral site in the world that runs the risk of having its sources dried up in favor of the company's export earnings, as well as the risk of having many of its businesses affected by the privatization of the park. Even citizens will not can longer use the park free, increasing the number of people doing physical activities, such as cycling, running and walking, on streets that do not have lanes for these activities. In addition, leisure paid is something more distant from the reality of the residents of a small town of twenty thousand inhabitants. 

In conclusion, it is extremely important to analyze each impact that the privatization of the Parque das aguas of Caxambu can generate, since it will affect not only the inhabitants of the city and their incomes, but also the abundance and existence of the richest and most diverse waters with medicinal properties world.
