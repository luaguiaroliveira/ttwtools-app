Learning a foreign language while kid is an interesting way of be a person who speaks fluently two or more languages.
Children has more facility to assimilate foreign languages than adults, because of their development related to learn their native language.The process of learning becomes more natural this way.
For teenagers or adults, is more difficult to learn correctly, without akcent, grammatical errors, because they mix their acknowledgment about their native language with the new foreign language.
Children who learn at primary school another language are able to be fluent in both native and foreign languages because they will be bulding at the same time their vocabulary in both idioms.
The parents have to know what is the priority for their kids. Sometimes they are so busy with the activities at school, that they forget that they are just kids and have to play with friends, and enjoy free time. This learning process has to be something natural, according to what both parents and kids want, dividing time between being a kid and learning.
For children maybe a class with games, music, interactive activities, conversation between them and with the teacher is more interesting, because of their way of assimilation with the world. Material things are more understandable to them, so, a toy, some color games, interactive animations, are simple ways of learning another language.
The school and the parents have to incentivate the kids to learn things. They have the responsability, mainly the parents, to know what is best for their kids, and what is the best way for them to gain this acknowledgement. The school may know that the method with children is diferent than the method with adults, and that their potential have to be explored to improve their lexic, their fluence when talking, and all the important things when talking about a foreign language.
At last, if maybe the kids could choose which language they want to speak, according to their interest, it will be more poductive and good to them the process.
