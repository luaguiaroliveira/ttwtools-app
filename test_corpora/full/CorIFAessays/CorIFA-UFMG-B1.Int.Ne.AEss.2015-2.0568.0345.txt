American adults divorced more after 45 years old and it is valid both to men and women. We can observe that the people born until 1954 are responsable for more than 30% of the divorces and usually they had more than 45 years old when they get divorced. 
People born after 1954 tend to divorce less, what means that the youngs are preserving more the marriage, probably because the social values now are different than that when the olders got married. Nowadays people are more interesting in their welfare. Marriage and children are not anymore the principal objetive in the life. And when a person decide to get married, this decision is more studied than was before.
This reflect the change of the society. Women and men work, they have their own money and the dependence in relationship is almost over. There are lot of mechanisms as shared custody that became simpler to separe a couple. 
Probably we are going to see less marriages but less divorces in the next years.
