<Smart grids and the efficient energy management>

The fourth wave of industrial revolution come with the internet connection of most of our day bases tools. This connection also happens in our energy system and brings a new universe of possibilities, the smart grid. The real-time data acquisition and the automation of the systems make possible a reliable forecast of uncertain sources, like photovoltaic and eolic, and the management of loads to avoid losses in the system and peak of energy consumption. This new approach of the electrical system can bring a revolution to the way we produce and consume energy. The proposed paper will present the advantages and disadvantages of the smart grids and analyses how far from this technology we are.
Through the many possibilities achieved with the smart grid and the efficient energy management, the most important is, without doubts, the increase of the reliability in renewable and sustainable energy sources. As the energy generation from photovoltaic and eolic, the most attractive energy sources, is impossible to be forecasted with a 100% sure, these solutions were neglected for a long time. With the smart grids, is possible to increase the ratio of these sources in the energy matrix without affecting the system energetic security. Another great advantage is the possibility to avoid energy losses in batteries stores and sources disconnections. The smart grids can optimize the energy consumption to meet the energy generation through the automation of preschedule activities, and so, consume energy in the exact moment it is generated.
This solution has also many disadvantages. The most prominent is the cost to apply this technology in the current electrical systems. The actual configuration of these systems is different from the proposed one by concept and the costs to a change their topology at once is unattainable. Another disadvantage is related to the information privacy and ethical issues. An interconnected system with supervision of data provided by numbers of consumers and generators could have lacks that allow frauds and data manipulation. This subject is on focus of many academic works and avoid data break and biased systems are the main aim of these researches.
The smart grids allied with energy management is the future of electrical systems. However, many obstacles must be faced. It is necessary a more substantial investment in this technology and support from governments, since it can allow a higher insertion of renewable energy and as consequence, decreases the greenhouse gasses emission.
