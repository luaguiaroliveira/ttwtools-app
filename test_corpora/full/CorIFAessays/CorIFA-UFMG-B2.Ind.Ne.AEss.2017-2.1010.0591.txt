Nanostructured systems have be intensively studied because of the idiosyncratic behavior of these materials in atomic dimensions. In face of this studies highlights the thin films and the strong correlation between their structure and their magnetic properties.Recently, has shown that the magnetic properties of iron films that have been grown on the surface 001 of the palladium brings forward interesting characteristics such as spin reorientation and surface perpendicular magnetic anisotropy. Strontium titanate have and lattice parameter close to the palladium lattice parameter, so it permits a similar epitaxial grown, but with some interesting advantages. The first one is that strontium titanate is cheaper than palladium, besides that, it have a high dielectric constant, which means it is ideal for growing magnetic systems with a perpendicular magnetic anisotropy, in contact with a dielectric material.

Researchers have discovered that thin films of FeCo (Iron/Cobalt) metallic alloy grown on palladium substrate at room temperature has magnetization anisotropy pointing out of the surface. It means that, if you apply a magnetic field pointing in a direction perpendicular to the surface, the material will response with an induced magnetization which remains in the material, even if the magnetic field removed. This is because the FeCo alloy is a ferromagnetic material. The great advantage of being a magnetic thin film with a surface perpendicular magnetization is that enables the manufacturing of data devices with a bigger capacity of storage.

Strontium titanate (STO) is an oxide that has a lattice parameter close to palladium, it implies, theoretically, that is possible to grow FeCo films and observe similar results as that was observed in Pd, but in a cheaper substrate with a higher dielectric constant. This is particularly interesting once it reduces the influence of the substrate magnetization in the thin film, so they magnetization that is being measured belongs to the thin film only. In this work, we have grown thin films of Fe/Co metallic alloy on STO surface and we measured their magnetic properties, via magneto optometry. We compare our results with those was obtained in experiments with Pd.

The thin film was grown epitaxially on the STO 001 surface, trough x-ray photoemission spectroscopy (XPS) we determined the concentration of each element in the film, we obtained the proportion 50-50 for FeCo. The results obtained through the magneto optometry indicates that for a 10 monolayers thickness, the magnetization is pointing in plane, in contrast with the results obtained in the Pd, but the hysteresis curves indicates that we have an anisotropy that depends of the crystalline direction. The next step is trying to repeat those experiments for different film thickness and we expected to observe the magnetization out of surface plane.
