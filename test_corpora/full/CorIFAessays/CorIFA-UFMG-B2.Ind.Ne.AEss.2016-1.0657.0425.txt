Since XVIII century, when Edward Jenner created the first vaccine against smallpox, has been a lot of discussion about vaccination effectiveness and its necessity. Although it is clearly known its efficacy, this controversial view emerged from many cases which vaccine had a lot of severe side effects. However, these cases can not be compared with the numerous advantages of using vaccines.

The effectiveness of vaccine has been shown since its first prototype, the vaccine against smallpox. Since then, vaccines has prevented the population to face so many other infectious diseases. Many studies have demonstrated that immunization reduced significantly the incidence of diseases such as diphtheria, measles, mumps, whooping cough, polio, rubella (acquired and congenital) and tetanus. Nowadays, smallpox is largely eradicated such that vaccination against it is unnecessary.

Along with its efficacy, the feasibility of use vaccines is based mainly on its cheaper cost compared with treatment of those diseases. Treating diseases includes spend high amount of money with medication therapy and medical procedures which many times causes a lot of severe side effects and can compromise the patient's quality of life. Even though there are some expensive vaccines (HPV vaccine for example) the cost is cheaper than the treatment (chemotherapy / surgical removal).

Also, vaccination is currently the most effective way to control an infectious disease in a population. As has already been cited, many diseases have been controlled through immunization. It is the best way to promote large prophylactic actions to the population because of the accessibility (low cost) and easy implementation.

Once we have seen all those positive reasons toward the use of vaccines, it is easy to understand why the government and bio industries put a lot of investments in the development and production of vaccines. Protection, prevention, accessibility and easy implementation make vaccination an attractive alternative to increase the population's life expectancy as well as its quality of life.
