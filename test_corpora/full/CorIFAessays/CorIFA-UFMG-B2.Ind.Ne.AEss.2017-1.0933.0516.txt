<The Portuguese Cartography and the Stages of Science Modern in early century eighteen>
The improvement achieved on the determination of more accurate longitudes that occurred in France, essentially at the third decade of the eighteen-century, propelled the mapmaking world labor to novel stage. Fearing the consequences of these accomplishments in their territories and possessions, several European monarchs began a true scientific running. This essay endeavoured to analyse closely the effects of the improvement French Science over one of these reigns, the Portuguese Kingdom.
In 1720, the French scientific advance was capable to fix with more precision the astronomical positions of meridians and with this to reduce the gross inaccuracies in determining of longitudes, in vigour until then. This improvement was responsible to compel several of these sovereigns remapping his territories according the French accomplishments.
Notwithstanding this menace, the whole Portuguese background added its experience in its overseas empire demonstrate that the sovereigns of River Tejo were as closer to the French Scientifics development than it has been debating. In the ends of 1690s, the French Jesuit astronomer, father Jacob Cocleo, who arrived in Ceará in 1660, was hired by the Portuguese crown to make a map the whole colony. Several studies have demonstrated that Father Cocleo has achieved a relative accuracy before French’s method, and more, his labor also contributed directly above the cartography in France.
In sum, an effective analyze of the Scientific knowledge development during the Modern Ages requires an observation more expanded background. This way, these studies will be able to distant from the common place that comprehends and construes the emergency of the Modern Sciences only over some nations, personalities or a specific period of time.
