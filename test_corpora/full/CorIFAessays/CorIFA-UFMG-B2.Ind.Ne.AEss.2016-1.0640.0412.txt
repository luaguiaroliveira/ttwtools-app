<The effects o strength training on health>
According to the World health organization, 3.2 million of deaths each year are caused by the sedentary lifestyle. That is because people who are insufficiently active have a 20% to 30% increased risk of death compared to people who are sufficiently active (WHO, 2015). One of the most recommended activities for health promote is the strength training (ST), as known as resistance train. The American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) defines resistance train (RT) as: A form of physical activity that is designed to improve muscular fitness by exercising a muscle or a muscle group against external resistance (ACSM, 2013). There are many positive effects of practice strength training which includes an improvement of the physical functions, a treatment of many diseases and also an enhance of mental health (Westcott, 2012).
When someone talks about physical activities benefits, the first obvious thought is about the physical capacities improvement. But each type of exercise can bring a specific benefit to the subject who practices it. In the current ACSM report, Westcott (2012) claims that the resistance training reverse muscle loss caused by aging, facilitate physical function, recharge resting metabolism and consequently provide a reducing of the body fat.
The development of all this points bring with it many benefits for the health, specially for people who suffer of diseases as: obesity, diabetes, dyslipidemia, cardiovascular diseases(Strasser & Schobersberger, 2011). Westcott (2012) points out that the ST is an effective intervention to counteract age-associated declines in insulin sensitivity and to prevent the onset of type 2 diabetes; it is related to the improvement of glucose and insulin homeostasis because of increases in muscle cross-sectional area and lean body mass and may be preferable to aerobic exercise for improving insulin sensitivity. ST increase HDL cholesterol by 8% to 21%, decrease low-density lipoprotein (LDL) cholesterol by 13% to 23%, and reduce triglycerides by 11% to 18% According to the ACSM (2009), it also increases bone mineral density (BMD) more efficiently than other exercises such as aerobic and bearing training (Westcott, 2011).
Although, the physical benefits is not the only area the ST improves. Westcott (2011) also demonstrate that this kind of physical activity provides the development of cognitive abilities and the treatment of psychological illness. O’Connor et al. cited by Westcott (2011), argue that ST causes the reduction of symptoms in people with fatigue, anxiety and depression. He also quotes a study of Singh et. al. that researched the effects of RT on depression in elderly individual. This study showed the incredible rate of 80% of the subjects were no longer clinical depressed after only 10 weeks o RT performed three times a week.
After all, we can conclude the ST is an excellent choice to provide health. Improving not only physical capacities but also counteracting diseases and developing mental health and abilities.
