<13 reasons why and the problems behind suicide.>

According to WHO, 800 people kill themselves annually.The Statistics are equivalent to one person every 40 seconds around the world. Although discussions on the subject have been improved in recent years, especially through sources such as The Netflix Series "13 reasons Why", The topic about suicide needs to be more addressed to give people more orientation and, consequently, save lives.
Lack of information contributes to suicide.The majority of cases could have been avoided if people had orientation about the subject and how proceed.When someone is aiming to suicide,normally people from family and circle of friends can see initially that something is wrong,but they don't know what to do next.It is necessary to give teenagers,adults and seniors information to identify the sintoms and which resources look for when someone close,and even themselves, are planning to suicide.
Because of the fact that suicide still is taboo in society people avoid talk about it like it was something contagious,but conversation is a perfect way to reduce the problem.That's whay The Netflix Series objects: when talked,it gets easier.That happens because victims feel less ashamed and put out their feelings and weaknesses.Doing this helps them to understand what are they feeling,opening to hear advices and thinking more clearer about getting help to solve their problems.It also give time to the interlocutor to refer to a specialist and another sources like CVV and psicologists.
In conclusion,debates and orientation are beyond important to prevent suicide around the world.Society needs to stop seeing the subject as something that does not occur or something that is better not talk about,because this kind of thoughts don't help and also contributes to victims feel alone and excluded.It also motivates to more lack of information about the topic and consequently more deaths that could have been avoided.
