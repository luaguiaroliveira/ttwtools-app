<Legalization is a right>

The abortion must be legalized in Brazil. Every day women die trying to abort in this country. Abortion has no face or social class, but the deaths arising from it have. They are black, poor, marginalized.

Abortion occurs. Regardless of criminalization or legalization, it occurs every day. Women of the most favored social class will pay dearly for clinics in confidence here or abroad. Women who does not have financial means are those that are at the mercy of clandestine clinics that generally do not have adequate sanitation. They are the ones who die because of abortion. The determining factor to legalize is the women's lives since it would save them if they could appeal to a public health agency and perform the termination of pregnancy with safety.

In Brazil, the State is secular, nevertheless, we can often see parliamentarians defending law projects sustained with religious foundations. Talk about family and god, as a representative, in a public space that is not guided by these precepts is take their personal convictions to defend the interests of an entire society. Using the argument that life exists from the beginning of pregnancy to defend the criminalization of abortion is to guide public decisions on personal beliefs.

Legalizing abortion is different than being in favor of it. Believe that life begins at some point in pregnancy, which is what their religion preaches, does not prevent you from being in favor of decriminalization. No one will be forced to abort because it's legal, just as no one should be prevented of aborting, because of religious and moral principles that they do not believe. Legalizing abortion is to strengthen democracy and the right of women to decide their lives. It means reducing the number of deaths to women who can not afford to make the process with the minimum health and safety care.

In conclusion, abortion is a right. The State shall ensure the ability of women to interrupt pregnancy without incurring the risk of death. Motherhood will only be complete if it is free and desired. Bringing a child into the world and not giving them decent conditions for survival, it is not to be in favor of life.
