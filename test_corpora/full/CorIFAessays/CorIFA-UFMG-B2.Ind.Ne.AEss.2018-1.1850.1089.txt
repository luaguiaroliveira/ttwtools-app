<The relevance of therapy animals>

There is an undeniable bond between animals and the human race. In contrast with the general tough,  its benefits goes beyond a relationship of affection and care, achieving a important protagonist as mental and physical supporters. A growing body of scientist’s researches reveals the importance and benefits of animal therapy, which is considerably increasing in the last years. Even being affirmed as an effective method, it remains an area of wide possibilities to be explored and invested. Unfortunately, the lack of regulation of the practice limits its application in some settings, such as clinics and hospitals.

Therapy animals can have a presence of impact in hospitals, nursing homes, schools, jails and mental institutions, being important in the treatment of disorders and disabilities, being a great investment for the educational and health field. Based on the impact of this method on the quality of life of all those who are neglected by society, it does not represent a substitution of all the traditional treatments and procedures. In other hand,  it is a complement, a new line of research in attention to diversity (ABELLÁN, 2009).  Another highlight is the relevance of building a relationship based on affection, communication and confidence which is crucial for social life. 

The relevance of constructing a team based on multidisciplinary areas to conserve the well-being of the humans and animals can not be forgotten. Veterinarians, psychologists, physicians, nurses, social workers and occupational therapists should be instructed to work together and amplify the results of treatment, minimizing the concerns about the socialization between the patient and the animals (SAN JOAQUÍN, 2002).  In opposition, the theory and researches are not walking along with the practice, resulting in a lack of regulation, investment and actual implement of this technique, which is still restricted.   

To conclude, investing in an alternative method of treatment that noticeably shows its benefits represents a great investment when it comes to expand the traditional procedures. The humanitarian value in this kind of treatment should be valorized as an act of vulnerability and love, proving that a positive relationship bond reflects directly in the physical and mental well-being. Giving support to this increasing field is making a stand for the importance of emotions and affection, not looking for medicines and seclusion as a synonym for disabilities and diseases."
