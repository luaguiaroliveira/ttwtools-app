<Civil construction industry and the environment>

The global warming is a real problem to environment and to the human life. The greenhouse gases, mainly CO2 (carbon dioxide), are one of the causes to the climate changes. The civil construction industry and the use of Portland cement release a great amount of CO2 in the atmosphere. Although this is an important industry to world’s development, it is crucial to reduce the greenhouses gases emission researching and using new construction materials. In this essay, I will argue about the environmental problems of using cement Portland in civil construction, and the new construction materials benefits.
The production of cement Portland, the main material used to make concrete, the major product used in civil construction nowadays, has many malfunctions. Its production is related to 5% of the CO2 world’s emission, and the extraction of raw materials used in its manufacture, like limestone and clay, is aggressive to the environment. Besides that the transport releases more greenhouses gases and the production requires high-energy consumption.
The use of new construction materials can bring many benefits to the civil construction industry. New technologies in this area can be environmental friendly releasing lower amount of CO2. It also can improves the material properties, the construction durability and quality. For example the geopolymer concrete is a new construction material, it can reduce the Co2 emission about six times comparing to Portland cement, the mechanical properties of these material are higher than conventional concrete, furthermore others geopolymer’s technical features can increases the building durability and quality.
In conclusion, the civil construction industry has a big responsibility in the climate change, mainly because it is responsible for a great amount of the greenhouse gases emission in the Earth. The new materials construction can bring an alternative to reduce the environmental impacts. Thus, it is important to make researches and work on developing new construction technologies to make the civil construction more ecological.
