It's common known that the climate in our planet is changing. The process called Global warming is based on the raise of the world's temperature, which is intensified by human actions. One of the consequences of this is the melt of the ice in North Pole what causes the raise of the ocean level. Recent researches have shown some possible easy solutions for global warming climate changes. Here I am going to defend that those easy and practical solutions should not be applied as permanent.
One of the solutions that have been funded is based on throwing chalk in the atmosphere¹. The chalk would create a layer that would reflect part of the sunlight back to the space. This would lower the temperature on Earth surface. The quantity of chalk necessary to apply this idea is not that big, and the chalk thrown into the atmosphere would fall dissolved in the rain, so there would not have any really bad consequences for the environment. 
If there would not be any problem with this idea, why do not we apply it? The problem is that this solution would be temporary and would not be able to motivate people to take care of our planet and stop destroying it. After all the chalk fall with the rain, the temperature would raise again, and we would be obligated to do this process many times. Doing that, people would still do not care about stop polluting the environment, burning forests and producing trash. This solution would mask the problem instead of solving it. 
As humans, the smartest living beings existent on Earth, we have the capability of treat our planet as it should be treated and an easy “way out” of our problem would not help us to create that sense of environmental care in the society. This is our home, we have to keep it clean and healthy.
