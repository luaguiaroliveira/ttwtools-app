Knowing a foreign language in the actual world is necessary for a better self development in every area of life, but many people ask about which is the most effective way to learn a language. Studies can show that the older you get, the harder it is to learn a new language, and their suggestion is to begin learning a foreign language as soon as possible.
To understand a language, you need to have in mind many things, such as culture and lifestyle, because for example, when you are talking with a person from Korea and another one from United States, their culture and lifestyle are different, and if you are talking to them in their native language you will know exactly what they are talking about and why they are choosing these words. 
It is always polemic when the subject is about children learning a foreign language as soon as they start school, but many studies show how this is effective, because at the same time children internalize their mother tongue, they are able and capable of internalizing the foreign language. It requires time and practice to internalize a foreign language, because it is not only about speaking, but also about thinking, writing, reading and most of all, understanding. And to get to know all of this, the earlier you began to learn a language, the easier it is to understand it. 
The world is requiring people to know a foreign language, at each time of our lives we need it for something, it may not be so clear, but at some moment everybody will need it. For children, the earlier they start to learn a foreign language, the easier will be life for them, because as they internalize it very fast, when they need it at any time, they will know how to deal with the world and will know the best way to be in contact with what they need.
