<The harmful side of playing video games (to children?)>

In 1966, an engineer called Ralph Baer created the first prototype of a video game. His scientific project enabled the electronic games industry to grow and turn into that massive market that we have today. Since its commercial acceptance, the video games have been drastically changing the traditional way of playing in childhood, and those changes have made arise an intensive discussion regarding the harmful effects of video games on children. In this essay, it will be argued what are the negative impacts of playing video games and how they may affect children development.

Play is essential to the development of a child. It enhances cognitive, social, and physical abilities, as well as contributes to the emotional well-being of children. One of the most common play among kids is computer and video games. That sort of games provides an alternative to explore an entire fictional world sitting on a sofa. You can thrive bloody battles and get immersed in medieval era by just moving your hand fingers. Such technology is impressive, and it is not a surprise that it leads children to spend hours in front a computer or TV screen. However, spending an excessive time playing video games has a negative impact on their social development, since it reduces the time kids spend engaging in outside activities and interacting with other kids. In other words, it reduces the time kids spend getting in touch with the real world, and real people. As a consequence, the ability to evolve and use appropriate social skills in different real-life situations may be compromised, leading them to become adults with undermined social behavior.

Prolonged gaming playing also has some health risks. It may create ergonomic problems from poor body positioning, or from the position of the computer screen. Furthermore, compared to other activities, such as practicing sports or climbing a tree, playing video games does not involve intense body movements which may lead to sedentarism. Another issue is related to the hand’s movement repetitions that may cause joint damage and wrist pain. (it is missing a conclusion)

The violent content of video games has been also a subject of concern. Numerous researches have been performed in order to explore the connection between violent media and acts of violence committed by children. Some researchers claim that violent games can make kids act more aggressively in their relationships since they do not have maturity enough to separate the virtual world from the real.

The excessive playing of video games may present social and health risks to children, jeopardizing their development and even their future as adults. Therefore, it is highly recommendable that parents guide their kids to select games with an appropriate content, as well as to determine their daily exposure to video games.
