The rate of divorces registered an increasing between 1940 and 1950 and was higher as older people were. In both gender, male and female tax increase during 1940 and 1950 decades. We can try to relate it with World War II, but here we don't have enough information to do so. For female cases we can find explanations in feminism movements. After 1954 the divorces decreased in every age and both gender, female and male. At that time it wa more common by age 30, in other ages the tax was very low between 1965 and 1974. In conclusion, we can see that male and female rates are very similar, probably because a divorce, in most of the cases, on that time, involved a man and a woman.
