<Should animals be used for medical research?>

For several years, the use of animals to perform experiments has brought great advantages in medical research. There are many studies that show anatomical and physiological similarities among human and others species of animals’ body. Those have demonstrated that these animals could be suitable for understanding and finding treatments or cure for many diseases that affect human and others animals. However, using animals in medical research raises up some controversial questions which many people argue why animals need to be sacrificed for studies that benefit most humans. In this essay, it is proposed to expose the benefits of using animal experimentation in medical research.

During a research project, the researchers must test their results in each life’s period of an organism. It's much easier to do these tests in animal that completes each period in a short period of time than humans. For example, it's possible to observe the effects of vaccine in each life’s period in a shorter time in mice than humans, and monitor how long it can maintain the immunization. Moreover, it is also possible to monitor the effects for various generations in a short period of time. Another point here is that during a study is necessary to maintain the same environmental conditions for all participants, it can avoid interfering factors in the results. In the beginning of a study, all animal to be used are selected carefully where the anatomical and physiological structures are found to similar between the model experimentation and humans.

Many diseases that affects humans also affects animals. A disease’s cure, for example, would benefit humans and animals. Furthermore, there are many diseases that are transmitted by animals. If the animal is cured, the disease could be controlled. For example, there are researches try to find a vaccine for leishmaniose, a tropical parasitic disease that affects more dogs than humans, it is transmitted for humans by the bite of the mosquito that first bites the infected dog.

Most people don't know that the animal model allows a faster study and also benefit many animals. It's known the many positive points of medical research with animals and animal experimentation seems to be irreplaceable in medical research. However, it is also important to points out the need of making efforts to decrease the number of animals used in a research project and try to find alternatives ways that can replace the animals without compromising the results.
