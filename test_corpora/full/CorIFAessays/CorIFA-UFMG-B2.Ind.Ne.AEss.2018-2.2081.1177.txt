<Alternative medicine: uses and challenges>

In recent years,alternative medicine techniques have gained many adepts.Some well-known techniques such as acupuncture,floral use and homeopathy have several positive results, they act in fact against certain diseases or they simply provide well-being to users.However,caution is needed when discussing about alternative medicine,since these techniques indeed have positive results,but  there is insufficient  scientific evidence for them to act as a substitute for conventional medical treatments.
Alternative medicine has gained popularity in recent years because of its positive results. Homeopathy, for example, is a successful case of placebo manipulation and is often used in the treatment of chronic diseases such as allergies, rhinitis and migraine. On the other hand, the flowers also work very well as placebo and represent a source of well being. Acupuncture, in the long run, is effective against side effects of medicines, nausea, pain and pressure problems. As a consequence, many individuals are interested in the techniques of alternative medicine and become adepts even if they have no scientific proof.
However, alternative medicine can not replace conventional medical treatments.The first and main cause is the absence of scientific evidences that allows this replacement.The second reason is the fact that science is still studing these techniques for understand how they act in the human body, although already recognises their positive results.In this way, science is not abble to provide proofs that certify the security of the use of alternative medicine on the place of traditional medicine.
Therefore, alternative medicine and their techniques are gaining many supporters because of the positive results provided by their practice, but they can not replace conventional treatments for lack of scientific evidence for this. However, the advancement of the study of these techniques may change this panorame in the future.
