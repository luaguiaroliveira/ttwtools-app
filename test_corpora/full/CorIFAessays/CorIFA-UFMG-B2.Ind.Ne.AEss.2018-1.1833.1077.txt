<How the crime scene is settled>

Since the explosion of TV shows that have the plot developed from the criminal expertise, forensic science has been under the spotlights, being explored by several criminal situations that instigate the public interest. One of the points that generates most interest and curiosity is the amount of areas of knowledge that can be approached, from chemistry and biology, going through law and going to psychology. As the forensic science deals with the resolution of crimes is the main way to provide evidence for law enforcement and ensure for the population that criminal offenses are resolved.

Even though the forensic evidences are intended for the judge's trial, they are also directed to the victims and their families as well as the society, which has its rights assured through the application of the laws. A very famous case in Brazil, the one of Isabella Nardoni, the expert concluded that the girl was thrown from the sixth floor of the building where her father and her stepmother lived, but they declared that someone had entered in the apartment and threw the girl out the window. But the expertise denied this version with the traces of blood on the door of the apartment, in the living room, the hallway, the door handle, the car, and there was no sign of burglary or theft. The expertise also revealed that the girl was gently thrown by her wrists.

Another case with big repercussion was the Richthofen’s case, which the couple Manfred and Marísia von Richthofen were killed with blows of iron bar. The police were called by the boyfriend of the couple's daughter, Suzane von Richthofen, and he told that the house appeared to have been robbed. At first, the situation suggested robbery and then murder but no signs of break-in were found, and the house alarm did not ring which raised doubts. While the experts were examining the library, Suzane was questioned by them and she told a version incompatible with the scene that was found. Later when Suzane and her boyfriend were faced with the analysis of the expertise, they confessed the crime.

The place where most of the vestiges that indicates the dynamics of a crime and mainly its authorship is the crime scene. Hardly a crime occurs without leaving any material vestiges and this is how begins the analysis of the place. The recognition of the scene is fundamental but before this, the place should be isolated and then start collecting and identifying the items that were found, which are then analyzed and then placed with the dynamics of the crime to understand how the crime occurred. After these analyzes begins their interpretation, but often because of the lack of preservation of the crime scene, can make the police analysis more difficult.

The use of instrumental analysis techniques applied in identifying the presence of substances has contributed in a valuable way for the promotion of justice. By using modern equipment, experts in this area can analyze and detect minute quantities of materials even when they are present in a blend. The technical-scientific work developed by the expert has a large importance to those who are investigating and judging the crimes committed, since the facts can be proven by science in a rational and systematic way, allowing a more accurate analysis of reality.
