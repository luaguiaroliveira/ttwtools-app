Since internet has emerged, world became a place where people can easily communicate to each other. This means that people can communicate with people that speaks in another language. Also, someone that speaks a second language is, many times, a requirement for a job. Indeed, English became so important that in western world almost all schools teaches English as a separate subject. Therefore, many children has contact with English very early in their lives.
Many studies support that as soon as you learn a language, better you will be in that language. I also believe that children should study English as soon as possible and I think that there are many positive reasons for a child learn English in early ages. First reason is that child’s brain absorb better this kind of knowledge. Second reason is that the effort in learning English spent by a person when she or he is a child is bigger than the effort spent when the person is an adult. Third reason is that the person who learns English earlier is prepared to communicate with many people around world. Finally, the fourth reason is that a person knowing English can have access to a lot of knowledge that is reported in English. Some people can argue that learning English in earlier ages can overwhelm a child. However, learning English in earlier ages is not hard subject. Also child would learn only basic lessons, and step by step she or he would advance in English.
Other languages are also learned by children in schools. Nowadays, Spanish is a relevant foreign language taught in Brazilian schools, because countries speakers of spanish surround Brazil and there is some commercial and friendship interaction between south americans. As soon as a child learn Spanish, she or he can interact with many other child that came to Brazil on vacation. This kind of interaction is beneficial to children, because knowing other cultures turn a person into a open minded person.
Therefore, learn foreign languages as soon as possible, can improve professional option for child and it turns possible for children contact different people around the world. Also, child can discover knowledge available in other languages. Price that comes with learning a language in an earlier age is small compared to the benefits.
