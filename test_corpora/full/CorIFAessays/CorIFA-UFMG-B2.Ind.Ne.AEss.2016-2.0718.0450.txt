Which is more important: talent or hard work?
There is a lot of discussion about how to achieve success, because everybody wants that that. There is not a simple answer, it is necessary to consider many points, two of them are talent and hard work. Both of them are essential to obtain great results, but when talent is more evident, the objectives are achieved easier, faster and more perfect, so talent is more important than hard work.
It is broadly agreed that if one person do something very well, he invested a lot of time
practicing and improving the activity. Although it is a true, it is not the main reason for this success, due to the training is just a way to improve the gift. For example, to became a great runner, a person need to be negro, because is already proved that the way how the muscles are made, provides more resistance and force. The talent is not only a genetic gift in physic area, it is also in psychological area.
Everybody has a way of life and this way is guide by the natural characteristics and the society. So, the talents given by God is a way to give a direction to the life. Then is clear that everybody has a kind of mission in Earth, doing what “born to do”, however, is extreme important to improve this talent. So the gift guides the way of the life and consequently the society around and the “place” in life.
It is a common knowledge that all people influence and be influenced all the time, and to influence other people in some area is necessary to became a leadership in this area. The talent creates a opportunity to be leader in a subject because, for that, be good at the activity is essential. In this way, became a great leader will inspire other people to that without saying a word. For example, a person who is a greater drum player, he will encourage people to do the same thing, a type of follows.
To sum up, it is obvious that the talent is more important than hard work, although these two things are complementary. The essential became from the talent and achieve the success.
