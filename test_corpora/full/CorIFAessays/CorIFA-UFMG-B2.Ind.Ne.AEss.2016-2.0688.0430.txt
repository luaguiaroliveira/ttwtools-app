The internet has emerged in a necessity to connect people and information around the word. Even the geographical distance being considerable, that distance does not affect the connection among people. However, the large time spent on it, unlimited use of it, and the no care about what is said have separated people to talk face-to-face. People think are connected with which other, but in a real scenario, they tend to be less sociable avoiding personal talk.

The first point is that we have spent much time on the internet, with the justification about there are a lot of tasks to do. But, have you look to the past and think that the last generation also had to do tasks without the use of the internet? So, we can reduce these tasks to save time to live better, for sure.

Second, a vast tools and knowledge to get our attention are used for addiction us. For this reason, it becomes difficult to use just a limited set of tools. In other words, we cannot limit our use of the internet, because we always want more.
Finally, when we are not talking face-to-face, we tend to avoid the perception that there is a person as we on the other way. Considering this we feel safe to talk whatever we want without much care about the feelings of the other way.

It is clear that the internet also give a wide opportunity to communicate and exchange information, cultures and knowledge. But, we do not know, how this new world works -- called virtual world for some people -- and we are exploring without much care.

This virtual world, opened the possibility of people be wherever they want to be. For this reason, without a look carefully they are losing who they are and they are ignoring how it is important the face-to-face relationship among them.
