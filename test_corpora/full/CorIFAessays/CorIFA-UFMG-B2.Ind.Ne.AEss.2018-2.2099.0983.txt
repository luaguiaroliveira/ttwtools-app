<Confidence is the key>

Confidence is a characteristic very important when we are learning a different language. Many people do not practice their second language because are afraid, or because they are not confident enough. This kind of action can be harmful for their learning since to learn we need to produce output. Some exercise can help people gain more confidence such as mimic someone, be prepared, understand the language as a tool are some of these practices that can help, the latter being one of the most important. After those practices and being more confident probably you will be able to lead with any situation. This essay aims to show some way to gain and the importance of to be confident while we are speaking one language.
According to Gurler in his article, he brings some idea of the importance of the speaking in our life, <direct citation deleted>. This reflection can help us to understand that not have a confidence to speak will harm our communication that is something really important in our daily life. Low confidence can bring some bad results such as have blank during our speech/ performance, stutter or stumble over words making our speech sound a little confused for the other person, and all this can compromise or make our communication more difficult. 
One way to gain confidence it is through mimics. Through mimic you will learn the different intonations of the language make it sound more natural, will get the fluency on speaking and the good pace for you, and also can help you with your body language. Another way to gain confidence it is through understand language as a tool. According to Marianna Pascal in her TED Talk she presents that when we understand language as a tool that we use to get a result, we changed the focus on our grammar mistakes that we could do, and start to focus on the person and on the objective of the conversation that is to make the other person understand you no matter our speaking level. 
By this way we can conclude that confidence is the key to learn and practice another language. Through this will be able to lead with any situation, being clearly and understood by the people, something more important than to speak fast or without grammar mistakes. It is also important to highlight that those tips are easy to practice and will lead you to be more confidence.
