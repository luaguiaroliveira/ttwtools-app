<Predicted influences brought by the Precedents System instituted by the New Civil Process Code in Brazilian Civil-Law System>

Precedents are decisions made by tribunals (or Courts) in legal cases that are capable to be reproduced by other judges due to their authority. Precedents play a key role in Common-Law Legal Systems (as is in the United States), in which judges can make rules and principles beyond what is legislated by the Congress. The precedents systems’ pass into brazilian Civil-Law Legal System has brought important impacts on how legal cases flow in our legal system. The advent of “IRDRs” and the development of decision-making coherence are among its most important predicted impacts. 

A general opinion on justice in Brazil does not seem to neglect two negative critics: the delay and the uncertainty of judgments. Although other factors contribute more or less to this scenario, the procedural system was one that, however, thanks to the efforts of lawyers throughout the country, underwent deep changes in 2015 and instituted important items.

There is a large number of demands in our justice system that are reiterative. Once it is imposed by the Brazilian Constitution that judges have to ground their decisions, each case had to be meticulously analyzed, even if they were identical, by cause that there were no specifical rules established by precedents for the same demands. The “IRDRs - Incidentes de Demandas Repetitivas” are items brought by the new Civil Process Code, promulgated in 2015 decreeing the suspension of the process in which the risk of offense to isonomy and legal certainty is observed - so that they are able be judged all together in a superior tribunal or a Court. As an amount of public and human resources is allocated to solve each demand, and also, because repetitions increases the duration of process, this item seems to be desirable. Despite this, part of the doctrine believes it prejudices the principle of ample defense, since each case is a case. 

Other impact, which is also a scope of the precedents system, is building coherence in decision-making. Since precedents uniforms the response for identical cases, it helps people to preview what Justice will decide in their cases and also promotes the widely requested legal security.  Otherwise, the perpetrator or defendant of an action will never know what to expect from justice - this is commonly the case.

It is important to point out that this model has provoked enthusiasm in the juridic community, as it promises to solve some obstacles that hinder the speed of proceedings and the very legitimacy of the justice system. Nevertheless, scholars have been pointing out that changes in the New Code, made due to judges lobbies, have deviant the recent precedents system. However, both the New Code and the recent changes are laws whose long-term effects have not yet been considerably faced.
