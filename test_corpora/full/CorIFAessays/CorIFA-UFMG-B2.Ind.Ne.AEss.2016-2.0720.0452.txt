<Does technology make people more alone?>
The recent development of computing, smartphones and social services has raised some questions about how these factors interfere in the human relationships and social life. Although there are people who believe that technology makes us more alone, the facts show that the situation is, actually, the opposite. The devices and services offered are able to make the distance that severs people smaller, and helps them meeting other individuals. In this case, it is fair to say that technology nowadays has positive effects in the human relationships.
The advance of technology does not make people more alone, since the connections made between people, such as friendship, are much easier to be done with the devices and services that were developed with the progress of the computing and communication. In addition, the technology allows people that had already met to maintain contact, no matter the distance. Facebook, WhatsApp and Skype are examples of tools that can be used to that purpose. Some of them can even provide a very interesting way of communication, the video conference call, which enables people to see each other while talking.
Another good example is the app named Tinder that enables the user to meet random people within a certain distance. These and other devices help people to meet, talk, share information and images, and even see each other in real time. Therefore, it is fair to say that technology has an important paper in making people closer, friendly, and to strengthen the bonds between them.
The message services have become very popular in the last years. It is used in order to have quick talks to other people, so it is a good method of maintaining contact with those that are not near us. It allows us to know about the life of the ones we care, and also receive news about them very fast. In addition, these services generally send photos and videos too, what makes the experience of sharing the moments with friends and family even more realistic and fun.
In summary, the technology gives its users many opportunities that are related to their relations and social life, allowing them to easily communicate, share information, talk, meet new people and even see each other in videos or video conference calls. These factors enables people to connect, overcoming the barrier of the distance, and it is undeniable that the technology does not make people more alone, once it plays an important role in getting them closer, spreading the love and friendship, and making relationships more fun and interesting.
