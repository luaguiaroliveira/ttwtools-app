<The benefits of studiing several areas>

Due to the way that educational system is constructed in Brazil, in general, brazilians young people choose wich carrer to follow when they are around 16 - 18 years old. It can be affirmed that at this age a person has only one slightly idea about the life that he / she wants to follow.
Considering this idea it can be supposed that during the graduation, or even after it, there can appear other options that would works for all kinds of people: the ones that weren't satisfied about the first choice, and even the ones that were fulfilled on working with the first choice. This is because making a second graduation course in a completely different area of knowledge can bring several benefits, as to expand ideas about work possibilities and the development of different abilities that can be applied at professional and personal life.
Regarding the social implications that can derivate from people studying about several subjects, we can infer that more the people varie the areas of studies, passing by exacts / biologics and also humans areas of knowledge, more the society has better citizens, more prepareds to make decisions that affects the common life, for example, decisions about public politics. At this point we can think about important historical personalities as Aristoteles, that studied several different areas as philosophy and mathematics.
For sure, there are limitations for people to study more than one graduation course, that derivates from the high common workload of Brazil: the major part of brazilians citizens work around 40 hours per week. Thinking of it, it is possible to suppose that a governamental decision about raising up the level of society's study could facilitate enormously the access of the citizens in achieving the second graduation course. This could consists of some kind of lower load of taxes for the companies that stimulates its employees in continuing studies, for example.
In conclusion, studiing is one of the more important ways of evolving as society at the direction of a better life status for everyone, and if the government decides to encourage this kind of thinking, it can be really benefitial at the longterm.
