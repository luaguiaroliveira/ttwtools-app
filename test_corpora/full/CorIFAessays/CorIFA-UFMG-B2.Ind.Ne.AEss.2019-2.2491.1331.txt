In modern society, those who have success in School or University context are considered extremely intelligent. However, despite the fact that these people are certainly very smart, the called unsuccessful ones are also capable to obtain knowledge. The problem is that the education system that is in effect in nowadays is unfair and people who are not adapt are label as powerless. As long as the maladjusted ones keep being label like that, they start believe in this vision of themselves and do not change. In this essay, I will defend that how others define people in the education - mostly in subtle ways - and consequently how these people define their own habillity in studying affects the learning process. 
Primarily, it is important emphasize that care about each other's opinions is a basic characteristic of the human behavior. People start to concern about what society thinks about them since they are very young and change their natural behavior due to that - even without realizing that they are changing. The mechanism that results in social readjustments is very simple: if one behavior makes someone get uncomfortable, this person stop the initial action, so the discomfort goes away. Humans do that all the time because the are social beings and that is why people structure their perspective about themselves based on their relationship with the individuals in their environment.
This relationship is extremely observable in the education context, where teachers and classmates crate expectations on the students based in things that do not necessarily reveals the cognitive abilities of people - one bad result on a test for example. At 1966 Rosenthal and Jacobson validade this phenomenon in the experiment “Teachers expectancies: Determinants of pupils IQ gains”. In this test the psychologists told the teachers of Oak School that their students took the ""Harvard Test of Inflected Acquisition"" - it was supposed to show wich kids would have more academic success - , than gave the educators a list of the 10 students that achieved better results, but this ten names were selected randomly and those kids had nothing else different then the others. At the end of the school year, the students were submit at the same test and the improvement in the IQ score was more significant at the experimental group (the 10 kids on the list) compared with the control group (the other kids). It became clear that teachers opinions of the students had influenced on the academic achievement of the kids. 
Nevertheless, the more impressive result at this experiment was that the preference for some students was not explicit. The teachers tended to smile more frequently to the designed smarts ones and be more open to the questions of this students. Just because of this subtle actions, those kids who were receiving special treatment believed in their own capacity and had the opportunity to development their process of learning; in the other hand, those who were implicitly unappreciated stopped to believe in themselves, what had affected in a negative way their habillites on studies. This situations demonstrates that students are influenced by teachers opinions about them that consequently became their own version of themselves. Also, is important to remind that students have more influences than only their teachers, family and friends for example. That way, it became clear that learning process isn’t a individual development, but is actually a social growth.
In conclusion, it’s impossible to think about learning as an one person responsibility, this cognitive ability is build by a serie of influences that are, most of the times, out of control of those who are trying to learn. The education system  is unfair, because humans action naturally in an unfair way, constantly shaping others, making people accept others' view of them as their own perspective. Even though the majority of this influences are an unintentional process, they cause a large amount of people not trusting in their capacity of learning. If society could see learning as a social construction, the maladjusted ones will stop to label like that, and, therefore, star build a fair and real vision of their cognitive abilities.
