Epigenetic is an interference from environment in the way our DNA express the phenotype, without mutations in that structure. That interference can be beneficial or maleficent, and depends of the predisposition of each person. It is consisting of changes in chromatin conformation, making it more accessible or not to transcription factors. Can be heritable and malefic as in illness development, or helpful in cancer treatment. Epigenetic interfere in many physiological aspects and researches are trying to use property to develop genic therapies. In this statement I will argue that, epigenetic is present in different behaviors in life, being helpful or not, changing the phenotype according to the environment that we live.

Failures in epigenetics maintenance can result in activation or inactivation of genes witch can develop pathologies. That process is complex, and involve multiples factors. For example, a mutation in DNMT3b gene result in hypomethylation, followed by immunodeficiency and facial anomalies. Many researches are finding relation between tumor and the failure in DNA methylation and histones modification. When happens decrease of methylation in promoter zones of oncogenes, associated with hipermethylation of tumor suppressor genes, is more probable to develop a cancer, as showed in OLIVEIRA (2012) article. Therefore, epigenetics is directed associated to human diseases, because involves the most important molecule of the body, the DNA, for this reason those changes are expressive in human phenotype.

Epigenetic therapies have being research to be use in treatment of human illness. The epigenetic can be helpful, as in cancer epigenetics therapies. For example, medicines can incorporate into DNA and inhibit methylation, reactivating genes witch are helpful to control the tumor cells. As MULLER (2008) cited in his article, combinations of epigenetics therapies or epigenetic therapy with conventional chemotherapy may be more successful, because reactive silenced genes that can destroy tumor cells. Another interesting actuation is the inhibition of proliferation of those cells, as cited by OLIVEIRA (2012), with the use of medication that interfere in DNA methylation. Therefore, epigenetics has being used to research to treatment of human diseases.

In conclusion, epigenetic is present in different modes in life, changing how organisms react facing an interference from where live, and, as scientists know, cancer is an illness that can be a consequence from that changes. Researches are using this interference to develop therapies to combat human diseases. Therefore, treatments using epigenetic is a future in medicine.
