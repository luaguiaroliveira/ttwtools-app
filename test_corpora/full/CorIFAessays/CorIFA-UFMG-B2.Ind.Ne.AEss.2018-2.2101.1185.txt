<Art and Diference>

The study and the relevance of art, education and inclusion are presented as a important theme to be discussed in society and in the academic environment. This discussion is significant because art allows the emancipation and inclusion of the disable person as a legit human being.

Goffman (1998; p.15) indicates that the deficient individual, may find out that they feel insecure in the way as "normal" people identify and accept them. Our purpose is to show some challenges for the completion of artistic practice, such as: be willing to learn and hold onto something again; do concret and simple proposals without resulting the deficience as an impediment.

In this context, we alluded to the study of reseachers doctors Paula Gaudenzi and Francisco Ortega as an important reference to relate the circumstance of the environment and individual capacity. The authors announces that the disable person can't be adjusted to the social standard, but should have the right to normalize by himself, your own life according to your possibilities.

It is about these definition that asseverate the experiences lived in Art and Diference Project; the artistics workshops allowed the suject to expand your space to be whohe wanted to be and developed his autirality, which means that the individual is the author of his dance. The art, therefore, manifests as a new estetical field and social space of inclusion; which the suject is not marked by you deficiency and starts being able through of dance, theatre, among other things, develop your capacity of listening to himself, and recognize your identity as something that exceeds your limitation.
