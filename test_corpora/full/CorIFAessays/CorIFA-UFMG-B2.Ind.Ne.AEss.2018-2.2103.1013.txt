It is common knowledge that  co housing system has been growing all over the world and much has been discussed on how to effectively  implement this system considering the cultural barriers, related to Brazilian way of living. Co housing is a system in which families live in their homes and share common leisure areas and facilities such as laundry and kitchen. This system has many benefits including the sense of community and connection with other people besides, on the other hand there are several obstacles to this implementation due to the different  concepts of privacy  in Brazilian way of living. This essay aims to show that despite cultural barriers, co housing has many benefits and can work well in Brazil.
Sharing cars and bicycles, caring for the neighbor's son, using out-of-home laundries and kitchen and sharing spaces is common among community members in Europe, Canada, and the United States. In contrast, Brazil still has a sense of privacy where each house has its own spaces and nothing is divided. Nowadays it is possible to find buildings with shared living areas such as a ballroom and even gym, at the same time in the house space itself there are smaller windows, darker curtains all to guarantee the privacy of the home. As a result of this, in Brazilian way of living it is possible to live several years without knowing your neighbor next door.  Considering this, the system can be invasive and restrictive in regards to privacy and individualism.  Another important fact is that this system sometimes requires direct resident involvement in planning, operating, maintaining and governing the neighborhood this may not be possible if the resident leads a busy life. 

As a result of Brazilian way of living, the residents may take time and energy  to  develop community skills. Due to the fact that the co housing system aims to transforming the condominium of residents into a community that is connected and directly related,changing the idea of ​neighbors to roommates. This goes beyond sharing common spaces, but also sharing time and moments together. For this it is necessary to modify the concept of privacy and individualism and bringing together people willing to live in community, sharing material goods such as tools and food, values ​​such as friendship and solidarity. 

In conclusion, co housing could work well in the country and can bring many benefits for the Brazilian people, however the thought of privacy and individuality in the Brazilian way of living is a cultural barrier that the co housing faces for an effective implementation. If in the future this thinking changes, this could be a success in the country.
