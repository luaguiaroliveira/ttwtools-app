<Benefits and risks of agricultural use of sewage sludge>
The increase of sewage treatment is an important step to reduce environmental and public health risks. In general, sewage treatment processes produce byproducts, and one of them constitute the sludge, also called biosolids. These byproducts have to be appropriated treated and disposed, and some options for this is: incineration, landfilling, landfarming, agricultural reuse and others. In Brazil, agricultural reuse seems to be an attractive disposal method, so in this essay some advantages and disadvantages will be discussed.
On the one hand, Brazil has some interesting characteristics for agricultural reuse. Frist, climate conditions contribute for sludge mineralization in soil, which reduce the risks of pathogens and pollutants contaminations. Another factor is that agricultural reuse is a low-cost technique, and simple operation. Should also be considered that this way the biosolid is a source of nutrients, so the consumption of mineral fertilizer might be reduced. Further, there are some secondary benefits that can be opportunely mentioned.
On the other hand, disadvantages should also be highlighted. If the sludge management and handling are not appropriate, for example, not properly disinfected or applied on soil, the agricultural reuse can represent a potential source of water and soil contaminant. Farmer acceptance is another important factor to be analyzed. If farmers not feel secure to dispose the biosolid on their land the technique will not fulfill its objective.
In conclusion, agricultural reuse is a potential biosolid disposal method, but all the process (since the sewage generation until sludge treatment and transport) has to be well executed. Some problem can represent a risk for environment and public health.
