The graphs show trends in the percentage amount of Americans divorced at a certain age, according to their year of birth.
What we can see from the data is that there is a tendency of continuity in the amount of people who have been divorced at 30. There was considerable variation in the ages of the people who were divorced and were born from 1940 to 1944.
There is a tendency of a decrease in the number of divorces over the years for women under 40 and men under 45.
According to the charts, the average trend in the total number of divorces is declining.
