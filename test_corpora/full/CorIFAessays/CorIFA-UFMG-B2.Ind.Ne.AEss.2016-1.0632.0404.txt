<Light to generate light>

In the Enlightenment of the 18th century, philosophers and European economists spread their knowledge, and they deemed themselves propagators of light, comparing this with reason. These scholars made discoveries, previously unimaginable, and were fundamental in the event of the Industrial Revolution. Currently, knowledge and light are still intrinsic, since Brazil deals with the consequences of this revolution, for example, with LED lamp technology, which has been in recent years with many economic and ecological advantages compared to conventional lamps. However, the success of its implementation in all regions of the country depends on a decisive factor, which is the investment in technology education.
In this context, it is worth mentioning some advantages that this lighting device offers for daily life. It is true that the LED has higher cost than incandescent and fluorescent, however, different from what is spread by common sense, it must be considered that the investment will be returned in the short term, since they have a useful lifetime vastly larger and can save a lot of energy. Moreover, this illumination is considered sustainable, since its various technical features make it noticeably less harmful to the environment. After all, they have no toxic, mercury, lead and filaments, that is, not require special handling for disposal.
However, to ensure that individuals from all regions of Brazil, to enjoy effectively the benefits offered by LED lamps, both in public environments as in private it must occur certain changes. After all, while in Brazil there are companies that manufacture light fixtures with LED, there is still great dependency on the import component. Moreover, the production of this device requires care, skilled labor, so that its benefits are guaranteed, because a great project demands professional attention and heat dissipation and electronic circuits aspects, for example.
Therefore, since the facts are correlated, it is necessary the implementation of measures to improve the required technology education in the country. Thus, the Ministry of Education should continue to invest resources in programs that provide free technical courses and quality, in order to train skilled professionals to develop research and new technologies to support, for example, the LED lamps market in the country. Moreover, it is part of Inmetro (National Institute of Metrology, Quality and Technology) run campaigns on social networks, addressing the theme of the importance of using LED devices in daily life and its benefits, to raise awareness and inform the public about the use of such. Thus, the country can use the knowledge to generate quality lighting, economic and sustainable for the population, or, following the Enlightenment thought, Brazil is going to use light to generate light.
