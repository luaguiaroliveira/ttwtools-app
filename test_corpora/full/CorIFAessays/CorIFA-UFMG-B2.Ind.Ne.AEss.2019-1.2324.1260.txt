<THE ADVANTAGES ROBOTIZATION OF PROCESSES>

In the 19th century, the Second Industrial Revolution raised a concern for workers who were operating in factories because the robotization of processes brought the fear that robots were stealing jobs. In current days, the Fourth Industrial Revolution and the expansive advancement of automation and software brought the same concern on different scales. This essay will discuss the positive balance in job market and the real consequences of robotizing processes. 
Firstly, it is completely reasonable to assume that factories in all sectors have replaced much of the labor force by automation, therefore a substantial amount of workers have lost their place in the job market. Furthermore, researches indicate that a significant number of jobs are threatened by the fast technological evolution. The <direct citation deleted> study, from Oxford University, says <direct citation deleted>.
However, is important to emphasize that this study and all others that address this theme focus only on this point and fail to analyze the change in market behavior resulting from the new perspective of the production process. The introduction of robots in production plants demands maintenance and high-level software development. Both of these services generate many jobs and are essential for the functioning of the productive system.
Thus, maintenance and software development take workers out of factories but reallocate the jobs to other areas that are in ascension. Today, these areas require technical knowledge and represent a large part of the work force. In Brazil, the software development field has four available jobs per graduated developer, this means that there is a very large request for technological services instead of manual labor and the job demand is not like it was a few decades ago.
In conclusion, the introduction of robots in large factory plants implies a remarkable change in the production system but does not represent mass unemployment, as the most part of workers was expecting. The technical knowledge of the software development is replacing the manual labor and imposing new rules to the job market.
