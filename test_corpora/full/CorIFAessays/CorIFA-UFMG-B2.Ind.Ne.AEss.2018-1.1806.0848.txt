The League of Nations and the United Nations are examples of international organizations that are of extreme importance for cooperation among States in the international system. The League of Nations was created at the end of World War I to prevent conflicts of that magnitude from occurring again, but it failed in some points and eventually ceased to exist. The United Nations (UN) was the League that worked. Established in 1945 at the end of the World War II, the UN has emerged not only to prevent new wars from happening, but also to defend the rights of both States and men. These organizations were important to the working of international system known today. 
The First World War ended with the Peace Conference, which adopted on April, 28 of 1919, the League of Nations. It was a permanent association, which aimed at collective security among States. Its function was: safety, economic, social and humanitarian cooperation, and implementation of points of the Treaty of Versailles. The League was first International Organization dealing with peace with legal mechanisms, and only possible because of a large number of deaths in the war.
The United Nation emerged after the Second World War by the Charter of the United Nations, adopted on June 29, 1945. During the Nazi coalition, the United States and the United Kingdom established principles that would guide international relations after the war. The main function of United Nation is security administration; equality between sovereign states.  Its action goes beyond the principle that international law is constituted of norms referring only to the States an example is the Universal Declaration of Human Rights adopted by a big amount of States in 1948.
Some of the important achievements of the League of Nations was that specific declarations and conventions have been adopted worldwide in order to place racial, religious or linguistic minorities under international guarantees. The United Nations was responsible for drawing up agreements between aligned and non-aligned and very effective states in the decolonization disputes, taking Africa out of the imperialist remnants. It is also responsible for producing numerous policy studies and reports covering a wide range of economic and social issues of global concern. Humanitarian issues at the UN also gained momentum, and with that began the creation of UNICEF and the World Organization of Refugees to deal with the mass social dislocations, famine and refugee flows resulting from the Second World War. Major natural disasters in the early 1970s generated the need for greater coordination of the UN and its agencies in providing humanitarian services
To conclude, the League of Nations and the United Nations were the first organizations of international scope that were able to establish an agreement among countries that had some type of conflict between them. The main importance, however, was that these organizations opened the eyes of the world to the issues of being human and that we have rights to be respected and protected, and that this function is due to the State to guarantee our rights.
