<The feud between math and kids>

Mathematics is part of us. It developed along with our species and is great of importance both for the construction of society and for the perception of the universe, as human beings. But children don’t see math like this. For some reasons – like the way math is taught in schools; the stereotype it posseses – they see math as a bad and boring thing and avoid working with it in the future.
	Learn math is not tricky. But teaching can be. The teacher needs to make the student understand a concept and feel motivated to learn the next one. Unfortunately, this is not what we see in schools. The student memorizes the formulas and apply on problem-solving, not understand why it works and where it comes from (like the Pythagoras formula or the quadratic formula). There is no fun in learn math this way, and there is no reason to want more. Months later the student doesn’t even remember what he ‘learned’ and have to memorize again to apply on the following subject. And this is not math, this is copy and paste.
Another problem is the vision that everyone has of mathematics and how this stereotypes affects students. It is well known that the more you know about math, the smarter you are, or that it is difficult because it requires a lot of thinking. And this are lies. Math is only part of knowledge and there is no reason to be labelled it as superior that other ones. Some people learn easily, other needs to work with math a bit more, but eventually they will end up learning too. Thinking about challenges and problem-solving is as complex as thinking about linguistics and carbon chains. Math is seen as a problem for people, and this thinking get passed on to the next generation.
In conclusion, the way people see mathematics and show it to children, combined with the poor quality of education turn this field of knowledge in something hated by children. Only when teachers start to interact with students, showing the real math, and people phase out the stigmas about it, children will see it kindly and enjoy learning it.
