<Polyphasic Sleep>

In the present day we live in a dynamic context where is constantly required to us to complete a very hard schedule of work every day. Because of that, many people usually change their sleep habits, especially when a continuous prolonged work demands exist, in order to accomplish their obligations. The polyphasic sleep is a daily schedule which counts with a short period of sleep every night (that can be of only 2 hours) and equally spaced naps during the day of, at most, 25 minutes. With this in mind becomes clear the importance of discussion of the positive and negative points related with polyphasic sleep.
The polyphasic sleep has divided opinions in the scientific milieu. Several studies have considered that a nap taken in a polyphasic sleep is almost entirely compound by a phase of the sleep called REM, which is the most important and necessary phase, what explains why polyphasic sleep is effective. Furthermore, according with the book “why we nap” written by Dr. Claudio Stampi the memory and the analytical capacity present improvements when compared with the single-phase sleep (which is the schedule of sleep that most people have), to put it another way, it is possible to improve our efficiency even having a reduction in the sleep time by the polyphasic sleep.
One of the negative points that we have to consider is that if the polyphasic sleep is almost completely composed of REM sleep then, probably, this pattern of sleep may restrict the time spent with other phases of sleep. What is expected in these cases is that the body suffers the same consequences if there were sleep deprivation, even though there is no scientific evidence of this. Moreover is important to remember that the introduction of polyphasic sleep in the daily life can not be very easy once, at the beginning of the transition between a single-phase sleep to a polyphasic sleep, might be very hard to strictly follow the planned schedule, which can lead the person to make longer naps and, as result, make it harder to awake after a nap. Another challenge to overcome for people who have a restrict period of work is that sometimes they are not able to take a nap in the planned time, what can have catastrophic consequences.
In conclusion , the choice to adopt a polyphasic sleep regimen have to be made with some previous planning and a lot of responsibility in order to ensure a successful transition between a single-phase sleep schedule to a polyphasic sleep scheme and prevent any kind of side effects. With this in mind if you are struggling to have your work done you should consider to adopt a polyphasic sleep, what can give you more time available to manage your obligations.
