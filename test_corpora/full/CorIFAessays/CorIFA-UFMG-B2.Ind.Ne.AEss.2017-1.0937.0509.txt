<Are video games harmful to children?>
The answer for the question about the effects of video games is controversial. There are education experts who believe that video games can corrupt the brain of the children. However, there is another amount of researches that defend the practice of playing and argue that it can make children smarter. In this essay, it is being discussed about the benefits and the drawbacks about this topic.
Among the negative points is included the effect of violence. Many games, such as Death Race, Mortal Kombat, Doom and Grand Theft auto induces kids to aggressive behavior. Another problem is related to children becoming addicted to video games, which can make kids socially isolated. Because of that, they also have less time to spend on important activities such as sports, homework, reading, interacting with the family and friends.
However, some researchers defend that video games in reasonable doses have a quite powerful and positive effects on many different aspects of children behavior. Games such as the Incredible Machine, Angry Birds or Cut The Rope stimulates children’s brain to think in a creative ways to solve problems. Other games such as SimCity, Age of Empires and Railroad Tycoon also stimulate children skills for planning and logistics.
Furthermore, different video games have different impacts on the brain. The main impact of video games on the brain is related to the brain network that control attention. Quick thinking, making fast decisions, developing strategies and math skills are some of many positives effects that playing some types of video games can achieve.
Hence, it is important to meet together brain scientists, people that work in the entertainment software industry and publishers to discuss and produce video games that can be able to develop good skills for children, minimizing the negative effects. The participation of parents is also important, through the imposition of a time limit for spending on playing, because video games can only have benefits if it is played in reasonable doses.
