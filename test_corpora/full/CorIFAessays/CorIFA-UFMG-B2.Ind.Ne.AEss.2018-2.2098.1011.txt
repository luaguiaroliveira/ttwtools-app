The habit of people living their lives in a limited way, usually concerned about money, is not healthy and must change. In The United States young people are having high levels of student loans and compromising their future, this is so concerned that the government created laws to decrease indebtedness. The most effective way to prevent these high levels of financial loans is throughout financial education since childhood, because people with financial literacy have more control of consume situations and life decisions. This change in education can created more quality of life and happiness.
According to Lusardi (2010, p. 9) people are used to treat money the same way as people around them do. Therefore, family, friends and colleagues directly influence population's financial habits. Another situation that affects the relationship with money is how past experiences (good or not) are reproduced unconscious when it comes to spending and saving money. Because of that, it is important that population reflect about their financial habits, also it is necessary for the government to include financial education in school grades in order to decrease the high levels of loans over the years.
There are several reasons why it is important to improve financial habits. First of all, self-steam and health can be improve by decreasing amounts of debts that make people stressed out, the data proving that were shown by The Guardian in 2010 in an article that present how happiness is related to financial organization. Secondly, organizing the future's expends, such as being able to have a good retirement at least to maintain the actual life standard or being able to save money for a trip also improve quality of life because it makes possible to achieve certain wishes. 
To avoid the high levels of financial loans in adulthood, the government must implement financial education, in school grades, for students since childhood and encourage population to restructure their financial habits by applying to personal finance courses. By acquiring new knowledge and making life improvements, people will have more control of their consumption and more capacity to make better financial decisions, which will allow them to do what they always wanted without worrying about lack of money. These changes are necessary and will rise population self-steam and make them happier by increasing their quality of life.
