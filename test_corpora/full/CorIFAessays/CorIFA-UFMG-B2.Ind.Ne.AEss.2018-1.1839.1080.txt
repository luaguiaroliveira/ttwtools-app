<THE IMPORTANCE OF ZOOS>

At present, the opinion about the existence of zoos is divided. Whilst part of society enjoys going to zoos with family and friends, other portion is extremely against its implantation. Despite the amount of zoos without the correct structure or professionals, zoos have a significant role in scientific research, animal conservation and environmental education. This essay will explore the importance of zoos to these three aspects based on the concept of specie instead of individual.
“Zoos are prison for animals” is a regular argument used by people against zoos, but actually, most zoos are the last frontier for the animals, the last chance for a species to survive. Being in wildlife is a risk to every animal due the predatory activity, but endangered animals are more liable to be hunting and the zoos are the only salvation in some situations. Furthermore, zoos have the material to conserve gametes and provide a reproduction program that accelerates the species perpetuation of the species.
Also, zoos have a unique ecology that can be used to investigate the behavior, ecological niche and interaction between the animals. This type of study is very important for understanding hierarchy and conduct of animals, and this way elaborate strategies for conservation of the species. Unfortunately, not all zoos are linked with Universities or Institution, what makes more difficult to develop a research.
Finally, zoos are greatly important to environmental education, especially with children. Visits from schools with guides have been proved to be more effective in learning about animal conservation, history and curiosity than a regular class at school. The contacts between animals and humans make children develop empathy for animals and improve their interest in wildlife, increasing their willingness of maintain that reality, in other words, to conserve the species.
In conclusion, although some people do not agree with implantation of zoos, they are not a horrible place for an animal as long as well administrated. Moreover, when it comes to zoos, the professionals have to think about the good of an entire species, not about the individual, however the welfare of the animal contained shall not be ignored.
