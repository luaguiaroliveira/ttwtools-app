<The importance of Eletric Cars>

Over time, people have witnessed many changes in technology and in the environment. One of them was the increased environmental pollution caused by the growth in the number of vehicles powered by gasoline. This problem is becoming increasingly serious and we must think about what world we want to leave for the next generations. Considering this, I’m going to talk about the importance of using electric cars considering the health of the planet. Thus, the advantages of this choice will be addressed, such as greater driver safety, saving money and reducing the emission of polluting gases.

The first reason for choosing electric cars is the cost of maintenance. Fuel prices depend heavily on the political and military situation in the countries supplying oil; even if we do not take into account, a year of driving a gasoline car would cost about $ 2,100. At the same time, driving an electric car would cost just $ 475 per year. In addition to fuel costs, conventional cars require regular technical inspections, transmission repairs, and so on. By all means, electric cars are a much cheaper alternative for those who want to save money.

Electric cars are also known to be more energy efficient: on average, electric cars use about 60% of the power of electric power to spin the wheels, compared to 17% -21% of the energy stored in the gasoline converted into power on the wheels. This is important not only in terms of efficiency but also in environmental protection, because more effective use of energy means fewer resources taken from nature. So the more people choose to drive electric cars, the less damage to the environment.

In addition to the environmental and monetary benefits of hybrid electric and electric vehicles, these are also safer automobiles. Shock tests of traditional cars compared to electric / hybrid vehicles show the difference in safety. The fluid batteries featured in the low-emission transport control influence better than that of the battery found in a gas car.

In conclusion, electric cars are in many ways more preferable than conventional gasoline vehicles. The costs of supplying and maintaining an electric car are much lower than those of ordinary cars; Electric cars are safer, many car fires occur with gasoline rather than electric cars. And finally, electric cars are more environmentally friendly, which means that not only people, but our entire planet benefits from them. Thus, it is advised that as many people as possible choose to switch their vehicles to electric cars.
