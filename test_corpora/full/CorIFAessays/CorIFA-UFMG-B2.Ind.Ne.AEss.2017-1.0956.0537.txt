It is frequent to observe nowadays that people are very busy between many tasks like gim, college, solving problems, and, primally, job. It leaves no time to spend with family and friends, and this means also that we have less time to take care or to spend with aged people, then they are left in rest homes when they depend on someone else for their needs. Furthermore, advances in medicine and health care increased the life span, but they still can’t avoid diseases like Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s disease and dementia, all disorders which cause dependence. Therefore, people got a longer but dependent life.
According to Alzheimer’s Disease International[2], “Alzheimer’s and other dementias are the top cause for disabilities in later life”, in a population that 14.7 percent is older than 65 years old[5], and, further, the Alzheimer’s Association[4] say that eleven percent of people age 65 and older, thirty-two percent age 85 and older have Alzheimer’s disease; Also eighty-one percent of Alzheimer’s disease carrier are age 75 or older. This means we have a big part of the population disabled because of Alzheimer’s, and those numbers are getting bigger since we have an expectation of increasing in the life span due to the advances in science and the conscientization of good practices that carry to a healthier life.
Those reasons show us that, although the increasing in the life span, a huge part of the population can not have a productive live because of mental diseases. And, besides to take care of body health, it is important to make some effort on developing treatments to those disorders and, also, taking care of our mental health to prevent mental diseases.
