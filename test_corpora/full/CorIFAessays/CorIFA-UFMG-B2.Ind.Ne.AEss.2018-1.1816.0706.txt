The mobiles have become the way in which the individual can be connected to the world instantaneously.  However, the constant use results, most of the time, on the unpolished way to use, when the subject is where or when to use the cellphone.
Researchs about the insertion of cellphones in the daily life of the population, constantly, presents the hexes caused by uncontrolled use, seen nowadays as a new type of addiction. Among the negative points generated, we can find spinal problems – due to the curvature of part of the cervical spine so that the view reaches the screen – and tendinitis – in hands and arms, caused by the repetition of movements -, also the low incomes – in school and at work. 
The new technological reality has been faced by most of the schools in Brazil without a common sense of what should be done to improve the incomes, reconciling or repressing the addiction to cellphones. According to the law approved in 2008, the use of cellphones in classes is prohibited, but the breach of it is seen frequently.  The opposition is due to the disconnection of the school environment with the globalized universe inserted in the mobiles.
In the solution of this problems, it is necessary to manage how the process of learning can be reached, highlightining the current government slogan (Brasil, patria educadora). To make this effective, one should invest in educational talks between family and school, directed guidance and monitoring by an educational psychologist, and adherence of the devices in the requested time - which should correspond, for example, the activities proposed by the teacher in an application created by the school with the addition to content taught in class. The connection between pleasure and the student's duty will transform the classroom into a better living environment. Following the need of each school, it would be the responsibility of the government to provide financial support for technical courses - training citizens capable of increasing technology to education in a healthy way.
