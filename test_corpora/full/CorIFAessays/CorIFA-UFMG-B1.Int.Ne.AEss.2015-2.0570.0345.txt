Women divorced percentage is higher than Men. The most important increasing in the period researched is at the percentage of women that divorced at the age of 55, 50, 45 years old. In the case of men, the percentage almost didn´t increase or decrease considering the first and last numbers in comparison with women, except among men aged 55 years old that reached the same rate of divorce than women in the period. 
In general, the high percentage of divorces are among older people. It almost always increase. The opposite has happened with younger couples, who has divorced even less, specially through the last years. 
The graph shows that the percentage of divorces among people until 40 years old has a movement of increasing until 1950 and decreasing after 1950. Among men of 40 years old from 1955 to 1959, the percentage has a particular movement of increasing.
