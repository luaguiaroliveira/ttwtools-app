<Why you should invest in a Mutual Fund?>

Mutual Funds have become a popular type of investment in the last few years. According to Klapper et al. (2004), the growth of the mutual funds worldwide was boosted by the increasing globalization and by the good performance of equity and bond market in the 1990s. Furthermore, the authors point out that the higher market returns and liquidity have also contributed to mutual fund growth. In light of that, it is interesting to investigate why this type of investment became so attractive. Cuthbertson et al (2016) state that, mutual funds offer to investors advantages of portfolio diversification and professional management. Additionally, this is a financial instrument that is safe and liquid nevertheless promises high long-term returns (KLAPPER et al., 2004). Therefore, notably, there are many advantages associated with the investment in a mutual fund. Thus, this essay aims to discuss some of the benefits of investing in a mutual fund, such as diversification, professional management, and liquidity. 

Diversification is associated with invest in different segments and products. Due to the fact that, a mutual fund is a collective type of investment, the amount of money available to invest in different products is bigger than the amount that one individual investor could invest by their own money, and then the diversification is facilitated. Particularly, when you invest in a mutual fund you could access markets that you may not access investing individually. 

When it comes to professional management, the main advantage is transactional efficiency. The manager of a mutual fund is an expert that have the knowledge and skills that are necessary to make the best investments decisions. Consequently, the fund has a higher probability to have superior performance than any other individual investment portfolio. Even though, it is necessary to pay administration fees to invest in a mutual fund it is expected that the return provided by the fund could cover the cost of investment.    

Another factor to consider is the liquidity. Liquidity is associated with the facility off have your money back at any point in time. If you invest in an open-ended fund your money can be regularly withdrawn from the fund and be deposited into a bank account. However, this not applies to closed-ended funds.

To sum up, we could see that there are many advantages involved in the investment in a mutual fund. Especially for unsophisticated investors, that are investors who do not have the experience and knowledge to invest in the financial market, it is a good choice to delegate their resources to professionals, investing in the mutual fund industry.
