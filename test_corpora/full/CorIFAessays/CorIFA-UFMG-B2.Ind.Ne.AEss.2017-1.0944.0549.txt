<Some people believe computer and video games are harmful to children, while others disagree.>
The influence of computers and video games on children's development is a controversial issue. According to some researchers, the increase of children's aggression and violence due to desensitization - watching violent acts and making them appear usual - are result of exposure to violence in games. However, none of the evidence is conclusive to support this theory. This article discusses possible influences for children in an increasingly technological world and how the real influence may be on the motivation of the actions, since volunteers playing violent games whose objectives were to protect their allies from non-human enemies diminished their aggressiveness and increased their empathy.
First, worth mentioning that although desensitization is possibly a real process, the real cause of this phenomenon is not conclusive. Although there are studies on the case, the tests performed are not conclusive because they do not take into account the various factors that may alter their test result, that is, they do not present only one variant between the control group and the test group.
Nevertheless, after several studies we found that when a child watching violent acts is rewarded they tend to imitate these acts, that is, a child is influenced by their environment. And the results of this influence can also be seen in adults. However, one problem with these studies is accurate if we still be influenced after we become adults, with a critical sense already developed and with the capacity to recognize the difference between fiction and reality.
Finally, as there are studies against and in favor of this theme with similar tests, we searched for other variables that might be relevant to the study and were little explored. One of these variants is motivation, as previously mentioned. After a test with groups of children playing different violent games with different motivations was done, the results obtained were interesting, when the motivation of the children was the protection of their friends, it was observed the decrease of aggressiveness.
In conclusion, games do have influence on children, however not as it is passed by many of these studies. A game presenting violence does not necessarily make the child more violent, this varies according to a series of factors and this violence is not exclusive to games. Based on this, we see the importance of age rating in games that if properly developed is a great indicator to help parents decide which games the child is psychologically able to play.
