<Wastewater technologies - a new concept>

During refining oil process a large amount of water is spent, result in a considerable quantity of effluent in this sector. Generally, for this type of effluent, a combination of physical - chemical and biological treatments are used. However, the reuse cannot be achivied by conventional treatment.

Faced with a scenario where costs in raw water collection are hising, increasingly stricter laws and pressure for sustainable development, wastewater treatment systems that allows the reuse has gained a prominent position. The membrane bioreactors (MBR) are an example. It involves the conventional activated sludge process and membrane technology, where high rates of removal are achivied. Although, the fouling process is one restriction for this process. Attempt to solve this problem, some research has been done for trying fill the gaps. An example, that will be object of this work, are the processes osmotic hybrid bioreactor (OMBR) and ultrafiltration membranes (UF). Osmotic bioreactors are considered a promising wastewater treatment technology, but still in its early stage of development.

In my opinion, this is a way that we have to follow, the companies should adapt the reuse processes and change the mentality that just involves profits. We have to take care of our natural resources.
