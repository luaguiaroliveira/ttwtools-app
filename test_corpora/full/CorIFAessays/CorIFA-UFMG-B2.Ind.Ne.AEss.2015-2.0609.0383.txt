Learning a foreign language is a basic requirement nowadays. It includes studying a specific subject, traveling and getting personal development. The curriculums without the language topic filled at least with “English communication” are not likely to be analyzed seriously.
A foreign language is a concern to many adults. They see it as a barrier, difficult to learn fastly and most of grown ups just don’t have time or motivation to start something so demanding from the basics. The process is more difficult to an adult, because the growth process includes a loss of cognitive links in the brain. It is difficult to link ideas to another word, but the one he/she is used to.
On the other hand, children have a lot of cognitives waiting to be linked and a lot of memory to be filled. If their language learning process could start as soon as they were able to speak, that would be a great idea.
It already happens to multicultural families. With parents from two different countries, a child has to learn how to communicate and understand what their parents say.
As long as I can see (observing the kids I met), in the beginning kids mix up what they say, and talk two languages at the same time. It is hard to separate the words from different origins. But, as they grow up, they can easily differ from language to language and communicate perfectly in both ways. I met a guy from Romania that has been living in Brazil with his parents since his infancy. He can speak Romanian and Portuguese perfectly, without any accent (obviously, I was not the one to analyze his Romanian habilities, but he told me that his accent was complimented by a native).
Finally, the process of learning must be connected to spending a good time, specially for kids. If a new language turns into something they can not reach or something unpleasant, the process will be ruinned. These kids can develop a much bigger barrier to the language than learning adults.
