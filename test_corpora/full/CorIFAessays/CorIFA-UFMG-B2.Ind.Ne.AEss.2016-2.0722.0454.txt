Technology does not end up with relationships, people do.

The society has never been so connected. With the Internet's evolution, more than 45% of world population is connected worldwide, which represents almost 3.5 billion people [1]. At the same time people spent more time surfing the Internet than ever before, they fail to meet their family and friends in person [2]. Inevitably, a question is raised: Does the technology really help people to stay together? In this essay I will argue that people are responsible for keeping their relationships, not technology.

The positive effects achieved by the Internet are undeniable: People can communicate at real-time almost from anywhere through chat applications and social networks. Did you met someone from Thailand? Get his facebook and hold his friendship even thousand of miles away. Does your partner have to travel overseas? Keep in touch with him everyday through Skype. After smartphones became available, you can talk to your beloved even in the streets!!

Furthermore, everyone can read almost instantly about anything [3]. At anytime, it is possible to read the last news about Euro block and UK crysis, or watch last news about USA's presidential campaing. At 1-click, you can access the major newspapers from France, UK, Spain, Japan and USA. Are you planning a trip to eastern Europe? It is possible to plan a trip that fits exactly your needs. You can book hotels, buy tickets to museums and schedule guided tours from your home. That is, gathering information was never been easier.

Although our world expanded to the entire planet, we still leave physically at one single place. As the virtual world becomes more inviting, we are spending more and more time in front of computers, TVs and cellphones, denying the real world out there [2]. Consequently, people choose to live their desired reality in favor of their real lives. We must be aware and avoid this behavior whenever it emerges.

The Internet provides a communication channel that extends through the entire world. Through its services, it is possible to keep in touch with friends and relative wherever they are. Also, the Internet provides a simple an easy way to access the most reliable information sources available, such as newspapers, books, articles and encyclopedias. However, we must policy ourselves and do not underestimate your real life in favor of your virtual world.
