<Animals as experimental models>

	Human populations face every day the threat of different diseases, and it is essencial that the scientific community is prepared to provide ways to control this issue. Through research, medical science has developed various tools that made possible the better understanding of diseases and the consequent development of drugs to treat them. These advances were only possible due to the use of animals models in experiments; specially mice. Thus, mice are essential for biological research and has big impact in people’s lifes around the world.
	First of all, mice have many similiarities with humans. Their related metabolism, anatomy and physiology allows the scientists to study many disorders. Due to the extremely high similiarity of the genome of these two species, mice can develop many diseases that humans suffer from, such as obesity, heart disease, cancer, hipertension, diabetes and drug addiction. Furthermore, it is possible to easily manipulate the mouse genome and get offsprings containing desired characteristics. It gives the opportunity to mutate known targets to study specific diseases.
	Second, mice are easy to keep and reproduce. They are small and proper to house, besides they have low cost maintainance and are easy to look after. In addition, adult mice can reproduce quickly, giving birth in a short period of time, generally in 10 weeks. Not to mention that mice also have short lifetime what permits the study of ageing as well. All these characteristics help the use of mice in biological research.
	Lastly, the use of mice in medical research have helped to test therapies and develop new drugs. Every year, millions of humans lifes have been saved by the use of vaccines, which are mainly developed using mice, and the use of therapies and techniques that have been tested and improved in animal models before being administered in humans.
	As conclusion, the use of mice in research play an essencial role in medical science and bring many benefits to global heath. They share many common features with humans that allows the study of many significant diseases. Mice are also inexpensive, have short generation time and are easy to manipulate, which makes it an ideal model to work in laboratory. Finally, studies with this model have brought advances in therapies and development of medicines that improves and save millions of people’s lifes globally.
