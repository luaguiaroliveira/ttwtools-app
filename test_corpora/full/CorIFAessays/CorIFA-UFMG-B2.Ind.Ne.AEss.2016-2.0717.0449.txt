<ESSAY “WHICH IS MORE IMPORTANT: TALENT OR HARD WORK?”>

There are many discussions about the impact of talent and hard work at any creative process and achievements. Some would argue that talent is more importante because untalent people will never be as sucessful as talent ones, even if those work hard. On the other hand, those who defend that hard work is more important present many reasons among which the most important is that the talent is useless without hard working. This essay will demonstrate that the combination of both – talent and hard work – leads to incredible achievements and that hard working and enviroment factors are necessary for every sucessful process .
Firstly, it is important recognize that talent is a natural ability that makes difference at the creative process, which is demonstrated by the University of California’s research that indicates “between 22 percent and 36 percent of the differences in creative achievement in the arts and sciences may be explained by natural endowment” . However, talented people who do not work hard to improve their abilities remain at the same level of the average ones. It happens thanks to the fact that talent makes the person learn quicker and see beyond, still if the ability is not estimulated and improved, “the talent itself is essentially useless”, as said by Inspiria .
Futhermore, talent is an ability in certain area and, for that, needs to be improved by focusing in an especific field. A really talented lawyer would be the worst football player. That is the reason students should go through the area they like and have talent. Without motivation the talent is also useless. As Scott Barry Kaufman Ph.D says:
“when our brain enjoys engaging with particular material, whether it's breakdancing moves or math theorems, the neurotransmitter dopamine gets released—increasing our energy and drive toward further activity in that domain”
Lastly, it is essential remember that many talented people have no motivation to improve their abilities and accomplish a creative and innovated process for many reasons, such as they are obligated to study or work at an area they do not like or they do not have the material goods to do so. It is easy to see many talented people in poverty conditions that do not have the possibility of studying and working at their interested subjects. As result, many talents are being wasted.
Therefore, talent is essential for great achievements, but whithout hard work, motivation and minimum material conditions, it is ineffective. It is necessary understand that the combination of talent and hard work is the key for outstanding creative process. Also it is imperative to provide concrete conditions for the improvement of the talent and hard work.
