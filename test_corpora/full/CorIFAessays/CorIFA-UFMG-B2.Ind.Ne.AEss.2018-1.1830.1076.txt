By the year of 2050, 100 billion land animals will be needed to provide the world's population with the animal products needed such as dairy, meat, eggs and leather. The fact is that the maintenance of these animals for exploitation and slaughter has negative causes to the environment, and the consumption of their meat can even lead to long-term health problems. This raises the question of why decrease consumption of animal products, the benefitis it would bring to environmente and the best ways to replace these products in our lifes. 
Comsuption of animals products require a big amount of animals for slaughter The maintenance of those animals causes problems related to carbon emission and the expense of water.  Methane gas is a greenhouse effect intensifier and is naturally produced in bovines rumen by the fermentation that occurs during the metabolism of carbohydrates of ingested plant material wich is a anaerobic effect of the ruminal microbial population. Was estimated a emission of about 9.2 million tonnes of CH4 from livestock (Lima et al., 2001) in 1995, in Brazil (Statistical Yearbook of Brazil, 1997). This emission corresponds to 96% of all CH4 generated by sources of agricultural origin in the country. Besides it is necessary 15,000 liters of water to produce 1 kg of beef in all the production scale, even the grains that the animals are feed with. 
Another important point is how the consumption of animal products, specifically processed meat, can affect human health. Studies conducted by expert groups have concluded that consumption of red meat is associated with increased risk of colorectal cancer. The meat contains nitrous compounds, heterocyclic amines and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons. Nitrous components, for example, can react with DNA and initiate carcinogenesis. In addition, it is also possible to find studies that relate the consumption of processed meat to the high incidence of diabetes mellitus.
To reduce the environmental and health impact it would be necessary to replace products of animal origin. The vitamins found in meat can also be found in other products of vegetable origin. Vitamin B12, usually mainly found in animal products especially red meat, can be found in seaweed, soy products and tablets (culture of bacteria). Meat and leather can already be produced in vitro. Leather can already be produced in various colors, textures and viscosities. This techinique use animal cells and requires a small amount of animals, that would possible reduce environmental impact and animal explotation.
In conclusion, the consuption of animals products causes impacts to environment due the emission of methane gas and the expense of water, and in long-term it can increase the risk of cancer and other deaseases. Replace those products would be a good way of reduce animal exploitation and the damages it causes.
