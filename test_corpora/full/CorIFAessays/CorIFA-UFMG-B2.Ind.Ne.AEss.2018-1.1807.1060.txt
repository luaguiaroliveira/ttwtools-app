In the late 1970s, psychologists started to work with communities in the suburbs of many cities in Brazil. This fact changed a lot psychology history, once they got in touch with social problems that they had never experienced. However, many professionals  began to act in na authoritarian way and didn’t respect communities’ autonomy. This practice remains until today and had prejudiced the making of an approprite pscychological work with people in vulnerable conditions. This essay aims to defend a way of acting in which psychologists create conditions for the construction of a democratic process of decision in comunnities, respecting the diversity of opinions and mediating conflicts to increase people’s abilities of dialogue. 
Psychologists working in communities have to think about the psychossocial aspects of living in vulnerable areas, in which the health system, the education system and other ones are very poor or nonexistents. This context affects a lot resident’s mental health, and it’s important to find collective ways of resistance, pressing the governemnt to criate projects to improve these conditions. However, the construction of these ways mustn’t bem dictated by psychologists. Each community has a singular view of life, and gives different interpretations for their experience, so the work of psychologists consists in creating dynamics to stimulate the participation of all the residents in the process of making decisions about the best ways to press the government and solve community’s problems. Therefore, the professionals are going to work respecting the autonomy of these areas, and increasing the democratic process of decisions. 
Futhermore, community’s problems have structural causes that can’t be noticed with a superficial analysis. These causes only appear when people understand the social, political and economical context in which they are inserted. For example, when there are  economical crisis in a country, people loose their jobs and it reflects on the community situation. In this cases, psychologists should promote ways of creating a mutual support between the residents, so they can help each other and find temporary ways to resist the crisis. At the same time, it’s part of psychologist’s work to promote discussions about the causes of the crisis, stimulating residents to reflect about politics and economics and build a critical view of the situation. Thereby, psychology work will be transformative and democratic. 
So, although the beginnig of the history of community psychology was marked by authoritarian pratices, professionals today must rethink their way of acting, being more attemptive to social issues. Therefore, they have to act democratically, helping to build a more inclusive political space in communities, and critically, being aware of the macrossocial causes of the communities’ problems. Acting in this way, they will make much more difference in the society, empowering people to take the control of their own lifes and to cultivate a critical view over their reality and the reality of their cities and countries.
