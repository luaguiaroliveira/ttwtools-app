<Innovation in a Business World>

Innovation is a common word nowadays. Everything new is innovation and everyone is working to do innovation. But what does really mean this word? Why does it represent an important thing in a global world? Why is something innovative so good? First of all we have to explore the real concept of innovation. Innovation can mean something new for the world but it can be something different just for one kind of company or just for the inside of the company management.
Invention is different from innovation. Invention, according to a Normative Act from National Institute of Intellectual Property in Brazil (INPI), means something new, not from an obvious process, not completely theoretical and it should have an industrial utilization. Some researchers present an clear differentiation between invention and innovation. Roman and Fuett Junior (1983) describes invention like a conception of an idea while innovation is the usage, when the idea is directed to economy way.
In current world, if a company don’t renew their products or systems it doesn’t stay competitive in the market. The sectors needs novelty even in the way of how their management works. Because of that, innovation is an important concept in the business universe. But do innovation is not a trivial task. The executives have much work to do to create something new and useful for the future of the corporation.
Creativity and hard work has to be linked with the core business of the company. It’s not helpful when the idea for innovation it’s something completely different from the products or services that the company is used to produce. The novelty has to be effective to the business and to the clients. Changing everything is not a good way to do innovation because the company can loose the main way of creates profit, and without profits and resources any idea is able to go ahead.
