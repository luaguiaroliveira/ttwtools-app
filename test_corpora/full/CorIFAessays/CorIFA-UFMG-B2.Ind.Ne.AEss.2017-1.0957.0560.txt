<Aging: a good or a bad thing?>
Nowadays people are generally living more because they have more access to health care and a better life condition than a few decades ago. However this improvement in life expectancy implies in more costs for the government (pension scheme) and people itself, which have to pay more for medical agreement and medicines. Thus, in this essay we will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of aging.
In general older people have more experience in life, in this way they can share their knowledge with others, so this can create friendship bonds, respect and also can be a form of sharing their culture. Another good thing of aging is that elders tend to have more spare time, consequently they can travel more, meet other cultures and other people. On the other hand, get old is not always a great thing, most of all because of the health issues that come with age. Most of this problems are memory and mobility loss, diseases and all the cost that this implies. In some cases, when a person turns 60 the monthly payment of the medical agreement can double. One other problem of aging is the loneliness, either by the family abandonment or by the death of the nearest ones.
In such manner, getting older have good and bad sides, like everything in life, and maybe the perspective in the future can be better with improved quality of life.
