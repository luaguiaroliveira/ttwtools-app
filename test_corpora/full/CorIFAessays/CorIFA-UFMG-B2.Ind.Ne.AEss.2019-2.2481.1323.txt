<MUSEUMS AS A TRANSFORMATIONAL PLACE>

Museums are spaces of culture, arts, education and, around the world, they became an important piece of cultural democracy and social development. However, why public politics for brazilians museums seems to be, always in second plan for the government? In current days brazilian museums are scrapped, It has low visitation and most of the population does not understand the reasons for the existence of a museum. This fearful scenery needs to change, and this essay wants to demonstrate that museums, connected with effective public politics, can be places that receive and multiply cultures, becoming a fundamental instrument in an acquisition of  cultural democracy, appreciation of local communities and brazilian social development.

The first point to be addressed is the need to strengthen bodies related to Cultural Heritage, such as IPHAN - National Institute of Historical and Artistic Heritage and IBRAM - Brazilian Museum Institute, created in 2009. These institutions act as safeguards for culture. material or immaterial, selecting, preserving and disseminating artifacts through catalogs, exhibitions or videos.

Second, Brazilian museums require more efficient security projects. In the last 10 years, at least eight historical and cultural buildings have been burned, causing the loss of thousands of cultural and scientific artifacts. Two cases, the Portuguese Language Museum in 2015 and the National Museum last year, illustrate this reality. Precarious electrical networks are the main cause of fires.

Finally, it is necessary create educational projects that approximate public and museums. According the FVA -  Annual Visit Form, in English, research developed by IBRAM,  38.463.602 million people visited 1.279 museum during the year of 2018 in Brazil, in other words, only 18% of brazilians have been in a museum or cultural centres in the last year. (IBRAM, 2019) These numbers shows how distance public and museums are.  

In conclusion, when a government decides invest in museums, it is also invest on preservation the a history of nation and cultural heritage, stimulate the cultural democracy, even as contribute to social development, education, tourism and economy of country.
