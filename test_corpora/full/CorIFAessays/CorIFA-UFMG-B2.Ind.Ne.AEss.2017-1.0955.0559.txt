Life expectancy around the world has risen dramatically in recente years. According to the World Health Organization, this value increased by five from 2000 to 2015, reaching the level of 71,4 years. Over the last four decades, the increase was 12 years, and the trend is that the rise will continue for the next years. So, it’s important to understand the main reasons for this fact and its impacts on society.
The first – and perhaps the foremost – reason for the longer life of people is the development in medicine. The development of new medicines and the improvement of cirurgical techniques made it possible to heal people with diseases that had no cure few decades ago. Also, the greater knowledge acquired over the years with studies and researches have resulted in a better understanding of the human body and how diseases affect it, bringing forth treatment methodes more efficient, even for simple illness. It means that diseases like cancer and tuberculosis are no longer a death sentece. Nowadays, they have cure, and people can live many years after contract them.
Besides, the consciousness about leading a healthier life is more present in today’s society and can be another reason for the increase in the life expectancy. This consciousness, on a certain level, convert the life style of ordinary people to a healthier way. It’s very common to see advertisements asking to quit smoking and talking about drug addiction, and experts’s suggestions about functional exercises are easier for people to get. And, not less important, the decrease in wars have contributed to a longer life, especially for men population.
Otherwise, a greater life expectancy means more old people living in the world, and it has impacts to society. First, the increase in the number of retired people has prompted governments to find new retirement policies, once it will be necessary to spend more money with them. This may even be a problem in the future if the number of retirees becomes greater than the number of active workers. Another impact is the development of products and services for old people. Today, there are companies that offer care and leisure services specifically for the elderly population. It means that a new branch of the economy is emerging, and it is necessary, because, once they live more and are in greater number, these people must have services dedicated to them.
Finally, there are many causes and effects of the rising of life expectance, and studying them allows a better understanding about today’s society. This fact is a great achievement for the humanity and shows that, even with problems, it has been possible to keep going forward.
