<How the teaching / learning process is influenced by Teacher-Student relation>

The relation between students and teachers it has been target of changes and debates in the last reflections about the teaching/learning process. The approximation among these two sides of education can be very important both to promote a better working environment for the teacher and to increase student confidence with the teacher. However this approximation can also reflect negative impacts, such as excessive intimacy, that would disadvantage in the students' discipline. In this text, we will discuss how to promote this approach in a beneficial and healthy way for both parties.
One of the many problems of education in Brazil is the poor condition teachers face every day and students' lack of interest. It is increasingly necessary that the teacher seeks new methodologies to capture the attention of these students; however there is always the question of the demotivation of these professionals, which can be improved with a friendly and pleasant work environment, where the teacher can follow the evolution of its students. Similarly, students who have some approximation with their teachers, have the freedom to discuss issues, to ask questions, and may thus be more interested in classes.
Previously, in Brazil, teachers had a position of authority, as the only being to have knowledge in a classroom. But today, with the diffusion of information, the student has several ways of seeking this knowledge, and the teacher has become an intermediary, to provide discussions, and critical sense of the students and this is facilitated by this closeness, where both have the freedom to discuss knowledge. The role of the teacher has changed over the years, and concomitantly his classroom posture must also be changed, the teacher needs to approach the level of understanding the student, always seeking to remain in a balance between professional relationship, friendship and respect of both parts.
Taking into account Brazilian educational conditions and the constant changes in the teaching / learning process, the need for a relationship that favors both sides of this process becomes evident. Thus making education more enjoyable and stimulating for students, and more rewarding for teachers.
