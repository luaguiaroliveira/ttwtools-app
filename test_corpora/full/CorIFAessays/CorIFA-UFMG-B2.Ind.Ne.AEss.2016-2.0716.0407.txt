<Does technology makes us more alone?>
Technology is getting more and more advanced every day. The computers are faster, phones have many other features besides making calls and more people are having access to the internet. This may sound good, but all this high-tech devices, instead of bringing people together, are making them more distant from each other. Relationships are more superficial, kids are getting addicted early and the sedentary lifestyle rate is increasing.
When couples go out on a date or to have dinner, as soon as they get to the restaurant and sit on the table, each one picks their phones up and start staring at the screen, they do not even look to each other. Face to face conversations do not exist anymore and phone screens receive more attention than people.
Another issue due to technological advances is that kids are getting addicted too early. When babies starts crying, the parents give them their phones so the babies get distracted by the bright colors and sounds and stop disturbing. This is making the children learn how to use a phone before even learning how to read or write, what can lead to relationships problems in their adult lives.
Sedentary lifestyle is other problem related to technology. People do not need to leave home to “socialize” with their friends or family anymore. They just want to stay inside their houses all day long surfing in the internet, watching movies on Netflix or playing video games. This home addiction is increasing obesity and health problems related to it.
Technology is an important part of our lives, makes life easier and is good in many aspects, but we need to use it carefully. Spending time with friends and family, going out for a walk or talking face to face with someone you care is really important for health.
