Learning a foreign language is very important these days, specially English. It is frequently used in all areas, such as academic or business and is also important to travel knowing at least english, as it is considered the universal language.
Children are often overloaded with activities, such as sports, ballet, language classes and school itself. The reason why parents put their kids so early in these activities, is because the world is getting more and more competitive each day. They think that, if they don't put their children in a language class, for example, they will get behind when compared with other kids at the same age. Another reason is that children learn languages faster, as science already proved.
However, I don't agree that children should begin learning a foreign language as soon as they start school. First of all, kids start school generally with three or four years, so they are not literate in their native language yet. When they reach approximately six years, they are in a semi-literate state, they are begining to correlate words with images, for example. So, if parents wants to literate their kids in another language, they should wait at least seven years.
Another point is that, as said earlier in this text, kids are frequently overloaded with so many activities that they begin to stress out at early stages in life, probably creating teenagers that carries many responsabilities. It is already seen that the generation of children nowadays has more headache, back pain and behaviour problems than the other that came before. These factors could be caused by the amount of activities performed by them.
Parents should let their kids be kids, let them play and learn some skills in their own time. Language skills are very important, but they can wait until the children are older and capable of handling with a variety of events that their parents imposes.
