<The benefits of animal-assisted therapy>

Since the beginning of mankind, it is possible to identify a strong bond between man and animals. This relation can be given in different spectrums, one of them that has been growing significantly in academic research is the use of animals in therapies. It is important to recognize how these connections can bring long-time benefits for the therapy patients. This essay aims to discuss how equine therapy helps treat individuals psychically and mentally.
In order to recognize the reason for using animals in therapy, we need to understand how they connect with people. Firstly is clear to see the relationships built between humans and their pets, most of the times animals are seen as members of the family and this brings benefits to the health of the people who maintain this connection. 
As well as the pets-animals like cats and dogs have this connection other animals can build it too. Nowadays, an animal commonly used in therapies are the horses because they have what the specialists call mirror behavior, which reflects the actions and emotions of the patient. Then secondarily it is important that the animal-patient relationship brings information to the therapist to enhance their work of diagnosing problems with the patient. 
Thirdly, it is essential for the professional to know what kind of patient he is working with. Although the animal therapy can be used with most of the patients some of them for reasons such as trauma and fear may not respond in an expected way to the treatment. Having said that the therapist should choose the most appropriate animal for therapy, equine therapy is widely used with patients who have problems of communication and trust, since in most cases they can not open themselves to the therapist. In this way, when the patient comes in contact with the horse the same will reflect his behavior and this will give the professional a path to be drawn during the appointment. 
All things considered, it seems reasonable to assume that animal therapy can bring benefits for the patient and open a new range of opportunities for alternative treatments since many times the conventional ones may not work with some patients. In conclusion, opening our minds to new things is also, in consequence, generating opportunities for those who need to overcome different challenges as well.
