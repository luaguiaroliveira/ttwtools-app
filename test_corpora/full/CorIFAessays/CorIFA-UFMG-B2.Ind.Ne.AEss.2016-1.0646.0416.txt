It’s know that a grate number of the general population is afraid of dentists. This fear is usually because of the lack of knowledg when it comes to the procedures involved in the treatment and why it’s necessary. Because dentists do not have much time in their clinics to answer a lot of questions, many of them tends to say anything at all to explain why the procedure is needed and how they will get it done. That is why people do not like to go or do not trust dentists in general. The dentistry knowledg is very especif and it comes with a lot of responsability, therefore, I believe that is necessary to rethink about the ethics in the clinic and to remember a little bit more about the human side of the procedure than only remember about the right techinique.
To exemplify why the honest talk between pacient and doctor is so important, a study publish in 2003 named ‘’The impact of restorative treatment on tooth loss prevention” shows that the majoraty of the teeth extracted due to caries had been restored before, suggesting that opting for restorative dentistry may not guarantee the preservation of the tooth. Thus, the pacient needs to understand that this kind of procedure will not always save their teeth and that a restorative treatment does not mean that the pacient can be reckless about their health and hygiene. Not every dentist likes to talk about the negative side of the precedures and it shows a poor sense of ethic and responsability with the pacient’s health.
In my opinion, this problem begins early with the professional training of the dentist mainly because we are a kind of independent worker that is, most of the time, free to do what we think is best. A lot of procedures depend on the good sense of the professional and on the clinic experiencie. For an example, the studies about the dental materials often show divergent results that tend to create diferent protocols from time to time. It means that if you are not motivated in school that you should always keep on learning and researching you may be doing a wrong procedure without knowing. Based on this fact, we can see why it’s difficult to pacients to understand the dentistry way of work, besides the fear many of them already have because of the lack of knowlegde or bad experiences before.
It shows that it is necessary for professionals of the area to improve the relationship between dentist and pacient, being always honest, informing properly about every procedure, to build a sense of security and confidence.
