With the evolution of technology, scientists were able to understand the structure of tiny particles with a nanometers scale and manipulate it and applying in society favor. This manipulation that change a molecule or an atom and create another one with different properties is called nanotechnology and it is really important to many types of sectors as medicine, agriculture, energy production, pollution treatments and civil construction, for example. Thus, investments in nanotechnology is necessary because it benefits this several areas.
Moreover, nanotechnology in medicine, called nanomedicine, can be really effective, according to Dr. Rodrigo Siqueira-Batista, author of “ <Nanociência e nanotecnologia como temáticas para discussão de ciência, tecnologia, sociedade e ambiente> ”, in nanoscale diagnoses with nanotrackers of infectious agents and also in the implementation in therapeutic modality with nanomagnets that can be guide to a specific place for treatment. This will revolutionize hard diagnoses, procedures and treatments of some disease, as cancer, alzheimer and Parkinson's disease.
As well as in medicine, nanotechnology can be really helpful in pollution treatment and purification of water, using nanoparticles with their property of absorbing due to their large surface area. According to some scientists of an Indian engineering university, they are already testing nanotechnologies to purify the water using  membranes of nano carbon tubes to filter it. It can also be applied for treatment or correction of soils, removing contaminants or putting fertilize substances by using magnetics nanoparticles. Those applications are clearly necessaries, due to the enormous pollution in Brazilian’s water and soil, as the Doce river contaminated with the rupture of the dam of Mariana and with the incorrect discharge of residues to the open dumpster.
Furthermore, the biggest source of energy comes from the burning of coal and this process is inefficient, unstable, and in addition, harms the environment in many ways. However, scientists at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) and Harvard University have created a new way to store and generate solar energy in azobenzene molecules inside carbon nanotube molds. Yet, they still don’t know how to build a machine with this material, but the lab tests were a success. 
Therefore, with all this benefits, there must be investments in nanotechnology area. First, the government should invest money in universities that have research on nanomaterials and their uses and, moreover, promote studies in this sector creating more opportunities of works. In this way it will be possible to modernize various sectors of society, improving their functioning and people well-being.
