The relevance of healthy ecosystems for the humanity is huge, in the recent decades scientists have shown the impact we cause on our environments in many ways, from building to planting and transportation. More recently researches points of deeper impacts than just the modification of environment but also in the loss of species, that will have great impact in our survival and has to be treat carefully.  
The human species have a great impact in the ambient they live, we can make big abiotic changes like pollution of water, air and soil, modification of river courses, deforestation of areas for building and planting and all of this examples have enormous impact on the environment. There is also the biotic impact such as favoring of some species like rats and pigeons, introduction of exotic species for food or as domestic animals, these kinds of impact have also huge problems but they may be less likely to be seen.
Once that we are part of the environment the human specie is also really affected by these chances, they can increase diseases by increasing encounters with reservoirs or transmitters or by increasing tissue damage by chemical and physical compounds. There is also a danger coming to produce food, like diminution of rain during deforestation, or susceptible plantations during monocultural methods.
Over all, even seeming impossible a balance between the way of living for humanity and the preservation of healthy environments is necessary for our survival. Even the humans having the potential of changing the ambient were it lives there is still a deep dependence of it, once the human beings still depend of water, air and food production to survive.
