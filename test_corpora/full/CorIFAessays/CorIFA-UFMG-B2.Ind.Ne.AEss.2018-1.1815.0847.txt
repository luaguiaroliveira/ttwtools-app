<Pathologies in reinforced concrete bridges>

Reinforced concrete bridges are susceptible to a number of actions that cause their aging and deterioration, affecting their structure and functionality. This leads to the needing to conduct a study about parameters responsible for this deterioration and techniques applied to inspection and recovery of the structure, in order to analyze their performance. Pathologies in reinforced concrete bridges are in function of their continuous use and the lack of preventive maintenance programs in most cases. Deterioration can be caused by physical, chemical, biological and mechanical agents. 
The environment is one of the main causers of pathologies, so much that an aggressive environment can accelerate and even lead a pathological process (SARTORTI, 2008). The main pathologies caused by physical agents in reinforced concrete structures are surface wear, solar radiation degradation, cracking by shrinkage and infiltration. The pathologies caused by mechanical agents are those caused by requests acting on the structure, for instance, cracking by flexural compression, shear twisting and tensile forces as well as degradation by vehicle collisions and fire.
Chemical reactions manifest through physical deficiences of concrete, such as increased porosity and permeability, decreased resistance, cracking and chipping (DEPARTAMENTO NACIONAL DE INFRAESTRUTURA DE TRANSPORTES, 2006). Attacks by sulfates and chlorides, carbonation, corrosion and leaching are also examples of pathologies caused by chemical agents. Biodeterioration is the main pathology caused by biological agents and consists of the change in the properties of the material, due to the action of microorganisms. The growth of vegetation roots in cracks or porous concrete sites causes expansive force resposible for mechanically degrading the concrete, contributing to the entry of aggressive agents in the interior (AGUIAR, 2006).
Besides visual inspection, there are some destructive and non-destructive testing, which have the objective of contextualizing more precisely the situation of the problem identified previously. Among the non-destructive testing are sclerometry, ultrasound and pacometry. Sclerometry is a technique used to estimate compression resistance of the concrete. Ultrasound is used to check the existence of porous inside of the concrete and pacometry is used to verify the existence of steel inside in the concrete.
Reinforced Concrete Bridges can present many pathologies caused by physical, chemical, biological and mechanical agents. Because this, it is very important to carry out preventive maintenance programs in order to analize the current state of the structure and its funcionality, as well as to identify the existence of any agent that may cause a pathology and compromise the safety of the structure.
