<FACTORS WHICH MAKE YOU SUCCEED IN A FASHION CAREER>

To me, fashion is ceaselessly fascinating because it is an expression of self> .” This is a quote by the famous chief editor of Vogue magazine, Anna Wintour. She certainly achieved a great position in her career following some strategies, but also learning from mistakes. This essay intends to bring up factors which can help you succeed in a fashion profession, along with the importance of knowing this aspects and also discovering yourself. Furthermore, this paper presents solutions on how this process can be facilitated and optimized nowadays.

Not only in fashion, but in most of today's careers, professional experiences tend to be valued. Which means that the only way to get a real sense for the wide range of approaches is to see them in practice in the real world, especially when we speak about fashion - a career without a single formula for success. A recently survey posted on The Business Of Fashion website (2016) mentioned that fashion students are hungry for more practical training and real-life exposure. This shows how important and necessary is the offer of opportunities and support from schools and universities.

Other important factors associated with the creative process are originality and expression. When you are capable of showing innovation producing differential projects, your job tends to be highly valued by employers. Having that in mind, the strategy is to look for inspiration instead of copying from other professionals, bringing expression to your work. In this context, one great example of originality was the last Gucci Fashion Show, which presented a kind of creepy environment, where models had replicas of their own heads tucked under their arms and other strange stuff. Despite of having caused a variety of opinions - good and bad - the show drew attention from the entire world and brought great visibility to the brand.

With all these tactics in mind, the secret to apply then is to love what you do and to express yourself and your feelings in creations - not only in design, but also in the many fields fashion can be associated with. Certainly, if you love your job, you do it well and if you put expression in your works, they become original.

We need to recognize that mistakes will happen and the path to success is not easy, but knowing some strategies and identifying opportunities is a great way to start. That’s why I reinforce the importance of Fashion and Communication courses along with schools in providing more practical experiences for the students and prepare then better for the fashion market. Also, students need to look for opportunities and leave their accommodation status. Therefore, when we have will power and professional chances together, fashion careers tend to get even more fascinating.
