It is known by most that the life expectancy of the world's population is growing fast. Factors such as the modern medicine and the decline of deaths associated with heart diseases, contribute to the aging of the population. According to the World Health Organization (WHO) for the first time in history most people can expect to live after their sixties. The increase of lifespan brings deep consequences in the health field like the lack of skilled labor and the costs with health care. Many common perceptions and assumptions about the aging are based in outdated stereotypes. Actually there is not a "typical elderly", but a diversity of capacities which take into account the whole course of one's life.
Health both from mind and body in the old age are important aspects to consider, Merzenich, a neuroplastic scientist said that although you can reach life expectancy around late eighties when you are eighty-five, there is a fourty-seven percent chance that you will have Alzheimar's disease. In Merzenich words “we have created this situation which we are keeping people alive long enough so that on the average, half of them get down before they die”. The good news is that Merzenich is doing something about the mental lifespan, to extend it out and into the body’s lifespan. According to his researches is possible to develop a brain training method to increase your ability in old age.
The World Aging and Health Report published in 2015 by WHO brings reflections not always remembered when the topic is approached as the fact that advanced age can involve significant changes beyond the physiological loses. This changes includes their social positions and the loss of close relations. But the goal is to achieve the healthy aging. Despite the stereotypes, a healthy and active aging is possible, in which the elderly maintain their independence, autonomy and dignity without diseases and with minimum losses.
The loss of abilities usually associated with old age is in fact vaguely related to people's chronological age. A person history life is very important when we talk about aging and the healthy aging is more than just the absence of diseases, for most people to keep their functional abilities is the most important thing.
