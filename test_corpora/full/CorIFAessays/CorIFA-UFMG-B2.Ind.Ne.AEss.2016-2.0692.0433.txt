<The Transgenic Foods Question>

The alimentation is vital for any humans, because it offers energy for all activities of daily life. Today, in the world have 7,3 billion of people and they all need to eat, that is why the problem about supply of foods is very critical. From there, many governments started to research about genetically modified food, better know as transgenic foods, but they not yet proven whether they are safe to human health a long term. The transgenic foods should participate of human alimentation or they should to be prohibited? The use of transgenic foods is better for three reasons.
First, the transgenic technique has the main objective of selecting plants and animals more resistant to disease, pests, pesticides and climate change, and are also more nutritious and productive. As consequence, the transgenic agriculture will more productive of foods than conventional agriculture, the food will be more cheap and accessible for population.
Second, the transgenic food can be designed to prevent, reduce and avoid risks of certain diseases. Modifying the genetic code of the plant so that it produces vaccines and can stimulate immunological system to produce more antibodies, thereby decreasing the use of drugs that can have serious side effects for human organism.
Third, the implementation of transgenic technique can stimulating the development science in the country, producing knowledge and jobs. Country as Brazil need to be good in education, technology and knowledge and not just in football and Carnival.
On the other hand, food security is very important subject, because everyone has the right foods offer no health risk. The biggest concern of consumers, is that these foods can cause diseases such as allergies and even cancer. It is important to remember that there is in every country the government agencies that control and inspect quality and food safety. Some examples, Overseas Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) and the World Health Organization (WHO) and in Brazil, the Ministry of Health (MOH), Agriculture and Food Supply (MA) and the Brazilian Institute of Consumer Defense (IDEC).
In conclusion, there are many well-documented benefits of participation transgenic food in human alimentation. It is observed that use transgenic plants are more productive e benefits than conventional plants. In consequence these factor, it is indicated the use transgenic food in everyday alimentation.
