<Some people believe computer and video games are harmful to children, while others disagree. What do you think? Take a position and defend it.>
It is widely known that computer and video games are the most common fun used by children these days. Unlike the children of a few decades ago, who were happy to play on the street, ride a bicycle or fly a kite, this generation spends much of their time having fun in the virtual world. However, what can be a good leisure tool, can also become a cluster of problems if there isn’t moderation in the amount of time spent in front of these devices. The present work will shows the benefits and disadvantages of children's computer and video game use.
Access to technology is happening earlier and earlier. Children in their few years, who have often even learned to tie their shoes, already make use of the computer and live playing video games all the time. Over exposure to these devices can lead to problems such as sedentary lifestyle, social isolation, lack of communication and addiction. In addition, the computer carries with it a huge range of information backwards from the internet, which can negatively influence a child, that are in the process of learning of what is good and bad, and that still beginning to build your critical sense to make decisions and make choices.
If on the one hand there may be harm caused by the indiscriminate and free use, on the other hand the use of these technologies can be very beneficial to children if done in a balanced way. Researchers conducted in some universities in the United States indicate that those who use video games as leisure have greater agility of reasoning, creativity and better spatial notion. The information found on the computer can be educational and provide a more playful view of content that are more abstract and difficult to understand.
Is needed to accept that children belong to a technological generation. Instead of banning, joining technologies may be the best way. In order to be more profitable and free of negatives uses, it’s necessary balance and guidance of those responsible. Controlling time and access, offering other leisure options, and participating in children's play is important to they learn that other activities can also be fun and that it is unhealthy to interact only with virtual toys.
Finally, the computer and video game are not harmful by themselves, it’s their misuse that causes damage. If use them to educate, in moderation, they can be a very valid source of entertainment. But for this to occur, it is necessary the presence and guidance of those responsible together with the children at the time of fun.
