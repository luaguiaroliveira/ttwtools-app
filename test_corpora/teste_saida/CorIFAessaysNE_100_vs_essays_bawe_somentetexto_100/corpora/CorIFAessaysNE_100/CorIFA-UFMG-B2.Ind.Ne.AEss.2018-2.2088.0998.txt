<MATERIAL CULTURE AND PAST AS EXPERIENCE OF CONSUME IN CONTEMPORARY>

Material culture studies shows the multiplicity of object functions and their relevance to humans. Objects remains the setting of human societies, their ability to interfere at the environment changing the nature landscape to built their own ones thru at the time. In a brief analyse, human societies seems to configure themselves measured by objects. Once into society as an specie of reason for what people organize their lives in a territory and built systems of distinction between them, objects becomes to human beings the more than a reason why they expand themselves into territory and change the environment landscape; they establish a certain supremacy in it and attribute to the object different meanings and uses. Every object is functional: they obtain a social life, which their uses depend of social class, gender and personal interest, which means that people attribute many values as aesthetic, ethnic, historical, documental, functional, merchandise, technical, etc. It means that objects are mechanism that materialize ideas, emotional expression, provision of services, exchange, etc. There is an specific importance of objects to human beings that's the main of material culture studies: they compose the scenery where history pass thru. Meaning and uses of these objects turns possible analyze the social structure, the influence of some type of professional and the relation between social classes, gender and the subjectivity of these one. Thus it whether object measure human relations, his experience in environment produce memories and it is the bases of tradition and unity of these societies. 
As a result of human experience in environment which turns possible the exchange of their experiences in a territory, once shared into groups, memories propitiate identification between people and them with things that compose the environment. It turns possibile to say that human experience in the world is essentially sensorial, communicative and interpretative ones. That is the main of groups and society: the exchange of memories shared in the same context in a territory cause identification between them and it is inserted as element that compose someone or some group subjectivity. 
Although, in modern society, measured by techniques and constant culture update, which notion of time is marked by rupture between past, present and future - past becomes some unusual, a memory that cannot be revived into nowadays or their culture and material elements exactly way they were cannot become an experience in present - objects gain a social life, which means that it relevance, fabrication of certain serial model or even that type of object may be substituted by other technique that stimulate consume trends and turns it obsolete in a short period of time. Therefore, objects can be seen by two optical: (1) it got some merchandise value or, (2) in other hand, they does not have relevant monetary value that justify keep it on hold, but it compose someone’s collection thus relevance it got to collector’s memory. 
In his reflection about objects biography, Bonnot (2015) talks about the experience of Creusot-Montceau ecomuseum, Saone-et-Loire, in France. During 1990’s, the ecomuseum started a deep study about ceramic industry in that region during XIX and XX centuries. In 1998, they received a letter asking if they got some about a 19th century cider bottle found near Creusot-Montceau. Archaeologists found this bottle composing the church wall. Allied to archaeologists the ecomuseum started a new research about the bottle social life, tracing in that territory possibilities of uses that bottle would had in the past, the manufacturing technique, and consequently, social relations traced by consume and fabrication of cider and the bottle. They discovered the family's producer bottle collection; they documented deployments of artisans and collected material evidences in situ. Actually, this bottle is exposed in Saone-et-Loire ecomuseum. In this brief example, is possible to understand the merchandise cider value to the detriment at the bottle. It doesn’t had exchange value. So, it is any recipient: discarded, used to many other way which it was possible to serve to people interest and needs.

I -  Material Culture and identity in contemporary

Specially in XX and XXI centuries, when industry start to fabricate in large scale and uses technological, semiotical and design updates as resources to stimulate the consume, culture becomes a dispositive to be manipulated also by market. Until first decades of 19th century, the cultural logic was thought by royalty and the ascendant bourgeoisie. In 20th, the logic of equality of people and the consume empowerment, insert a new conception of culture interested in folk, popular artisan and art creations. In this context, it is measured by three agents that manipulate culture: (1) government, (2) industry market and (3) cultural industry that stimulate the consume articulating between them public policies and trends.
According to Canclini (1989), in modern society culture becomes a sharing ideas and conduction measured by institutions as schools, museums, library,  cine, a new conception of family and the social paper of religion, and exploration of folk culture as tourism. In a  society that project themself to future and progress which will come producing ideas, knowledge, technology and things in large scale, in other words, a society organized and structured to serves the capital interest the tradition get lose on cities flows, in the shot free hour.  Here, we back to start of text: whether  memory is the result of cognition of experience, and human experience is measured by things, a large scale production causes a memory crisis. What I intends to talk about is not necessarily the modernist crisis caused by I and II wars and all disasters that involved the technology's experience in 20th century's and provoke a certain fear about future. I admit that is inextricable link between this and what about to say, but I want to show other approach, and it is focused on material culture studies about memory which is the valuation of elements of past in contemporary. I argue that market serial updates in short time to stimulate the consume can cause in people a certain aspect of FOMO (Fear of Missing Out), inducing them  to think that lives in society is to adapt and follow trends, in this case, the consume. In other hand, the nostalgic feeling about using something in a certain time of their lives, in nowadays, is seem to industry's as a market share and it is explored by them with new products with vintage aesthetic and technology optimization.
This memory crisis also is a identity ones. Once in society that  work for progress people have no more a perception about themselves  separated of their job and what it consumes.  The subjectivity is built by it identification. Differently, in traditional societies, the subjectivity is main of compilation of religion, the cyclical perception of  time, memory and the interpretative knowledge about reality. So, here, the person is agent that follow a millennial tradition and grasp itself as unity with well defined importance to it social  structure. In both case, the material culture measure their ethnic identity and their subjectivity construction vary by social setting. In traditional one, time is  a cycle and past is revived in commemorative dates and sazon. The social memory is constantly revived and reaffirmed by preservation of practices and it transmission to new generations. But, in complex societies, time is separated. Past is a some that cannot be revived or experimented in nowadays. What it got from the past is brief references or it aesthetic adaptation.
