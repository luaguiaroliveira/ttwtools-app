The development of science has changed people's lives, for example, research in chemistry and biology has improved the quality of life by the study of new medicine. Due to the high-cost to develop new drugs, the science nowadays, tries to improve the efficacy of old drugs. This enhancement consisted of delivering drug at controlled rate, slow and targeted delivery. In this context, the use of nanotechnology could be prioritized to help this improvement, using methods as nanospheres. Preliminary results show a real benefit using nanotechnology, however, this new science finds a huge resistance to be incorporated by the pharmaceutical industry. More than ever, is a need continue studying the applications of the system of drug delivery.
  
It is really common that the doctors recommend medicine to treat diseases, but this just works if, inside of a body, the concentration of the active ingredient of a drug is kept in high levels. In this way, someone who is in a medical treatment with a medicine needs to take several pills to keep a high concentration of the active ingredient. Furthermore, another factor that affects the quantity of medicine needed is conventional medicines do not go straight to the place they are expected to react, they go all around the body, bigger is the area to be covered, higher will be the level of active ingredient to be taken. When the taken medicine is weak, like the ones used to treat a headache, this is not a real problem. But, to people that submit the own body to a conventional cancer treatment, it becomes a real problem, those medicines are expensive and the sides effects very aggressively.

One of the best-known alternative treatment for cancer is the oncological hyperthermia. This technic consists in the increase of temperature where the tumor takes place due to magnetical nanoparticles, until approximately 42 ยบC, enough to kill or to weaken the cancer cells. However, this method has their own risks, healthy cells could be a target if the nanoparticles disperse all over the body, like a conventional medicine. So, the magnetical nanoparticles must be guided to the tumor to be activated. In this context, nanospheres can be used once they are able to lock the magnetical nanoparticle inside them and to transport to the right place. To take the nanospheres until the injured tissue is relatively simple, cancer tissue has a bigger space between them than a healthy tissue, using nanospheres in the right size is possible to guarantee the medicine arrive at the right place. Applying this method is a good way to reduce the quantity of medicine used, in a practical way this means a fewer sides effects and lower prices, giving to the patient a better way to be treated.  

The pharmaceutical industry helps to make better the human life, but on the other hand, for years they do not incorporate the scientific advances in their work, getting more and more outdated. Thinking about the conventional cancer treatments, it is possible to see that is not easy to the people who submit to it. In this context, the science tries to improve the efficacy of some medicines, for instance using nanotechnology. In conclusion, the science is evolving but this will not be enough if do not exist a communication with the industry. A good start to improving live conditions will be the increment of the study of nanotechnology applied to the medical field, in this way at least is possible guarantee a more humanized remedy treatments.
