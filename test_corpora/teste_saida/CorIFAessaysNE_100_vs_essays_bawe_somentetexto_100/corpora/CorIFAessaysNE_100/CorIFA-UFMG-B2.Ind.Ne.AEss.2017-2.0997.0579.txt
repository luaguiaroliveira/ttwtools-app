<A gradual change towards electric vehicles>	

Since fossil fuel consumption becomes a world debate issue, alternatives cars purposes have been developed in order to achieve more efficient vehicles. For example, different types of fuels, as ethanol, were studied and developed for cars in general. Considering the environmental challenges of current days, in last decades the researches in how electric engines can be used in vehicles contributed to the introduction of the electric car concept in the automotive world. Therefore, due to all the benefits that this new idea can cause, the hybrid and electrical vehicles are becoming a tendency in the world car market and suggest the beginning of a transition time forward a full electric age in automotive field.

In recent years, the rate of sale of electric cars increased considerably in many countries in the world, especially the developed ones. Although the total percentage of these types of cars is still short comparing with the internal combustion vehicles, the numbers show a promising perspective for the next years. For example, important automakers have already developed electric cars, like Toyota, Volkswagen, Fiat, as well as Chinese and American companies. A great indicative was provided by the Sweden Volvo, which expects to produce only electric cars by 2020. Other important factor is the government policies, which has been introduced in some countries and also have a direct influence in the market of this type of vehicles. China, United States, United Kingdom, France, Germany and the Scandinavian countries, that leads the sector, are examples of countries with a special program for electric vehicles, including specific goals for next decades. According with the International Agency of Energy (IEA), in 2030 most of that countries are expected to achieve 30% of their total car fleet on electric vehicles. In Norway, for example, 29% of all vehicles are electric and 37% of new ones are also electric cars.

Besides the growing up market and the incentive policies, the electric car concept tends to be more acceptable in the future due to the environmental issue. The possibility of having vehicles moved by electric energy, and it can be provided from several sources including renewable ones, represents a great step for oil dependence reduction. According with IEA, it would be necessary the amount of six hundred million cars in the world due 2040 for the goal of limiting the global warming on less than 2 degrees. For instance, the internal combustion vehicles represent 14% of the world total emission of greenhouse gases, which can represent an important incentive for the electric cars production. In addition, there are some local environmental consequences as the less noise and air pollution in the cities that are effects with direct impact on the consumers, especially on sales of electric vehicles.

As we have seen, in conclusion, the sale of electric and hybrid vehicles is increasing gradually, but the pace has become higher each year past. Because of the infrastructure and public policies, the amount of these types of vehicles is still more numerous in developed countries. However, the environmental benefits are also important and indicate the beginning of a new age of electric cars, which tends to increase in all countries not only in Europe, US or China. For it become a factual truth, it would be necessary that more countries and people do what the cited countries above are doing, not only related on the infrastructure but also on the economy and policy. Fortunately, it seems to be something inevitable and so much necessary.
