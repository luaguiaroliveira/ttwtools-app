<What is more noteworthy, talent or hard work?>
The hard work is linked to one success, while the talent can be meaningless. The natural talent is a skill achieved by no effort. It should be used as a lead of which path to take in life, the talent, for itself, become useless if one does not develop it. Therefore, for triumph achievement, the hard work is essential.
It is all about the practice and perfecting the skill. Learning a foreign language for example, even though one has aptitude for languages in general, it is not take for granted that he/she has the required fluency anytime needed. It is more likely to have a foreign language proficiency the one who keeps practicing even if greater effort is necessary. That is why most of the no native English speakers keeps attending English classes, although the English proficiency is already established.
Great sports athletes are also a good example. Despite the fact many fans only look for their reward, such as enormous paychecks and fame, most of them had to give up of many things for that. In general, their professional life starts when they are still young, so abdication to be near their family, to have a properly school instruction is extremely necessary. Besides, with regard to the adult professional, they have to deal with the public judgment, biased news reporters, which do not accept average results, always demand outstanding performances. Moreover, their profession involves an unrealistic physical condition, thus the body is like a holy temple any slip or misbehavior about the body care, can lead to a premature career. For sure, there are a bunch of more factors to list, but with this skimming in an athlete life responsibility, it is possible to conclude that the talent is not enough.
However, it is noteworthy to find your own vocation. You must push yourself to seek for that. For many persons it is not an easy task, some of them even claim they have none. Nevertheless, whether one try diverse options he will eventually discover the activity or job in which he has a talent for it. That is the purpose of the vocational tests, the psychologist make you answer specific questions about random subjects, then tell you in which kind of activity your personal profile fit best. And after finding your vocation, it is overt that one must go straight for it and make a huge stake in making it work.
Finally, the talent can help one to achieve success in life. But the hard work, the will, must support the natural gift of a person otherwise the ability will always be considered a promise and not something real. So it is advisable to make a filter to discard those activities you’re sure you have no vocation for it and have the knowledge of what kind of areas you like and try to make the best you can in the focused areas, since success is 99% hard work and 1% talent.
