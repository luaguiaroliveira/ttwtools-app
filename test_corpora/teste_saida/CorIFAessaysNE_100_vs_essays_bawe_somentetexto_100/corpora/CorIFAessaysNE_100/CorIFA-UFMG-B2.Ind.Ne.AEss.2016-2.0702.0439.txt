<The importance of popularizing Science in Brazil>

Brazil has faced a scenery of sharp changes in most of essential sectors for the functionality of the country, mostly the ones that are strongly influenced by politics. These areas are, principally, education, health care, Science, technology and innovation. Although they are not naturally separated and none of them works singly, the main themes here are the last three ones.
Science, since before it is called by this name is not a kind of knowledge that can not be shared with all population. Instead, as claimed for great Scientists, such as Einstein (1920), every single person should be allowed to be aware of Science, taking part of decisions and being informed of all the activities developed by Scientists (and politicians, of course). In this context, the science divulgation grows together with the proper science.
If science is “what scientists do”, we can say that it is a medley system which involves a number of activities, from watch the reality, make predictions, get data and testify or refute to partnering with sponsors, negotiate patent and, moreover, maintain the contact with clients, this is, the population. This is the point of scientific divulgation: tell the story of the research since the beginning and be attentive to possible feedbacks during and until after publication.
When discussing about popularizing Science, more than communicate, the objective is making the real scientific world accessible for all, into or out of the academy. The importance of this matter is evident: if all people must have equal rights, everyone should have the opportunity of being in contact with all kinds of knowledge. However, there is another point hardly ever seen by “holders of knowledge” and this is that instead of showing Science in a way that maintain that outlying, a better exposition plays a role of inviting public to participate of Science. This goal is only reached when the disseminator awakens a critical view of Science and all the hidden factors influencing it, including politics.
In previous years, Brazil has been the target of multinationals (Fundação Dom Cabral, 2014). The president not democratically elected Michel Temer has being proposing attitudes threatening the public sectors, as PEC (proposed amendment constitutional) 257, which establishes a limit for public spending, and this includes the investments in Science, technology and innovation, such as public education, in basic and high schools and in the universities, in which professionals capable to make Science work, are graduating.
It can be said that this is an example that Science does not survive without Science divulgation. All the process that a scientist faces involves an environment much bigger than the laboratory and this requires a game of explanations and requirements. An example of a situation like that is a scientist who aims develop a research about a transgenic corn. A series of account should be given to the institution, to the government, to the other scientists, to the farmers and to the consumers. This last group can be decisive from the beginning to the end of the work. If the acceptance of those who, in the future, will spend or not their money in a new product of science is good, then, probably, the investment in the research in question will be good, and this can strongly change the directions of Science. Later, perhaps in the middle of research, if public defends the idea of a new product, difficultly a cutting found, for example, will be accepted by the population.
Talking specifically about Brazil, we can say that something has failed in this issues: or the scientists and divulgators has not done a successful job when being in contact with population, showing that science is extremely important and should be valued as a public question, avoiding privatizations and precariousness, or the government in partnership with influent business people has done an incredibly good marketing for making up the changes to come. Still, both could be probable options. At the same time the mass media does not promotes a quality science divulgation, it has shown uncritically the attitudes of the new government. Perhaps, the scientists also took an impartial position, not worrying about put science out of the barriers of universities and institutes.
What must be soon done is a heavy awareness in those scientists who did not perceive yet that the support of population is essential and in the people who did not perceive as well that Science is one of the bases for progress and this, such as its decisions, must be not only used for the welfare of everyone, but also done by everyone. This requires that divulgators know, not only the subject in question, but the public and the media, so that the popularization can reach their objectives. Moreover, science e society, if these seen separate, need unification in a way that the forces become enough to prevent setbacks for anyone of sectors.
