Technology has been a target of concern when linked to the childhood. Among children, games and social medias have been more and more popular as the number of access platforms increases: computers, smartphones, tablets, video games and so on. Since the role of technology in children lives is an important field of discussion at the post-modern world and a controversial one, this essay has the intention of treat that theme in some of its relevant aspects, showing that the society is evolving with the technology and it should not be seen as harmful.

First of all, it is necessary to demystify the early exposure. It is very common to think that it is the children predisposition, here considering those who have a certain level of thought independence and ability to manipulate appliances, that leads to an overly alienation. Nevertheless, that exposure can come from the parents as well, one example is the so called “sharent”. Sharent is a blend of the verb “share” and the noun “parent”, a concept created to illustrate the parents action of communicate by social medias their children’s pictures, private moments and general information. As all the aspects of technology, this action has a bad and a good side: although it interferes in the child privacy and security, it can be a mediator between the family and distant relatives, all depends of the people’s information usage.

Whereas some see the electronic usage as problematic for many reasons, like sedentary lifestyle, difficulty in the development of empathy and curiosity, aggressive behaviour, sleep deprivation and shortening of family quality time, it is important to say that the blame cannot be laid in the devices and in the figure of children and teenagers. It is worthwhile to utter that younger generations are provided with a much wider framework of action and interaction, thus the same behaviour as that of previous ones is highlighted and expanded, what happens in extraordinary cases is a problem of the family structure, which is not being able to deal with those environment changes – environment changes do not mean, by themselves, character or behavioural disorders.

Given the right usage, technology can lead to many positive effects in children and family lives. Computers, videogames and other devices can help with fine motor skills like the development of eye hand coordination, promote contact with a wider cultural field, contributing with language and intellectual development, can mediate and facilitate family conversation on more delicate topics and can be involved in family reunions in order to keep up with the youngers dynamics.

As shown, computers and videogames play a big role in children lives nowadays, but that should not instantly have a bad meaning. Technology does not have a free will, all it does is reflect humans’ values. As it can be a very present trigger, it is important for the society to learn how to deal with this tool which can, very easily, become a weapon. The family as a whole have to learn about the potential dangers and benefits they may face and about the power it gives for the user. Build a solid foundation about the topic leads to a well-formed digital child and home.
