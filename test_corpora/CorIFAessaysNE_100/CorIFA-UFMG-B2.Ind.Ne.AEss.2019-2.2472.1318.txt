<The importance of fossil record in biology>

 	Biology is the science of life studies. It is an extensive field and one of the oldest natural sciences. One of the interest areas of biology is paleontology, the study of ancient life. Because the life of the past is currently extinct, the only method to study the creatures that once lived is through pieces of evidence called fossils. Although the fossil record be incomplete it still provides valuable considerations in different areas of biology as this essay will explicit.
The word fossil came from Latin 'fossilis' and can be translated as 'dug up'. It describes the remains or impressions of organisms from the past that were naturally preserved in stones (PROTHERO, 2013). This means that an impression of a shrimp body found within stones or a real body of a fly inside tree resin or even the footprints of dinosaurs can be considered fossils.
Through this context, it is possible to think that the study of paleontology is easy, after all, it needs only fossils to understand the past of the planet. However, the process of fossil formation is extremely complex, delicate and difficult, which means that the fossil record corresponds to a small fraction of all species that once lived in a period of Earth.
The process of fossil formation receives the name of taphonomy (from Greek 'thaphos': grave and 'nomy': classification). The taphonomy is a process that depends on many factors, to cite a few: the place where the organism lives (a lake or terrestrial environment), the type of soil (sand, clay or basalt), the intensity of weathering, the type of the stones (sedimentary, metamorphic or magmatic), the biological and mechanical agents, among others. This study will focus on the actions only by the biological and mechanical agents that are related to the quality of fossil appearance. 
These agents are responsible for the destruction of the potential fossil. According to Prothero (2013), in the terrestrial habitat, for instance, the biological component could be represented by predators. The predators consume the soft tissues of a prey and could even break its bones to access the nutrients in the interior to provide a better fed. Through this, the fossil cannot present skin for scientists study the texture or colors. In addition, there is no possibility to identify the diet, muscles and internal anatomy. In this way, the fossil is already damage. However, it is possible harm even more the material. If it left expose in the environment, what remains could be trampled by a herd of other animals and destroyed or it have theirs bones spread by the mechanical agents represented by waves or currents (this process is particularly important in shallow waters). According to that, to find a complete skeleton is an usual situation (and very important). 
In an ideal process, immediately after the death of the organism, it must be buried in an anoxic and special pressure condition to preserve its parts. Places that provide such conditions are rare, but they exist and are called Lagerstätten (from German: storage place or motherlode, the singular is Lagestatte). In Brazil, there are two of these places and they provide good records from the Cretaceous period: Crato and Santana Formation, both in the state of Ceara, Northeast. The Crato Formation, for instance, was a lake in the past and provides a habitat without currents, what helped the existence of many bacteria that lived on the bottom of the lake and provide a kind of cocoon that involved plants and crustaceous that died helping to preserve its bodies (GARCIA, 2019). Another famous Lagestatte is Solnholfen, in Germany, and provides fossils from Jurassic period with good quality and almost complete skeletons.  
In this context, it is possible to explore how the fossil record is important in different areas of biology. In this study, it will be present three examples of the implications of this delicate material and its contributions to elucidating aspects of other fields in biology.
The first example of the importance of fossil in biology is about the most significant area: evolution. To illustrate the impacts in this area, a good example will be the register of an early bird, from Jurassic period, called Archaeopteryx sp. This specie helped to support the link of evolution between birds and their most close relatives: the crocodilians (as presented by Silveira (2016), Crocodilya, Lepidosauria and Chelonia are groups of what was called popularly ""reptiles"". These three groups and Birds, formed a clade called Sauropsida, and they are closer related to each other than with Mammals). 
It is considered the 'missing link' of this relation once it presents the common features of organisms of the Sauropsida clade: scales (that occurs in the legs of birds), a jaw with teeth (a feature of crocodilians and lizards) and fingers with claws (a specie of current bird called Opisthocomus hoazin still presents this features as showed by Mayr (2011)). The Archaeopteryx sp. was found in a Lagestatte in Germany: The Upper Jurassic Solhofen Limestone, in 1861, two years later the publication of On the Origin of Species by Charles Darwin and helped to support the Evolution Theory proposed by this naturalist. (PROTHERO, 2013). 
In science, hypothesis are being created and put in check all the time. With the fossil record, it needs just find an older register about an organism to change a previous hypothesis. This could be illustrated by the human colonization of America that also provides the second example about the impacts of fossil record in biology.
As presented by Pivetta (2012), although none bony of Homo sapiens was found in the arqueological sities of New Mexico, litic spearheads dated with 11, 5 thousands were found. And, using these materials, scientists suggest that human colonization in America was made by people with mongolian traits (called Clovis civilization). However, in 1970 in Minas Gerais, a partial skeleton of a woman was found. She was called Luzia and presented features of negroids traits and age dated by 11 thousands years. This means, she was the oldest human fossil find in America. 
According to this, a hypothesis of Clovis people, that explains that only mongolian people contributed to colonization of America, could not be accept; and a new hypothesis called Two Biological Components was proposed. This new idea exposed about the two waves of people that helped to colonized America, beginning with negroid people and then with the mongolian.    
And the last example about the theme of this essay is about how creatures that no more lived today are still influence the extant organisms through the use special fuels.
According to Nunez (2019), although lots of animals and plants are buried, when they are submitted to heat and especially huge pressures, these potential fossils are destroyed and became deposits of carbon. This is true once the life on Earth has this element as a mayor potential to binding with another elements and structure larger molecules that provides life as it known. Because that, these carbon deposits, originate what are called fossil fuels. 
The fossil fuels can assume many forms: natural gas, coal or oil and depends on which were the origin (animal or vegetal) and the time when it was formed. For instance, coal was formed by vegetation buried in mud during carboniferous while oil is from algae and plankton deep-buried during the Mesozoic period. When these products are burned they liberate carbon dioxide that is an important component from the greenhouse effect, a required process on Earth, but that in huge amounts, contributes to improve the global warming (NUNEZ, 2019).
A consequence due to increase in temperatures is the death of delicate organisms, for instance, the zooxanthella, seaweeds that live within corals and perform significant nutrients changes with these organisms, influencing theirs colors and healthy. This situation can lead to an environmental imbalance because the corals are nurseries for a huge marine species (zooplankton, crustaceans, poliquetos, and fishes).  
Finally, it is possible to conclude that although the fossil record be incomplete due to the difficult to be formed, when a good fossil is found, its discover has an important role in different areas of biology. They allow scientists to presume about life in the past: the diversity of the living beings that once domain the seas, forests and fields, also, contributing to knowledge about the natural history including the human and helps to support the theories developed or elucidates another. But, these ancient organisms can still offers questions about the humans actions related with the consume of fuels and the consequences for the another species and for the future of balance of the planet.
