The relevance of education is widely acknowledged and it is also recognized the relevance of its quality. This question is important because it influences the learning of the students, and, as a consequence, their professional quality. Since the quality of education involves its methods, it is worthy to discuss about it. The traditional education has prevail for centuries. It is clear that it has changed, but its structure, its standards and assumptions persists. However, this educational approach has been flawed. As an alternative, others methods have been proposed by psychologists and researchers. This essay will present the characteristics of traditional and reformed education, pointing their advantages and their problems. Then will propose an alternative for the question and discuss the actuals and possible future challenges in education.
	The traditional education is centered on teacher’s or professor’s role. The instructions and the concepts are transmitted as a didactic lecture and the students have a passive role; they learn from listening and observation. At universities, the focus is independent learning and the students frequently learn by reading of textbooks and by researches they do by their own. The learning of the students is measured by their tests scores and their grades. It has no interaction between professors and students, or even between students on learning process. Furthermore, the logical, linear and objective thinking are valued to the detriment of creative, abstract and subjective thinking.
	Influenced by different theories about the concept of human intelligence and of the processes of learning, in order to provide an alternative form of education, reformed educational methods have emerged, such as constructivist and progressive education. These methods emphasize the student’s role on learning process. In this approach, they’re active; they are conducted by teachers, but they get to their own understanding of the content. Group activities are common and students discuss ideas with other students in order to build their knowledge. The instructors play a facilitator’s role. The focus is on understanding the facts, analyze it and think critically about it.
	Universities present a complex context. Different areas of knowledge demands different approaches. The debates and discussion as a tool of learning are frequent in Humanistic courses. On the other hand, in Biological’s and Exacts Science’s courses, the traditional methods dominate. Whereas, the learning process differs to each person; each one has his own pace of learning, assimilates the contents by using different skills – e.g. visual, auditory, kinesthetic, associative method, etc –, has different structures of thinking, different limits of concentration and different abilities. Therefore, people who do not fit in traditional education have to struggle to adapt to educational system. It indicates that the methods must be adapted. However, alternative methods may be impracticable to reality of some courses. For example, in alternative education, the student’s measurement of learning is complex. It is completely infeasible to university context. So, the solution should be the easing of traditional method through the use of some practices of alternative method, like group activities of solving practice problems, simulating real situations, classes taught with student’s participation, in order to induce their thinking, leading them to conclusions about the content and others.
	In conclusion, both traditional and alternative educational methods work according to the context and according to the student’s profile. In the university context it is impossible to adapt the method to all particular needs of students, but the system may be adapted to effectively attend the majority of the student body and the different forms of learning with a combination of different approaches. Since the concepts are assimilated with clarity and are well established, the individual can easily recognize a problem and use these concepts and all his knowledge to find a solution. Therefore, the quality of the education, which directly involves its methods, reflects on the professional quality and impacts the society and its development.
