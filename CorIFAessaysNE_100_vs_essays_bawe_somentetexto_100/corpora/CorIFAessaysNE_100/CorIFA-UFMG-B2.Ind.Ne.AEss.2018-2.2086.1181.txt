<A better environment for foreign language learning>

Learning a new language has been increasingly important in the globalized world. People need to know at least one foreign language to get successful in personal or professional life, like getting a better job or simply traveling abroad. However, learning a foreign language is not as easy as it seems. There are many factors involved in this process that are related to the students’ behavior as well as teachers’ techniques. This essay aims to provide possibilities of what could be the good conditions in both sides for a better learning to occur.
First, it is important that the students have motivation to learn. When they know the purpose of this new language in their lives, consequently they get engaged in the process of learning and it gets easier to occur. Otherwise, if they have no reasons to be learning it, even their brain can interfere as a barrier and block this learning process. They may also dedicate some time to study alone the subjects, in order to continue the job started in the classroom, considering that the teacher only facilitates the learning to occur. The practice is the key to improve the skills in a language. As a consequence, the students stimulate their brain to get acquainted with the new language learned.
The teachers also have an important role in the process of language learning. There are many studies and theories about how students learn better a foreign language. One of the most accepted currently is Task-Based Language Teaching (TBLT), that introduces the use of contextualized tasks. Lessons based on these tasks are more meaningful and, consequently, more productive, because its activities require learners to use language in order to attain an objective. This is a good way to present to the students real situations of the use of language, providing them a more natural environment to learn. 
Furthermore, the relationship between teachers and students in the classroom can have a positive impact on how to make classes more interesting. The more the teachers know their students, the better they will enjoy the classes. The teachers should know the profile of the students in each classroom in order to focus the topics in their interests. When the teacher knows the profile of the classroom, it also contributes to the type of material that will be used and the different modes that may be explored. For instance, some students can be visual learners, while others are auditory. So, it is better when the teacher explores different ways of stimulus during the classes.
In conclusion, it is very important that both student and teacher are engaged in the process of learning a new language. It is commonly given to the student the responsibility of being successful or not in the process of learning, but the teacher has also a very important role in this process. Better than that, the relationship between both sides makes the most effective interaction to provide a good environment for learning a foreign language.
