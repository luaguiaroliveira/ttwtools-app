<Provenance of Neoproterozoic metasedimentary rocks of Salinas Formation, Araçuaí Orogen, Brazil>

The Salinas Formation occurs in a wide region of Médio Vale do Jequitinhonha, northern Araçuaí orogen, NE state of Minas Gerais, Brazil. The succession includes greywackes sandstones, pelites and polymictic ortoconglomerate deformed and metamorphosed in greenchist to low-amphibolite facies. In this region Salinas Formation lie unconformably on top of Macaúbas Group and is intruded for pos-colisional granites of G4 Supersuite (ca. 500-530Ma; Pedrosa-Soares et al. 2007, 2011). For a long period the Salinas Formation had been considered to be a stratigraphic unit of the Macaubas Group, new detritical zircon ages and provenance studies reclassified as been an orogenic basin (flysch type) of the Araçuaí Orogen dated at ca.560Ma as the maximum depositional age (Lima et al. 2002, Pedrosa-Soares et al. 2007, 2008). This study presents new geochronological (U-Pb, SHRIMP) and litochemical (major and trace element) results that permits a more detail interpretation about the potential source areas and geotectonic settings during the Salinas Basin deposition.
The geochronological analysis were performed at ten zircon grains using the Sensitive High Resolution Ion Microcoprobe (SHRIMP II) of Chinese Academy of Geological Sciences. The zircon grains were concentrated from volcanic rocks pebbles contained in ortoconglomerate which outcrops in northeast of Salinas City. This ortoconclomerate presents pebbles intensely rounded, volcanic and subvolcanic rocks, quartz, quartzite, gneiss, granite and limestone intermingled with a scarce greywacke matrix. Pebbles of volcanic rock are the most common, varying between andesite and riolite composition. The interval 568-698 Ma reported from the Salinas Formation metasedimentary rocks suggests contribution from sources areas probably originated during the passive margin, accretionary orogeny and collisional orogeny stages.
The Group 1, main interval (6 zircon grains) ca.568-633 Ma indicates as potential sources the Rio Doce Magmatic Arc (Pedrosa-Soares et al., 2011), the sin-collisional granites of G2 Supersuite (Gradim et al., 2014) and ophiolitic complex related with the Ribeirão da Folha Formation (Queiroga et al., 2007; Queiroga, 2010). The Group 2 comprises zircon ages between ca.645-680 Ma that also suggest contribution from ophiolitic complex associated with the Ribeirão da Folha Formation. The oldest age ca.698 Ma is compatible with provenance from Sul da Bahia Alkaline Province (Rosa et al., 2007) and possible sources from the West African would be the volcanic mafic rocks of Lower Diamictite Formation (ca. 694Ma, U–Pb on baddeleyite, Straathof, 2011) and volcanic rocks of La Louille (U-Pb on zircon, Thiéblemont et al., 2011).
The litochemical study includes 46 litochemical analysis, trace and major elements, 16 analyses are unreleased and the others were compiled from studies of Pedrosa-Soares (1984, 1995) and Grossi Sad e Motta (1991). The samples are represented by quartz-biotite schist, muscovite schist, carbonatic schist (regional and type-area represents) and metagreywacke quartzose, characterized as bedded, weakly deformed. Mineralogically are composed by quartz, biotite, muscovite, feldspar (mainly plagioclase), and in minor proportion calcite, k-feldspar, chlorite and lithic fragment. The accessory mineralogy consists of tourmaline, garnet, apatite, zircon, titanite, rutile, staurolite, sillimanite (fibrous) and opaque.
The assemblage of lithocemical data reports low Chemical Index of Alteration (CIA = 48 to 67) and low SiO2/Al2O3 ratio (2.3 -7.9) that indicates limited weathering of the source rocks and deposition close to the source-areas. Furthermore, the litochemical characteristics indicate of the greywackes of Salinas Formation were derived from mixed sources. In terms of provenance, continental magmatic arc, active continental margin and appear to be the most important geotectonic environments that contributed to the sedimentation of Salinas Formation.
	Considering the geodynamic evolution of the Araçuaí Orogen, the sediments of the Salinas Formation seem to be deposited in a flysch basin located above the passive margin sequence and boundary with the continental active margin in the east. The sediments contributions were probably brought through the turbidity currents originated mainly from the east of the Salinas Basin.
