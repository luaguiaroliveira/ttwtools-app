<Do Exercises and Live Better>

The human beings were very active in the past. We needed to catch our own food and our specie lived as nomads, thus, we had to walk long distances and consequently, we were moving all the time. Nowadays, technology came to make the human lives easier and more comfortable, however, also has making us more and more inactive and our body have to do less things that they were made to do. This brings us many dysfunctions and health problems, which required us to create ways to make our body move, and we call it physical activity. Although the benefits and the importance of doing regular exercise it is wide known, many people still do not realize the great impact that being active can proportionate in their lives.
Nowadays, it is very clear in the Literature (Pate, et al. 1995; Haskell, et. al. 2007) that practicing regular physical activity have a strong impact in the general health of the peoples. Prevention of chronic diseases as diabetes, coronary heart disease, hypertension, osteoporosis, colon cancer, etc., is a great example of the importance of being active. In addition, the cellular metabolism, the body composition, and the physical fitness can also have an improvement. On the elderly population, stay active is even more important for their independence in daily living activities and for a better wellness. In this specific population, is even more important to do a strengthening work-out, because due to aging, is natural the process of weakening by the loss of muscles. We also have strong evidences that the practice of regular exercises can contribute to improve the mental health, preventing from depression and anxiety, moreover, can bring benefits to humor and disposition.
It is been proved that professional support is crucial for a good and safety practice. Physiotherapists and Physical Educators are examples of professionals that can help with it. While the first one focus more in injuries treatment and prevention through exercises, the second one works with the fitness improvement. If the exercisers look for a good professional assistance, their results will be much better and the risks of injury will be reduced drastically, for this reason, before start a new practice, it is highly recommended to search for a good Physiotherapist, a Physical Education, or even for both professionals. They can show the better options, if it is better to do some modalities rather than others based on some physics characteristics or if the person needs more strengthening, flexibility or aerobic exercises. Clearly, to join a specific sport intending to compete, or even just for leisure, thus, is indispensable a professional guidance.
In those days, the science has no doubt about the benefits of exercises. From the feet to the head, our whole body needs to be active to better do it functions. Certainly, is natural to the humans to move, however, with a guidance of a good professional the results can be better and the practice can be more safety. If someone looks for a life with a good quality and away from the suffering of diseases and inabilities, that person have to put the regular physical activity practice in his priorities and see it as his everyday medicine for a healthy life.
