The accounting standards issued by IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards) Foundation enable the investors and other users of financial statements access a piece of accurate and high-quality information about leases contracts. Then, the understanding what it is this modality of contract and how the same is applied in entrepreneurial context is the reason of this scholar work. To illustrate this, it is discussed about how valuable and efficient might be the use of leases contracts as companies funding mechanisms. For instance, when an enterprise decides to acquire an underlying asset during a long-term and determined period, by all means, it must be registered by a leases contract. As a result, the most interested in this financial information will be able to perceive what are the best investments that they might do on-trade market, because the financial reporting must follow this accounting standard if they want to keep a relevant and spotlighted place on business scenario. IFRS 16 emphasises what were the changings for two members of leases agreements, whose participants are lessees and lessor. In the case of the first one, it happened several modifications on accounting rules, that are worthy of discussion and comprehension why this phenomenon was established. On the contrary, it notes that any changing was pointed out by new standard in comparison with IAS 17.

In past years, the accounting rules suffered a gradual process of convergence, because stakeholders needed to assure that their decisions were based on accurate and precise financial statements. Then, IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards), an institution responsible to promote adoption and comprehension of accounting standards, issued in 2013 IFRS 16 that established a new pattern of lease contracts. Before this statement, there was IAS 17 a rule that was not able to clarify the real recognition of right-of-use of non-financial assets and financial liabilities acquired by companies through leases. Consequently, a lessee will recognise on his balance sheet depreciation by property of assets and interest on the lease liabilities. Therefore, both types of leases contracts will be registered in statements being able to disclose the real financial position of enterprises. Moreover, this standard cooperates to create value and increase wealth, being considered one of the most important funding sources for enterprises.

Financial market will be able to use transparency and high-quality accounting information as long as there will be regular institutions responsible for regulatory roles. Thus, IFRS standards charges of this role in the global economic scenario by improving financial report, such an important statement to investors and other market participants enabling their economic
decisions (IFRS 16). In this context, it is fundamental to explain that was such a great accountant-registering difference between two types of leases contracts before IFRS 16. So, lessee must segregate “the total amount paid into principal” (IFRS 16,2017), whose presentation was just in financial contracts, from interests, which was issued in both contracts. As could be identified during these years, this model of accounting disclosure, pointed out by IAS 17, indicates that was not able to identify right-of-use and property of financial assets and liabilities acquired by lessees. By new standard implementation, a huge number of rights and obligations that were not found in balance-sheet will be disclosure (SACARIN, 2017). Therefore, a new guidance will be responsible by improving quality of information showing off an accurate financial position.

As an important part of lease transactions, IFRS concerned with how it would be the situation of members of this contractual relationship. For lessors, there was no material changing in terms of classification, thus, the two types of leases will be accounted in different ways as financial and operating leases. It might be noted that IFRS 16 maintained the same requirement pointed out by older rule, in this case, IAS 17. On the other hand, it was a significant modification for lessees, who have the rights of use of underlying assets. IFRS 16 states that assets and liabilities regarded with leases contracts must be recognized in the statement of financial position, in other words, every right and obligation cannot be out of balance sheet. For instance, any enterprise which detains a property or equipment and do not register these non-financial assets in their financial reports fails to apply IFRS 16, the major international current standard. For this reason, it is plausible that lessees attend to standardization aiming to follow the rules, so that investors might be access high quality, fair and accurate accounting information (SACARIN, 2017).

As discussed in this essay, IFRS 16 contributed significantly to clarify about leases contracts might be implemented in financial statements. Sacarin (2017) makes clear that implementation of accounting standards in the business universe it will provide investors put their money in safe and transparent companies. Therefore, enabling the users of information might perceive if their wealth is invested on an assured source of money cooperates for the welfare state of the trade market. Current standardization expresses how relevant it is accounting an underlying asset with its “total amount of cash paid into principal and interest” (IFRS 16) in the perspective of lessee. Actually, there is such evidences that illustrates how this rule contributes to accurate presentation of accounting information.
