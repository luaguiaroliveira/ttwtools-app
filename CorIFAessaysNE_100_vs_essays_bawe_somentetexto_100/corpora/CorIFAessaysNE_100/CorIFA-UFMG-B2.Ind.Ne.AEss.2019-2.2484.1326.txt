<The importance of T type voltage gated calcium channels in breast cancer and breast hyperplasia>
	Ion channels consist of a pore at the cell membrane that enables the passing of ions from the extracellular space into the intracellular. Generally, they have selectivity and only permit the passage of one specific class of ion (HILLE, 2001). T type voltage gated calcium channels it is one of the various existing types of channels with calcium specificity and their pore only open when the cell changes the electrical potential at the membrane surface, then the calcium influx is permitted (CATTERALL, 2011). This channel is generally expressed in excitable cells, like muscle and cells of the central nervous system, showing their importance in movement, heart rate control and Nervous synapses. Controversy, some studies showed its presence in some type of cancers, like breast, leukaemia and melanoma (TAYLOR et al., 2008).
	Cancer is a group of malignant diseases characterized for cells that begin to divide without control, with no cell death, generating old and genetic damaged cells and then tumours in tissues (NATIONAL CANCER INSTITUTE, 2015). When this disease happens in the breast it calls breast cancer, that it is the most common type of cancer in women and at 2018 made more than 600.000 deaths (GLOBOCAN, 2018). There is another lesion in breast defined by an increase in proliferation that consists of what is called breast hyperplasia. The difference between these two diseases is that breast hyperplasia is a benign lesion and even if there is a growth in the number of cells, it happens in a limited way, and without any genetic damaged (GURAY; SAHIN, 2006). There are two major problems faced by women that developed breast hyperplasia: the first is that as it is a small lesion is difficult to diagnose in conventional exams, such as mammography, so it is generally discovered in association with other bigger breast lesions; Secondly, is that in patients with breast hyperplasia, the risk to developed breast cancer increases a lot (KADER et al., 2018).
	Numerous components are involved in the cell cycle, and one of these is the intracellular levels of ion calcium, that is controlled by calcium channels. It controls a range of cytokines, activates proteins, like PKC, CaM and MAPKs cascade, all involved in the process and regulation of proliferation. Bertolesi et al. (2002) demonstrate that blocking calcium entering in tumorous cells promotes cell death, proving that this ion controls the cell cycle. Besides the proliferation, ion calcium is involved in many other processes that participate in the transformation from benign to a malignant cell, like apoptosis, gene transcription and migration (DeLIOT; CONSTANTIN, 2015).
	A normal breast cell does not express T type voltage gated calcium channels, showing that physiologically, this cell controls their cell cycle without this channel (HILLE, 2001). However, Taylor et al., (2008) proved this channel expression in breast tumours and even showed that tumours in the early phase of development, where proliferation is greatest, there was an overexpression of these channels than when compared to the later stages with slower growth. In agreement with them, Palmieri et al. (2012) compared metastatic and no metastatic breast cells and demonstrate that in no metastatic cells, T type voltage gated calcium channels are overexpressed too. 
	There is strong evidence suggesting the involvement of T type voltage gated calcium channel and the development of neoplasms, principally in the breast. Knowing that hyperplasia is an increase in proliferation and additionally has a raise in the risk of developed cancer, this channel may play a role in this regulation and may also be altered in these lesions. 
	Ion channels have a property that they can be selectively blocked so the ion influx can be stopped (HILLE, 2001). This characteristic is usually used to create new drugs, as an example anaesthetic, that act blocking sodium voltage gated giving the loss of sensation. Taking advantage of this, create a drug that promotes the stopping of calcium influx in breast cancer can hold the tumour’s growth and improve women's prognosis. Better than that, use these drugs in women that still have only the benign lesion but higher risk to develop cancer, like hyperplasia, can decrease the number of cases of breast cancer. The good point is that this medicine already exists, voltage gated calcium channels blocker is highly used to treat hypertension since they promote vasodilation mediated by decreased of intracellular calcium (SBC, 2016). Unfortunately, few studies have been done to evaluate the action of these drugs in women with breast cancer, but the ones already performed showed that antagonists of T type voltage gated calcium channel reduce proliferation in vivo and in vitro in many cancer types, including breast; another one using interlaced therapy with Mibefradil, as an antihypertensive and anti-cancer therapy sensitizes cancer cells to radiotherapy and chemotherapy (BHARGAVA; SAHA, 2019). More studies have to be done to better understand all the mechanisms involved in this relation, but the importance of T type voltage gated calcium channels in breast cancer and breast hyperplasia can not be denied.
